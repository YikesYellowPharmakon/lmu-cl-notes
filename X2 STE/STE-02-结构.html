脚手架: 动笔之前要定下的事
The Scaffolding

Slide 3

The Authors

The first question to answer is: Who should be an author?

For example, should your supervisor be a coauthor of your research paper?

According to the German Research Foundation, “an author is an individual who has made a genuine, identifiable contribution to the content of a research publication of text, data or software.”1

1 German Research Foundation, Guidelines for Safeguarding Good Research Practice: Code of Conduct, 2019.

第一个问题: 谁应该当作者? 例如, 导师应该是你研究论文的合著者吗? 按德国研究基金会 (DFG) 的规定: 作者是对文本、数据或软件形式的研究成果内容做出了真实、可识别贡献的人.

所以答案取决于导师实际做了什么: 只提供经费、职位或在最后审读一遍, 按这个定义不足以成为作者 (可以写进致谢); 参与了想法、方法或写作, 就应该署名.

Slide 4

The Authors

The order of authors is also a concern. Three common approaches:

  • alphabetical by family name;
  • by decreasing contributions, starting with the main contributor or contributors;
  • by increasing seniority, starting with the most junior author.

The most visible position is the first (cf. Zhang et al.).

Junior authors typically need the visibility more than their senior colleagues.

The last position, identifying the “head honcho,” is also coveted.

作者顺序也是问题. 三种常见做法: 按姓氏字母顺序; 按贡献从大到小, 主要贡献者排在最前; 按资历从低到高, 最年轻的作者排在最前. 第一个位置最显眼: 别人引用时只说「Zhang et al.」(张等人), 后面的名字都被 et al. 吞掉了. 年轻作者通常比资深同事更需要这种曝光. 最后一个位置也很抢手, 因为它标明了谁是 head honcho (俚语: 头儿, 通常是课题组负责人).

不同领域习惯不同: 理论计算机科学和数学常按字母顺序; 实验性领域 (包括大部分计算机系统和 NLP) 常按贡献排, 最后一位是导师. 所以「第一作者」的含义在不同领域不一样.

Slide 5

The Authors

Roles of authors:

  • The “organizer” coordinates the work, keeping an eye on submission venues and deadlines.
  • The “editor” polishes the writing and unifies the style.
  • The corresponding author is the main point of contact for the publisher and readers.
  • The main contributors carry out most of the work.

For a single-author document, the same person performs all these tasks.

作者的角色: 「组织者」协调工作, 盯着投稿的期刊会议和截止日期; 「编辑」润色文字、统一文体; corresponding author 通讯作者是出版方和读者的主要联系人; 主要贡献者完成大部分工作. 单人作者的文档里, 这些任务都由一个人承担.

前两个角色加了引号, 后两个是加粗的正式术语: 「组织者」「编辑」只是非正式的分工, 论文上不会标出; 通讯作者会在论文里正式标明 (常用星号或信封图标).

Slide 6

The Topic

Often different authors have different ideas about the topic. It helps to clarify the following at the beginning and to have all authors agree on them:

  • tentative title;
  • tentative abstract;
  • tentative table of contents;

and, for a thesis or research paper:

  • tentative list of contributions.

These will keep evolving until the manuscript is finalized.

主题: 不同作者对主题常有不同想法. 最好一开始就讲清楚下面几项, 并让所有作者达成一致: 暂定标题、暂定摘要、暂定目录; 对毕业论文或研究论文, 还有暂定的贡献清单. 这些会一直演变, 直到定稿.

关键词是 tentative 暂定的: 不要求一开始就完美, 但要有, 它们是后面写作的骨架.

Slide 7

The Perspective

Is your text objective or subjective?

A thesis or research paper will try to convey that its contributions are important. Otherwise, the thesis might get a poor grade, or the paper might get rejected.

Even software documentation can be subjective. Suppose you work for a company and write documentation for its product. Since the documentation is a form of advertisement for the product, you will not go to great lengths to emphasize bugs.

Some authors use the passive voice to avoid I, but this alone does not make your ideas more objective. Conversely, use I only if you are relevant to the story.

视角: 你的文本是客观的还是主观的? 毕业论文和研究论文都要让人相信它的贡献很重要, 否则论文成绩差, 或者被拒稿. 连软件文档也可以是主观的: 假设你在公司给产品写文档, 文档也是一种广告, 你不会大费周章地强调 bug.

有些作者用被动语态来避免写 I, 但光这样并不会让观点更客观. 反过来, 只有当「你」本身和要讲的事有关时, 才用 I (例如「I chose this method because …」说明的是作者的决定). 单人作者的论文用 I, 多人用 we, 这在计算机科学里完全可以接受.

Slide 8

The Type of Writing

Narrative writing
The narrative style tells a story, commonly in the order in which it unfolds. Fiction and biographies often use this style.
Expository writing
Most scientific and technical writing is expository: It seeks to convey information.
Persuasive writing
Opinion pieces, advertisement but also grant proposals and customer support materials aim to persuade. Following an antique tradition, you can break down argumentative texts into ethos, logos, and pathos: You establish your credibility, make your case, and end with an appeal to emotions.

写作类型. 记叙文讲故事, 通常按事情发生的顺序; 小说和传记常用. 说明文 (expository): 大多数科技写作属于这一类, 目的是传达信息. 议论文 (persuasive): 评论文章、广告, 还有基金申请书和客户支持材料, 目的是说服. 按古代 (亚里士多德修辞学) 的传统, 论证性文本可以分成三部分: ethos (人格诉求, 建立你的可信度)、logos (逻辑诉求, 摆出论据)、pathos (情感诉求, 最后打动读者).

研究论文其实是说明文和议论文的混合: 主体在传达信息, 但引言和结论在说服读者相信这项工作重要.

两处用词值得注意 (不算错误, 但不是最地道的说法): advertisement 作可数名词指「一则广告」, 这里泛指广告这类文本, 通常说 advertisements 或 advertising; antique 多用来形容古董器物 (antique furniture), 说「古老的传统」时通常用 ancient tradition.

Slide 9

The Target Audience

Your primary responsibility is to readers, not reviewers, colleagues, or yourself.

For seminar papers and most theses, the audience is largely imaginary—hardly anybody except your instructor or supervisor will read them.

But even in such an artificial setting, you should write with an audience in mind (e.g., fellow students).

目标读者: 你的首要责任是对读者负责, 而不是对审稿人、同事或你自己. 研讨课论文和大多数毕业论文的读者基本是想象出来的: 除了老师或导师几乎没人读. 但即使在这种人为的情境里, 写作时也要心里有一个读者 (例如同学). 这个设定决定了要解释到什么程度: 写给同学, 就不能跳过他们没学过的背景.

注意卡片里的 em dash「—」: 两边不加空格, 引出对前面内容的解释. 这是美式英语的标准用法, 后面讲标点时会细讲.

标题
The Title · Examples of Titles

Slide 10

The Title

Titles should ideally be both informative and memorable. Two popular approaches:

  • A catchy title arouses the reader’s curiosity—e.g., We Know (Nearly) Nothing! But Can We Learn?1
  • An informative title succinctly captures the document’s content—e.g., Combining Inductive and Deductive Techniques in Proof Search.

Each approach has its advocates.

A double-barreled title combines a catchy title with an informative subtitle—e.g., Data for Good: Ensuring the Responsible Use of Data to Benefit Society2

Choose a tentative title early. Then keep reworking it.

1 Stephan Schulz, ARCADE 2017, pp. 29–32, EasyChair, 2017.
2 Jeannette M. Wing, BigData 2020, pp. 1–2, IEEE, 2020.

标题最好既有信息量又好记. 两种流行做法: catchy title 吸睛标题引起读者的好奇, 例如「我们 (几乎) 一无所知! 但我们能学吗?」; informative title 信息型标题简洁地概括内容, 例如「在证明搜索中结合归纳与演绎技术」. 两种各有支持者. double-barreled title 双管标题把吸睛的主标题和信息型的副标题用冒号连起来, 例如「Data for Good: 确保负责任地使用数据以造福社会」. 尽早定一个暂定标题, 然后不断修改.

标题的大小写: 英语标题通常用 title case: 实词首字母大写, 冠词、短介词、并列连词小写 (除非在开头或冒号后), 例如 Combining Inductive and Deductive Techniques in Proof Search.

摘要、目录与页数预算
The Abstract · The Table of Contents · The Page Budget

Slide 14

The Abstract

Most theses and papers have an abstract or summary.

The role of the abstract is to allow potential readers in the target audience to quickly determine whether they want to read the rest.

Usual format:

  • brief statement of the context in which the work fits or the question it answers;
  • brief statement of the main idea;
  • brief summary of the contributions.

大多数毕业论文和论文都有abstract 摘要. 摘要的作用是让目标读者中的潜在读者快速判断要不要读下去. 常见格式: 简述工作所处的背景或它回答的问题; 简述主要思路; 简要总结贡献.

Slide 15

The Abstract

Early on, perhaps months before your (real or imaginary) deadline, write a tentative abstract. This will help crystallize your thoughts.

The best abstracts are short. For a 15-page paper, four sentences may suffice. The introduction will provide more details anyway.

The abstract is meta-information. Readers might skip it. Everything you mention in it should reappear with more detail in the text’s body. In particular, any concept, notation, or abbreviation introduced in the abstract must be reintroduced later.

尽早, 也许在 (真实的或想象的) 截止日期前几个月, 就写一个暂定摘要, 它能帮你理清思路. 最好的摘要很短: 15 页的论文, 四句话可能就够了, 反正引言会讲得更详细.

摘要是元信息, 读者可能跳过它. 所以摘要里提到的一切, 都要在正文里更详细地再出现一次; 尤其是摘要里引入的概念、记号或缩写, 正文里必须重新引入. 易错: 在摘要里定义了缩写 (例如「large language model (LLM)」), 正文第一次出现时还要再写全称.

Slide 16

The Table of Contents

Early on, create a tentative table of contents.

In which order should the material be covered?

  • Some dependencies arise naturally and should be honored.
  • There is also the seductive order: Pull the most interesting material to the front, and push the tedious details to the end.

In your draft, annotate the table of contents with page budgets and, for documents with multiple authors, author initials indicating who is responsible for writing what.

尽早列一个暂定目录. 内容按什么顺序讲? 有些依赖关系是自然形成的 (要先定义才能用), 应该遵守. 还有seductive order 诱人顺序: 把最有意思的内容提到前面, 把乏味的细节推到后面. 在草稿里, 给目录标上页数预算; 多人合写时再标上作者姓名缩写, 说明谁负责写哪部分.

Slide 18

The Page Budget

One way to come up with a page budget is to take your page count target, divide it by how many chapters or sections you plan, and adjust the numbers.

Take into account that the conclusion is usually short, and the introduction and background should typically represent no more than 25% of your document. The reader then quickly reaches the document’s core chapters or sections.

Within the core, expand the interesting material, and compress the tedious details (or put them in an appendix or a technical report).

怎么定页数预算: 用目标总页数除以计划的章节数, 再调整. 要考虑到: 结论通常很短; 引言和背景加起来一般不超过全文的 25%, 这样读者能很快读到核心章节. 在核心部分里, 有意思的内容展开写, 乏味的细节压缩 (或者放进附录或技术报告).

幻灯片前后不一致: 上一页的示例目录里, 引言 (1.5 页) 和背景 (2.5 页) 合计 4 页, 占 12 页的 33%, 超过了这一页说的「一般不超过 25%」. 按这条规则, 12 页的论文里两者合计应在 3 页以内. (这条规则是「typically」, 允许例外; 但作为示范的例子正好违反它, 至少应加以说明.)

写作过程
The Writing Process

Slide 20

Gathering Ideas

Create an online document or repository early on in which you and your coauthors can collect ideas.

Whenever you have an idea, write it down using your favorite approach (e.g., bullet points, mind maps, Zettelkasten).

Start thinking about key terminology and notations. For mathematical texts, decide which letters stand for which concepts (e.g., x for real numbers, M for Turing machines).

Also keep track of everybody who helped you, so that you remember to acknowledge them later.

收集想法: 尽早建一个在线文档或仓库, 让你和合著者都能往里收集想法. 一有想法就用你喜欢的方式记下来 (要点列表、思维导图、Zettelkasten 卡片盒笔记法, 这个德语词在英语里也直接用). 开始考虑关键术语和记号; 数学文本要定好哪个字母代表哪个概念 (例如 x 表示实数, M 表示图灵机), 全文统一. 还要记下所有帮助过你的人, 以免日后忘了致谢.

Slide 21

From Ideas to Sketch

Next, enrich your document with more

  • bullet points;
  • examples;
  • tables;
  • figures;
  • etc.

Then put the items in order. Group them into (what will become) paragraphs. Each paragraph should contain one idea. Break long paragraphs along subideas.

Use this sketch as a basis for further thought and discussions with coauthors.

从想法到草图: 接着往文档里加更多要点、例子、表格、图等. 然后给这些条目排序, 分组成 (将来的) 段落. 每段只有一个想法; 段落太长就按子想法拆开. 把这份草图当作进一步思考和与合著者讨论的基础.

Slide 22

From Sketch to Text

Once your sketch is sufficiently developed, you can use it to write text that respects the norms and conventions of the target genre.

You can write the text in any order.

  • For example, you can collect ideas for Section 10 while sketching Section 7 while writing Section 2.
  • If you have coauthors, they can also work in parallel on different sections.

The sketch ensures basic coherence.

With this simple approach, you can defeat the blank page syndrome.

从草图到正文: 草图足够完善后, 就可以据此写出符合目标文体规范的正文. 正文可以按任意顺序写: 例如一边为第 10 节收集想法, 一边给第 7 节画草图, 一边写第 2 节; 合著者也可以并行写不同的节. 草图保证了基本的连贯. 用这个简单的办法, 就能战胜「白纸综合征」: 你永远不是从一张白纸开始, 而是从一份越来越详细的草图开始.

Slide 23

The Curse of Knowledge

As you write, remember that you suffer from the curse of knowledge: You might not notice that you rely on specialized knowledge or jargon.

According to Pinker, “the main cause of incomprehensible prose is the difficulty of imagining what it’s like for someone else not to know something that you know.”

Knuth made a similar observation: “If you try to write for the novice, you will communicate with the experts—otherwise you communicate with nobody.”

写作时要记住, 你受着curse of knowledge 知识的诅咒: 你可能察觉不到自己依赖了专门知识或行话. Pinker 说: 「难懂的文字的主要原因, 是很难想象别人不知道你所知道的东西是什么感觉.」Knuth 也有类似的观察: 「如果你试着为新手写作, 你就能和专家沟通; 否则你跟谁都沟通不了.」

注意冒号后面的大写: 「You might not notice …」是完整的句子, 美式英语 (按 Chicago 规范) 在冒号后接完整句子时可以大写首字母. 这门课的幻灯片一贯这样写 (前面「It seeks to convey information」也是).

Slide 24

The Curse of Knowledge

Symptoms:

  • omitting essential background information;
  • not defining unfamiliar terms;
  • writing too abstractly, without examples;
  • omitting logical links and jumping to conclusions.

Solutions:

  • Keep checking for the above symptoms.
  • Wait a few days or weeks, until the text is no longer familiar, and reread it.
  • Reread the text while imagining yourself to belong to your target audience.
  • Get feedback from your target audience.

症状: 省略了必要的背景信息; 没有定义读者不熟悉的术语; 写得太抽象, 没有例子; 省略逻辑联系, 直接跳到结论. 对策: 不断检查上面这些症状; 等几天或几周, 等文本变得不那么熟悉了再重读; 重读时把自己想象成目标读者中的一员; 向目标读者征求反馈.

列表的写法: 「Symptoms」下面是名词短语 (动名词开头), 小写开头、分号结尾; 「Solutions」下面是完整的祈使句, 大写开头、句号结尾. 同一列表内部形式一致, 这是平行结构的要求.

金字塔原理
The Pyramid Principle

Slide 26

Structure of Well-Structured Texts

Well-structured texts follow the “cascading level of detail” approach:

  1. The title very succinctly summarizes the entire document.
  2. The abstract, if present, constitutes a summary.
  3. The table of contents is itself a summary.
  4. The introduction provides a more detailed summary.
  5. The outline at the end of the introduction, if present, lists the remaining chapters or sections, a form of summary.
  6. The core chapters or sections present the same ideas in greater detail.
  7. The conclusion is yet another summary—one that recapitulates.

结构良好的文本遵循「逐级细化」(cascading level of detail) 的方法: ① 标题极其简洁地概括全文; ② 摘要 (如果有) 是一份总结; ③ 目录本身也是一份总结; ④ 引言给出更详细的总结; ⑤ 引言末尾的outline 内容概览 (如果有) 列出后面各章节, 也是一种总结; ⑥ 核心章节更详细地讲同样的想法; ⑦ 结论又是一份总结, 一份回顾式的总结.

要点是: 同样的内容在不同层次上重复出现, 每一层比上一层更详细.

读图: 金字塔每一层的宽度表示这一部分的详细程度. 顶上的标题最窄 (几个词), 往下依次是摘要、目录、引言、概览, 核心章节最厚 (占篇幅最多), 结论在底部, 又宽又薄: 它回顾全部内容, 但篇幅不长.

Slide 28

Pyramids All the Way Down

The pyramid principle applies recursively, like nested dolls:

  • Each of the core chapters or sections may start with a paragraph that summarizes it.
  • Each paragraph may start with a topic sentence that summarizes it.

Respectively:

金字塔原理可以递归地使用, 像俄罗斯套娃: 每个核心章节可以以一段总结开头 (左图: 标题 → 一段总结 → 其余部分); 每个段落可以以一个topic sentence 主题句开头, 概括这一段 (右图: 主题句 → 段落其余部分). 标题「Pyramids All the Way Down」套用了「Turtles all the way down」(层层叠叠的乌龟, 无穷递归的说法).

Slide 29

Advantages of the Pyramid Principle

With the pyramid principle:

  • The reader quickly understands where the text is going.
  • If the reader stops reading after the title, abstract, or introduction, they will have a reasonable understanding of the whole.
  • A “grasshopper” reader can exploit the structure to skim the text, skipping entire chapters, sections, or paragraphs without losing the thread.
  • The redundancy naturally emphasizes the essential points.

金字塔原理的好处: 读者很快明白文章要讲什么; 读者读完标题、摘要或引言就停下, 也能对全文有大致正确的理解; 「蚱蜢式」读者 (跳着读的人) 可以利用这个结构略读, 整章、整节、整段地跳过而不丢失线索; 重复本身自然地强调了要点.

注意「they will have」: 用 they 指代单数的 the reader, 这是现代英语里常用的中性代词 (singular they), 避免写 he or she.

Discover the Pyramids

Let us analyze the structure of two research papers:

  • Xuejun Yang, Yang Chen, Eric Eide, and John Regehr, “Finding and Understanding Bugs in C Compilers,” PLDI 2011, pp. 283–294, ACM, 2011.
  • Christel Baier and Marta Kwiatkowska, “Domain Equations for Probabilistic Processes,” Mathematical Structures in Computer Science 10(6), pp. 665–717, 2000.

课上分析两篇研究论文的结构: Yang 等人的「发现并理解 C 编译器中的 bug」(PLDI 2011, 介绍随机测试工具 Csmith) 和 Baier 与 Kwiatkowska 的「概率进程的域方程」. 分析时按上面的七层逐一对照: 标题、摘要、目录、引言、引言末尾的概览、各节开头的总结段、各段的主题句, 看它们是否层层概括.
