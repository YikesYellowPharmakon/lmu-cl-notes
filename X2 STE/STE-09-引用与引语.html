引用的格式
Citations and References · Citation Styles · Parenthetic vs. Nonparenthetic Citations

Slide 3

Citations and References

Academic writing makes ample use of citations and references. Citing properly is necessary to avoid accusations of plagiarism.

Some terminology:

  • A citation is a mention that you make in your text of a source typically listed in your reference list at the end of your paper or thesis.
  • A reference is a bibliographic item that appears in the reference list.

Until a few decades ago, authors often used footnotes instead of a reference list. Such footnotes survive on slides.

学术写作大量使用引用和参考文献; 引用得当才能避免抄袭 (plagiarism) 的指控. 两个术语要分清: citation 引用是你在正文里对某个来源的提及 (例如「[36]」); reference 参考文献是文末参考文献表里的一条书目. 几十年前作者常用脚注代替参考文献表; 这种做法在幻灯片上还保留着 (这一讲的幻灯片 7 就用了脚注).

Slide 4

Citation Styles

Consider the following paper:

Martina Seidl, “Never Trust Your Solver: Certification for SAT and QBF,” CICM 2023, pp. 16–33, Springer, 2023.

Different citation styles can be used:

  • a bracketed number such as [36];
  • a bracketed alphanumeric identifier such as [Sei23];
  • an author–year combination such as Seidl (2023) or (Seidl 2023).

同一篇论文有三种常见的引用样式: ① 方括号编号 [36] (按参考文献表里的序号); ② 方括号里的字母数字标识 [Sei23] (作者姓的前几个字母 + 年份后两位, BibTeX 的 alpha 样式); ③ 作者–年份, 写成 Seidl (2023) 或 (Seidl 2023). 计算机科学里前两种最常见, 人文社科多用第三种.

Slide 5

Parenthetic vs. Nonparenthetic Citations

A citation such as [36], [Sei23], or (Seidl 2023) is parenthetic. Never write

In [36], an overview of the state of the art is given.
In (Seidl 2023), an overview of the state of the art is given.

Instead, write any of the following:

Seidl [36] gives an overview of the state of the art.
Seidl [Sei23] gives an overview of the state of the art.
Seidl (2023) gives an overview of the state of the art.
Seidl gives an overview of the state of the art [36].
Seidl gives an overview of the state of the art [Sei23].
An overview of the state of the art is given elsewhere [36].
An overview of the state of the art is given elsewhere [Sei23].

[36]、[Sei23]、(Seidl 2023) 这类引用是插入语式的 (parenthetic): 它们只是附加的标注, 删掉之后句子仍然完整. 所以不能把它们当成句子成分 (「In [36], …」「In (Seidl 2023), …」把引用当成了介词宾语). 正确的写法是让作者名做句子成分, 引用跟在后面 (「Seidl [36] gives …」), 或者把引用放在句末作标注 (「… state of the art [36].」).

Slide 6

Placement of Citations

Put your citations neatly inside the phrases they relate to. Compare:

Our leading design principle has been to focus on a graceful extension of resolution, [2] following Stroustrup’s zero-overhead principle: “What you don’t use, you don’t pay for.” [25]

Our leading design principle has been to focus on a graceful extension of resolution [2], following Stroustrup’s zero-overhead principle: “What you don’t use, you don’t pay for” [25].

Also mind the gap between the citation and the text that precedes it. Compare resolution[2] with resolution [2].

引用要放在它所指的那个短语内部, 也就是标点之前: 「resolution [2],」而不是「resolution, [2]」; 「… pay for” [25].」而不是「… pay for.” [25]」. 后一种写法让引用游离在两个分句之间, 看不出它属于哪一部分. 另外, 引用和前面的词之间要留一个空格 (resolution [2], 不是 resolution[2]); 在 LaTeX 里写 resolution~\cite{…}, 用不断行空格, 引用不会单独掉到下一行.

Slide 7

Overciting

Young academics often overcite. Not every sentence needs a citation. Also, if you cite Seidl once, you do not need to cite her repeatedly in the same passage—you can usually rely on the reader’s intelligence and goodwill.

You do not need to cite the fundamental concepts of computer science or provide sources for common knowledge. You can write about Turing machines without citing the paper that introduces them.1 In case of doubt, ask your instructor or supervisor.

1 Alan M. Turing, “On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem,” Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society s2-42(1), pp. 230–265, 1937.

年轻学者常常引用过度: 不是每句话都需要引用; 同一段里已经引过 Seidl 一次, 就不必反复再引, 可以相信读者的理解力和善意. 计算机科学的基本概念和常识也不需要出处: 写图灵机不必引用图灵 1936 年提出它的那篇论文. 拿不准时问老师或导师. (幻灯片自己用脚注给出了那篇论文, 正好演示了上一页说的「幻灯片上保留着脚注」.)

Slide 8

Underciting

You should also not undercite. That is worse than overciting. You cannot state that

Haskell increases the productivity of its users.

without a citation or some other evidence to support this bold claim.

Nor is it enough to write

According to David Sabel, Haskell increases the productivity of its users.

Such a statement is difficult to verify, and its original context cannot be looked up.

引用不足比引用过度更糟. 「Haskell 能提高使用者的生产力」这种大胆的断言, 必须有引用或其他证据支撑. 只写「据 David Sabel 说」也不够: 读者无法核实, 也查不到这句话的原始语境; 要给出可以查到的来源.

Slide 9

Precise Citations

With the bracketed citation style, you can refer to a specific chapter, section, subsection, page, page range, figure, table, theorem, or similar in a cited work within the brackets—e.g.:

Only superpolynomial separations were known [45, Section 5].
Only superpolynomial separations were known [45, pp. 425–467].

With the author–year format, this information appears within the parentheses—e.g.:

Only superpolynomial separations were known (Urquhart 1995, pp. 425–467).

Such precise citations are useful when citing a book or some other lengthy document.

引用可以精确到章、节、页、页码范围、图、表、定理等: 编号样式写在方括号里 ([45, Section 5]、[45, pp. 425–467]), 作者–年份样式写在圆括号里 (Urquhart 1995, pp. 425–467). 引用一整本书或其他长文档时尤其有用, 读者不必自己去翻. 页码范围用 en dash (–, 见 STE 06).

Slide 10

Clustering Brackets

When multiple bracketed citations coincide, combine them with commas. Compare:

We know some lower bounds on the size of such circuits [23] [9] [12].

We know some lower bounds on the size of such circuits [9,12,23].

As an additional refinement, order the citations.

What if one of the citations has a page annotation?
If technically possible, combine the citations using semicolons (;)—e.g.:

We know some lower bounds on the size of such circuits [9, p. 142; 12; 23].

几个方括号引用挨在一起时, 合并成一个方括号、用逗号隔开: [9,12,23], 而且按编号排好序. 其中某一个带页码时, 改用分号隔开: [9, p. 142; 12; 23]. LaTeX 里 \cite{a,b,c} 自动合并成一个方括号; 加载 cite 宏包还会自动排序 (natbib 用 sort 选项).

Slide 11

Naming Authors

If you need to refer to works written by one or two authors in running text, their family names can be given—e.g., Bachmair and Ganzinger.

If there are three authors or more, you can refer to them using the first author’s family name followed by et al. (usually unitalicized)—e.g., Guttmann et al. In the reference list, you should list all the authors.

It is easy to misspell authors’ names. Guttmann can become Guttman with nobody noticing—except for the actual readers, or Guttmann herself. Copy-pasting is usually the best option.

正文里提到一位或两位作者时写他们的姓 (Bachmair and Ganzinger); 三位以上写第一作者的姓加 et al. (通常不用斜体; et al. 是拉丁文 et alii「及其他人」的缩写, al 后面有缩写点), 但参考文献表里要列出全部作者. 作者名很容易拼错, Guttmann 写成 Guttman 没人发现, 除了真正的读者和 Guttmann 本人. 最好直接复制粘贴.

Slide 12

Naming Authors

An author’s name can stand for the author or the cited work—e.g.:

Urquhart (1995) uses the notation [n] to denote the set {1, …, n}.
In Urquhart (1995), the notation [n] denotes the set {1, …, n}.

With multiple authors, this means both the singular and the plural are possible:

Aho and Ullman [1] describe the algorithm.
Aho and Ullman [1] describes the algorithm.

There is generally no need to write

Aho and Ullman’s textbook [1] describes the algorithm.
Foundations of Computer Science [1] describes the algorithm.

作者名可以指作者本人 (Urquhart (1995) uses …), 也可以指他的作品 (In Urquhart (1995), …; 这里 Urquhart (1995) 指那篇文献, 所以可以跟在介词 In 后面). 因此多位作者时单复数都对: 「Aho and Ullman [1] describe」把他们当作两个人, 「describes」把这个名字当作一部作品. 没必要写「Aho and Ullman 的教科书 [1]」或者把书名《Foundations of Computer Science》搬进句子.

和第 5 张对照着看: 第 5 张说「In (Seidl 2023), …」是错的, 这一张的「In Urquhart (1995), …」却是正面例子. 区别在括号的位置: Urquhart (1995) 里作者名在括号外, 是正文的一部分, 可以当介词宾语; (Seidl 2023) 整个在括号里, 是插入语, 不能当句子成分.

Slide 13

Nothing to See

You rarely need to tell the reader to see a source. Compare:

See Seidl [36] for an overview of the state of the art.
Seidl (see [36]) provides an overview of the state of the art.
Seidl (cf. [36]) provides an overview of the state of the art.

Seidl [36] provides an overview of the state of the art.

Cf. is particularly wrong above, since it means “compare with,” not “see.”

很少需要叫读者「去看」某个来源: 直接写「Seidl [36] provides an overview …」就够了. 「see [36]」多余; 「cf. [36]」更是用错了: cf. 是拉丁文 confer 的缩写, 意思是「比较」, 不是「参见」. 只有在真的要读者把两样东西对照时才用 cf.

Slide 14

Titles in Text

In general, avoid writing titles in the text. Readers who need the title can look it up in the reference list. But occasionally, titles do appear in the text—e.g.:

In Chapter 26 of the Handbook of Satisfiability, Barrett et al. [3] describe the extension of CDCL with theory solvers.

Use title case. In addition, use italics for “large works” and quotation marks for “small works.”

If you need to refer to the Handbook of Satisfiability multiple times, you can shorten its title to Handbook from the second time—e.g.:

We borrow these notations from the Handbook chapter.

一般不在正文里写标题, 需要的读者可以去参考文献表里查. 偶尔要写时: 用 title case (实词首字母大写); 「大作品」(书、期刊、学位论文) 用斜体, 「小作品」(论文、章节) 用引号 (见 STE 08). 同一个长标题要多次提到时, 从第二次起可以缩短, 如把 Handbook of Satisfiability 简称为 Handbook.

Slide 15

Initial Articles

Is it Knuth’s The Art of Computer Programming or Knuth’s Art of Computer Programming?

Both are correct. But sometimes you must peel off the article—e.g., Knuth’s monumental Art of Computer Programming.

If the is not part of the title, it should be lowercase and not italicized—e.g., Chapter 26 from the Handbook of Satisfiability.

标题开头的冠词: 「Knuth’s The Art of Computer Programming」和「Knuth’s Art of Computer Programming」都对; 但前面加了形容词时必须去掉冠词 (Knuth’s monumental Art of …, 不能是 monumental The Art). 如果 the 不属于标题本身, 就用小写正体: the Handbook of Satisfiability.

Slide 16

Personal Communications

Sometimes you need to refer to a conversation or email that has not been published. It is usually enough to mention your conversation partner and the date with the remark personal communication—e.g.:

In a personal communication on January 6, 2011, Rafal Kolanski quipped that “there’s no learning curve—it’s a series of cliffs.”

Alternatively, create a reference. For example, in the text you could write

As a user remarked [26], “there’s no learning curve—it’s a series of cliffs.”

and in the reference list, you would have

[26] Rafal Kolanski. Personal communication. January 6, 2011.

Do not mention conversations with your supervisors.

引用未发表的谈话或邮件: 正文里写出对方姓名、日期和「personal communication」就够了; 也可以在参考文献表里单列一条「[26] Rafal Kolanski. Personal communication. January 6, 2011.」. 例句的意思是「(这个软件) 没有学习曲线, 只有一连串悬崖」. 和自己导师的谈话不用写进去.

选择来源

Slide 17

Choosing among Multiple Sources

Which source should you cite if several are available for a particular fact?

Prefer sources that are regarded as more reliable.

Possible sources in roughly decreasing order of perceived reliability:

  1. book;
  2. journal article;
  3. conference paper;
  4. PhD thesis;
  5. workshop paper;
  6. technical report;
  7. website or other electronic media;
  8. personal communication.

But sometimes you might prefer to cite a technical report or PhD thesis, because it contains more details than another, more “reliable” source.

同一个事实有多个来源时, 引用公认更可靠的那个. 大致可靠程度从高到低: 书 > 期刊论文 > 会议论文 > 博士论文 > workshop 论文 > 技术报告 > 网站等电子媒体 > 私人通信. 但有时技术报告或博士论文细节更多 (例如会议论文篇幅有限, 证明常放在技术报告里), 这时可以引用它.

Slide 18

Indirect Citation

You might learn about the existence of a source A through another source B. You should try to get your hands on A and cite it without mentioning B. If you cannot find A, you must acknowledge that your citation of A comes from B. This is inelegant but sometimes necessary.

For example, suppose that you looked up the skip list data structure on Wikipedia and found a citation to a 1990 article by William Pugh. Before you can cite this article, you must verify that it really exists and contains what you expect. Fortunately, the original is easy to find, so you can cite it directly.

间接引用: 通过来源 B 知道了来源 A, 应当设法找到 A 本身, 读过之后直接引用 A, 不提 B. 实在找不到 A, 就要说明对 A 的引用来自 B (「转引自」), 这不优雅, 但有时没办法. 例子: 在维基百科上查跳表 (skip list), 看到它引用了 William Pugh 1990 年的文章; 引用前必须确认这篇文章真的存在、内容和你期望的一致. 好在原文很容易找到, 可以直接引用.

这篇文章是 William Pugh, “Skip Lists: A Probabilistic Alternative to Balanced Trees,” Communications of the ACM 33(6), pp. 668–676, 1990. 「先核实再引用」的理由: 二手来源里的引用经常有错 (年份、页码、甚至内容), 照抄会把错误传下去.

引用自己
Self-citation · Calling Yourself

Slide 19

Self-citation

Self-citations are acceptable, but do not overcite yourself. And avoid self-praise.

Self-citations should be clearly identified as such. Compare:

It has been argued [18] that Haskell increases the productivity of its users.

I argued elsewhere [18] that Haskell increases the productivity of its users.

This also applies if you have coauthors—e.g.:

Together with colleagues, I developed a tool for visualizing database transactions [12].
The research Banerjee and I have done on grammar transformations [3] forms the basis of the current work.

可以引用自己的工作, 但不要过多, 也不要自夸. 自引要让读者看得出是自引: 「It has been argued [18] …」像是在引别人的观点给自己撑腰, 应写成「I argued elsewhere [18] …」. 有合作者时同样要点明 (Together with colleagues, I …; The research Banerjee and I have done …).

Slide 20

Calling Yourself

Embrace the first-person pronouns I and we.
You can refer to yourself as I if you are a single author and use we otherwise.
This is clearer than using the passive.

There is hardly ever a need to write the author or the authors.
Occasionally, you might need to refer to individual authors—e.g., the first author.
You can also refer to individual authors by family names or initials—e.g.:

The first author was funded by the German Research Foundation.
Acheampong was funded by the German Research Foundation.
N.A. was funded by the German Research Foundation.

大胆用第一人称: 单独作者用 I, 多位作者用 we, 比被动语态清楚. 几乎没必要写「the author」「the authors」指自己. 偶尔要分别提到某位作者时 (常见于致谢里的经费说明), 写「the first author」、姓 (Acheampong) 或姓名首字母 (N.A.) 都可以. German Research Foundation 即德国研究基金会 DFG.

参考文献表

Slide 22

The Reference List

The reference list (also called “bibliography”) presents all the sources you cite throughout your document, and only those. It is usually put at the end, after your concluding chapter or section.

The references are ordered by ascending bracketed number, bracketed alphanumeric identifier, or author–year combination.

The precise reference format is given by a style.

Software such as BibTeX can help you manage your reference list.

参考文献表 (也叫 bibliography) 列出正文里引用过的所有来源, 并且只列这些; 通常放在结论之后. 条目按编号、字母数字标识或作者–年份升序排列. 具体格式由样式 (style) 规定, 期刊和会议一般会指定. BibTeX 这类软件可以管理文献表: 把文献信息存在 .bib 文件里, 正文 \cite 到哪条, 文献表里就只出现哪些, 格式也自动统一.

五张幻灯片各给一种典型条目 (这里合在一张卡片里, 依次是第 23–27 张): ① 书: 作者. 书名. 出版社, 年份. ② 书中的一章: 作者. 章名. In 编者 (editors), 书名, 卷, 页码. 出版社, 年份, 再加 DOI 链接. ③ 会议论文: 作者. 论文题目. In 编者, 会议名 (FMCO 2007), 所在丛书的卷号 (LNCS 第 5382 卷), 页码. 出版社, 年份. ④ 期刊论文: 作者. 题目. 期刊名 卷 (期), 页码, 年份 (pp. 1:1–1:26 是「第 1 篇文章的第 1–26 页」, ACM 期刊按文章编页). ⑤ 网页: 作者或网站. 标题. URL, 加访问日期, 因为网页会变.

注意论文题目用 sentence case (只有首词和专有名词大写: Computing small clause normal forms), 书名和期刊名用 title case; 和下面第 28 张说的「统一大写」一致.

幻灯片小错 (第 25 张): 会议论文条目结尾写成「Springer. 2007.」, 出版社和年份之间是句点; 同一讲的其他条目都用逗号 (「Elsevier, 2001.」「Picador, 2018.」), 应为「Springer, 2007.」. 这正好是第 28 张要求清理的那种不统一.

Slide 28

Cleaning Up the Reference List

Once you have compiled your reference list, you must clean it up to make it correct and uniform.

  • Do you abbreviate journal names consistently?
  • Do you use conference acronyms and initialisms consistently?
  • Do you capitalize and italicize consistently?
  • Do you provide page numbers, publishers, and URLs systematically?

Also look out for duplicate entries.

All the information, including author names, should be as it appears in the document you are referencing. If you are citing Joe Leslie-Hurd but the document states Joe Hurd as the author, write Joe Hurd.

On the other hand, you should capitalize titles uniformly—e.g., sentence case for paper titles, title case for book titles.

文献表编完后要清理, 做到正确且统一: 期刊名是否一律缩写或一律不缩写? 会议简称是否一致? 大小写和斜体是否一致? 页码、出版社、URL 是否都给了? 有没有重复条目 (同一篇文献在 .bib 里有两个键很常见)? 作者名等信息要按被引文献上印的写: 那篇文献署名 Joe Hurd, 就写 Joe Hurd, 即使他后来改名为 Joe Leslie-Hurd. 但标题的大小写要统一调整: 论文题目用 sentence case, 书名用 title case.

BibTeX 的一个常见坑: 默认样式会把论文题目改成 sentence case, 于是专有名词和缩写 (SAT、QBF、Haskell) 被改成小写. 在 .bib 里用花括号保护它们: title = {Certification for {SAT} and {QBF}}.

Slide 30–31

When to Quote

Quote if:

  • The quotation expresses your meaning better than you can yourself.
  • The quotation is beautiful or witty.
  • The quotation provides evidence.
  • You expect the quotation to touch a chord of association with your readers.

When Not to Quote

Do not quote if:

  • The original text brings neither color nor evidence.
  • You want to show that you are learned.

什么时候照搬原话: 原话比你自己说得更好; 原话优美或机智; 原话能作证据; 你预计它能在读者心里引起共鸣 (touch a chord of association, 唤起联想). 什么时候不要: 原文既不生动也不能作证据; 只是想显得自己有学问.

Slide 32

Quotations with Citations

The citation accompanying a quotation should appear outside the quotation, either before or after. Compare:

As Seidl wrote, “to increase the trust in a solving result, modern solvers are expected to produce certificates [36].”

As Seidl [36] wrote, “to increase the trust in a solving result, modern solvers are expected to produce certificates.”
As Seidl wrote [36], “to increase the trust in a solving result, modern solvers are expected to produce certificates.”
As Seidl wrote, “to increase the trust in a solving result, modern solvers are expected to produce certificates” [36].

引语的出处要放在引号外面, 前后都行: 放在作者名后、动词后, 或者右引号后 (此时句号移到引用之后: certificates” [36].). 放进引号里就像是原文自带的引用, 篡改了原话.

Slide 33

Inlined vs. Displayed Quotations

Short quotations (up to about 40 words) should usually be inlined—e.g.:

Was it Aho who quipped that “premature optimization is the root of all evil”?

Longer quotations look better displayed, without quotation marks—e.g.:

As Seidl [36] points out:

Despite the progress made over the last years, proof checking is still expensive. Examples for open challenges concern the reduction of proof sizes and parallelization of proof checking. Furthermore, for QBFs more emphasis needs to be spent on the certification of true formulas and on finding a unified proof format that is supported by all state-of-the-art QBF solvers in a similar manner as DRAT is supported by state-of-the-art SAT solvers.

短引语 (大约 40 个词以内) 放在正文里, 加引号; 长引语单独成段、整体缩进, 不加引号 (缩进本身就表示这是引语). LaTeX 里用 quote 或 quotation 环境. 注意第一个例子的问号在右引号外面: 问的是整句, 不是引语本身 (见 STE 06 的引号一节).

例句「Was it Aho who quipped …?」问的是「这话是不是 Aho 说的」, 答案是否定的: 「premature optimization is the root of all evil」通常认为出自 Donald Knuth 1974 年的论文 “Structured Programming with go to Statements” (Knuth 后来把它归于 Tony Hoare, 出处至今说法不一). 第 36 张就写成了「Knuth quipped that …」, 所以第 33 张多半是故意设问, 或是一处前后不一致.

Slide 34

Changes in Quotations

Your quotations should generally be faithful to the original.

Exceptionally, you can correct or modify a quotation or add information to it using square brackets ([ ])—e.g.:

“To increase the trust in a solving result, modern [SAT and QBF] solvers are expected to produce certificates” [36].

You can also delete words from the middle of a quotation using ellipsis ( … ) as long as this does not distort the meaning. There is no need to put the ellipsis in square brackets in English—e.g.:

“After many years I’ve finally come to realize that my main strength lies in an ability to delegate work … rather than to go it alone” [19].

引语原则上要忠于原文. 例外: 可以用方括号 [ ] 修正、改动或补充信息 (modern [SAT and QBF] solvers, 方括号里是引用者加的); 可以用省略号 … 删去中间的词, 只要不扭曲原意. 英语里省略号不必再加方括号 (德语学术写作习惯写成 […]).

Slide 35

Paraphasing and Emphasis

If the quotation needs a lot of modification, you should probably paraphrase instead. But even if you paraphrase, you must acknowledge your sources and clearly distinguish what is yours and what is not yours.

You can point out a mistake in a quotation using [sic]. However, doing so looks arrogant. Consider paraphrasing instead.

If you emphasize parts of a quotation using italics, indicate that the italics are yours. A parenthetical italics mine suffices. If the italics are not yours, indicate that as well.

引语要改很多时, 不如转述 (paraphrase); 但转述也必须注明出处, 并分清哪些是你的、哪些不是. 可以用 [sic] (拉丁文「原文如此」) 指出引语里的错误, 但显得傲慢, 不如改为转述. 用斜体强调引语中的某部分时, 要注明斜体是你加的: 括注「(italics mine)」即可; 原文本来就是斜体时也要说明 (常写 italics in original).

幻灯片笔误: 标题「Paraphasing」应为 Paraphrasing (少了 r). 正文里拼对了 (paraphrase).

Slide 36

Capitalization of Quotations

Adapt the capitalization of your citations to their surroundings. You can perform this change silently. Compare:

Knuth quipped that “Premature optimization is the root of all evil.”
Knuth quipped that “[p]remature optimization is the root of all evil.”

Knuth quipped that “premature optimization is the root of all evil.”

引语的首字母大小写要随上下文调整, 而且可以不声不响地改. 引语接在「that」后面、成了句子的一部分, 就该小写; 写成大写不对, 写成「[p]remature」(用方括号标出改动) 也不必要, 显得啰嗦.

用词不一致: 「Adapt the capitalization of your citations」说的是引语, 应为 quotations. 第 3 张刚定义过, citation 是正文里对来源的提及 ([36] 这类), 照搬的原话是 quotation; 这一页的标题也写的是 Capitalization of Quotations.

Slide 37

Quoting Mathematics

If your work is mathematical, it might rely on existing definitions and theorems.

Quoting is rarely an option. You will have your own notations and your own numbering for the definition, lemma, and theorem environments.

This leaves paraphrasing, which is difficult since mathematics are highly formulaic. Make sure to properly acknowledge your sources.

数学性的工作常常依赖已有的定义和定理. 这时几乎没法照搬原文: 你有自己的记号, 定义、引理、定理也有自己的编号. 只能转述, 而数学高度公式化, 转述并不容易 (换个说法很容易改变意思). 无论如何都要注明来源, 例如「Definition 3 (adapted from [12])」.

语法: 「mathematics are highly formulaic」中 mathematics 指数学这门学科, 是不可数名词, 动词用单数: mathematics is. (只有在「the mathematics of this proof are …」这类指具体运算的少数用法里, 英式英语偶尔用复数.)

Slide 38

Quoting Non-English Sources

As a courtesy to your readers, provide a translation for any non-English quotation. You can still include the original for color.

To quote Johann Wolfgang von Goethe: “Mathematicians are like Frenchmen: Whenever you say something to them, they translate it into their own language, and at once it is something entirely different.”1

1 “Die Mathematiker sind eine Art Franzosen: Redet man zu ihnen, so übersetzen sie es in ihre Sprache, und dann ist es alsobald ganz etwas anders.”

引用非英语原文时, 出于对读者的礼貌, 要给出译文; 原文可以一并附上 (例如放在脚注里), 增添色彩. 例子是歌德的名言: 「数学家就像法国人: 你对他们说什么, 他们就翻译成自己的语言, 于是马上变成了完全不同的东西.」

引文笔误: 歌德原文 (《箴言与反思》, Maximen und Reflexionen) 末尾是「ganz etwas anderes」. 「etwas anders」在这里不合语法: etwas 后面要接名词化的形容词 anderes (「别的东西」), anders 是副词.

Slide 39

Epigraphs

Epigraphs are quotations displayed at the beginning of documents or chapters, or occasionally at the end.

You sometimes find them in PhD theses and books.

Epigraphs should have some connection to the chapter. They should stand on their own without requiring any alterations or explanations. Epigraphs are exceptional in that they do not need a complete citation.

If you quote a fictional character, make sure to include their name—e.g.:

Have you redrafted the redraft of your draft?
— Sir Humphrey in Yes Minister (1980)

题词 (epigraph) 是放在文档或章节开头 (偶尔在结尾) 的一段引语, 常见于博士论文和书. 它应当和这一章有些联系, 而且能独立成立, 不需要改动或解释. 题词是例外, 不需要完整的引用信息, 写出说话人和出处即可; 引用虚构人物的话时, 要写上人物的名字. 例子出自英国情景喜剧《是, 大臣》(Yes Minister, 1980) 里的公务员 Sir Humphrey: 「你重新起草过你那份草稿的重写稿了吗?」, 正好点出下一讲的主题: 修改 (revisions).
