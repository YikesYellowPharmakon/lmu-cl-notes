八大词类
Words · The Traditional Parts of Speech

Slide 3

The Traditional Parts of Speech

Parts of speech are the word types. Traditionally, English grammars distinguish between eight parts of speech:

  • nouns (e.g., computer);
  • pronouns (e.g., you);
  • verbs (e.g., eat);
  • adjectives (e.g., appropriate);
  • adverbs (e.g., however);
  • prepositions (e.g., in);
  • conjunctions (e.g., but);
  • interjections (e.g., alas).

What matters is how words behave in a sentence: But and however have nearly the same meaning but are categorized differently.

Understanding the parts of speech is necessary to understand English grammar, which in turn will help you write better.

Parts of speech 词类: 传统英语语法分八类: 名词、代词、动词、形容词、副词、介词、连词、感叹词. 决定词类的是词在句中的用法, 而不是意思: but 和 however 意思几乎相同, 但 but 是连词, however 是副词. 区别在于: but 能直接连接两个句子 (It works, but it is slow.); however 不能, 两句之间要用分号或句号 (It works; however, it is slow.). 写成「It works, however it is slow.」是常见错误 (comma splice). 理解词类是理解英语语法的前提, 进而有助于写好.

Slide 4–5

Nouns

A noun names a thing or a concept. It can be a common noun (lowercased) or a proper noun (usually uppercased).

Examples of common nouns: coffee, snake, summer.

Examples of proper nouns: iPhone, Python, Switzerland, The Da Vinci Code.

Pronouns

A pronoun stands for a noun or another pronoun, called the antecedent. You should ensure the antecedent is clear from the context.

Examples of pronouns: you, she, his, itself, they, whom.

Below, who is a pronoun that stands for the antecedent Jessica:

It was Jessica who opened the windows.

名词指称事物或概念, 分普通名词 (小写) 和专有名词 (通常大写): 咖啡、蛇、夏天是普通名词; iPhone、Python、Switzerland、《达·芬奇密码》是专有名词. 「usually」是因为有 iPhone 这种以小写开头的专有名词.

代词代替一个名词或另一个代词, 被代替的叫antecedent 先行词. 要保证从上下文能看清先行词是谁. 例句里的 who 代指 Jessica. 技术写作里最常见的问题是 it 和 this 指代不清: 「The parser calls the lexer. It then …」, It 是 parser 还是 lexer?

Slide 6

Verbs

A verb describes an action or a state of being.

Examples of verbs: be, has, fly, displayed, transforms.

A verb is called transitive if it takes a direct object. Otherwise, it is called intransitive. Many verbs can be used both transitively and intransitively.

A transitive verb: She ate lunch.

An intransitive verb: She ate.

动词描述动作或状态. 带直接宾语的叫transitiv 及物动词, 不带的叫intransitiv 不及物动词. 很多动词两种用法都有: She ate lunch (及物) / She ate (不及物).

技术写作里要注意: 有些动词只能及物, 不能省宾语. 例如 allow 后面必须有宾语: 「The tool allows to compare files」是常见的德语式错误 (德语 erlaubt zu …), 应写「allows users to compare files」或「allows comparing files」.

Slide 7

Adjectives

An adjective modifies a noun.

Examples of adjectives: pink, wonderful, Austrian, many, this, such, five, my, which, each, some, the, a.

An adjective as an attributive: They bought a beige sofa.

An adjective as the complement of a verb: The sofa is beige.

形容词修饰名词. 传统语法把很多「限定词」也算作形容词: 数词 (five)、指示词 (this)、所有格 (my)、冠词 (the, a) 等; 现代语法一般把它们单列为 determiner 限定词. 形容词有两种用法: 放在名词前作定语 (a beige sofa), 或放在系动词后作表语 (The sofa is beige). 这个区别决定了复合形容词要不要加连字符 (见 STE 01).

Slide 8

Adverbs

An adverb modifies a verb, an adjective, another adverb, or an entire sentence.

Examples of adverbs: very, quickly, extremely, however, now, here, too.

An adverb modifying a verb: She ran quickly.
An adverb modifying an adjective: This color is very dark.
An adverb modifying an adverb: It all went really fast.
A sentence adverb: Unfortunately, there is no solution.

副词修饰动词、形容词、另一个副词或整个句子. 四个例子分别对应这四种: ran quickly; very dark; really fast; 句首的 Unfortunately 修饰整句 (sentence adverb 句子副词), 后面加逗号.

Slide 9–11

Prepositions

A preposition links a phrase with another part of the sentence, indicating the relationship between them.

Examples of prepositions: in, as, for, on, about, below, between, throughout.

Three prepositions in a sentence: Dongchen moved from Xi’an to Munich in May.

Conjunctions

A conjunction connects sentences, clauses, or words.

Examples: and, or, but, because, if, both–and, either–or, therefore.

Two important types:

  • Coordinating conjunctions join grammatical expressions of equal rank.
  • Subordinating conjunctions join grammatical expressions of unequal rank.

A coordinating conjunction: Prove or disprove the following statement.
A subordinating conjunction: Raise your hand if you know the answer.

Interjection

An interjection (also called “exclamation”) denotes an abrupt, brief remark.

Examples: alas, ah, well, ouch.

There is virtually no place for interjections in formal writing.

介词把一个短语和句子的其他部分联系起来, 表明两者的关系. 例句里有三个: from Xi’an, to Munich, in May.

连词连接句子、从句或词. both–and、either–or 是成对使用的关联连词 (中间用 en dash 表示「成对」). 两大类: 并列连词连接地位相同的成分 (Prove or disprove: 两个祈使动词并列); 从属连词连接地位不同的成分, 引出从句 (Raise your hand if you know the answer: if 从句从属于主句).

感叹词 (也叫 exclamation) 表示突然的简短感叹: alas (唉)、ah、well、ouch (哎哟). 正式写作里几乎没有感叹词的位置.

幻灯片前后不一致: 连词的例子里有 therefore. 但 therefore 和 Slide 3 归为副词的 however 是同一类词 (连接副词, conjunctive adverb): 它不能单独用逗号连接两个句子 (不能写「It is fast, therefore it is used」, 要写「It is fast; therefore, it is used」或「It is fast, so it is used」). 按 Slide 3 强调的「看词在句中的用法」, therefore 应该归为副词.

Slide 12

Etymology

A word’s etymology refers to its origin.

Etymology can be fascinating, but it does not give a word’s true meaning. For example:

  • thesaurus is derived from the Greek word θησαυρός, meaning “storehouse, treasure,” but in English it refers to a book of synonyms.
  • muscle is derived from the Latin word musculus, meaning “little mouse.”

A word’s true meaning can be found in a dictionary.

Etymology 词源: 词的来源. 词源很有趣, 但不决定一个词的真正意思. thesaurus 来自希腊语「仓库, 宝藏」, 在英语里却指同义词词典; muscle (肌肉) 来自拉丁语「小老鼠」(肌肉在皮肤下动起来像小老鼠). 词的真正意思要查词典. 也就是说, 不能用「这个词本来的意思是……」来为一个现代用法错误辩护.

拼写、缩写与复数
Spelling · Abbreviations · Plurals

Slide 13

Spelling

Even if spelling is your forte, you might miss a subtle misspelling such as arithemtic, occurence, and accomodate. Any spellchecker will flag these. But it might not catch lead in This lead to an explosion. (Change to led.)

Some words have multiple spellings—e.g., judgment vs. judgement. Often, one will mainly be American and the other, British, so you can simply choose the appropriate variant. In cases such as formulas vs. formulae, you can follow your preference or Google Books Ngram Viewer.

拼写: 即使你很会拼写, 也可能漏掉细微的拼写错误, 例如 arithemtic (→ arithmetic)、occurence (→ occurrence)、accomodate (→ accommodate, 两个 c 两个 m). 这些拼写检查器都能发现. 但它可能发现不了 This lead to an explosion 里的 lead: lead 本身是正确的单词 (铅; 带领), 这里应该用 lead 的过去式 led. 有些词有多种拼法, 例如 judgment/judgement, 通常一个是美式一个是英式, 选对应的即可; formulas/formulae 这种情况可以按个人偏好, 或查 Ngram Viewer 看哪种更常用.

句子「one will mainly be American and the other, British」里 the other 后面的逗号代替了省略的 will mainly be (gapping), 这是正式英语的写法.

Slide 14

Abbreviations

An abbreviation is a shortened form of a word or phrase.

Some abbreviations belong to the written language (e.g., Dr. or Dr). Others exist also in speech (e.g., exam for examination).

Written-only abbreviations usually end with a period (.). However, in British English, the period is omitted if the abbreviation ends with the original word’s last letter (e.g., Dr, Mr, vs).

Abbreviation 缩写: 词或短语的缩短形式. 有的只存在于书面 (Dr. 读作 doctor); 有的也用于口语 (exam). 只用于书面的缩写一般以句点结尾. 但英式英语里, 如果缩写的最后一个字母就是原词的最后一个字母, 就不加句点: Dr (Doctor)、Mr (Mister)、vs (versus). 美式英语一律写 Dr.、Mr.、vs.

Slide 15

Abbreviations

Two special types of abbreviations:

  • An acronym is an abbreviation consisting of the initial letters of other words and pronounced as a word (e.g., ASCII).
  • An initialism is an abbreviation consisting of the initial letters of other words pronounced separately (e.g., CPU).

In American English, some two-letter initialisms are spelled with periods (e.g., U.S.).

两种特殊的缩写: acronym 首字母拼音词由各词首字母组成, 并像一个词那样读 (ASCII 读作 /ˈæski/); initialism 首字母缩写词也由首字母组成, 但逐个字母读 (CPU 读 C-P-U). 美式英语里有些两个字母的首字母缩写词带句点 (U.S.).

这个区别影响冠词: 冠词 a/an 看的是读音而不是拼写. 所以是 an SQL query (如果读作 S-Q-L, 「es」是元音开头) 或 a SQL query (如果读作 sequel); an FPGA, a URL (读作 U-R-L, 「you」是辅音开头).

Slide 16

Abbreviations

Use abbreviations sparingly. They can be cryptic and off-putting, and are hard to find using a search engine. Does AR stand for augmented reality or automated reasoning? For such short phrases, do you really need an abbreviation?

The Latin abbreviations e.g., i.e., and etc. are generally acceptable. Beyond this, iff (if and only if) and w.r.t. (with respect to) are acceptable in mathematical texts.

Lengthy phrases such as nondeterministic finite automaton might conveniently be shortened to NFA. But often it suffices to write automaton. If you choose to write NFA, introduce it in parentheses at the first occurrence and use NFA from then on. It is good style to define virtually all abbreviations on first use and to avoid them in titles and headings.

If you use a lengthy phrase only once, do not introduce an abbreviation, unless the abbreviation is better known than the phrase.

缩写要少用: 它们可能晦涩、让人反感, 也难以用搜索引擎查. AR 是 augmented reality (增强现实) 还是 automated reasoning (自动推理)? 这么短的短语真的需要缩写吗? 拉丁缩写 e.g.、i.e.、etc. 一般可以用; 数学文本里还可以用 iff (当且仅当) 和 w.r.t. (关于).

长短语如 nondeterministic finite automaton 可以缩写成 NFA, 但往往直接写 automaton 就够了. 决定用 NFA 的话, 第一次出现时在括号里引入 (nondeterministic finite automaton (NFA)), 之后一直用 NFA. 好的文体是: 几乎所有缩写都在第一次使用时定义, 标题和小标题里不用缩写. 一个长短语只用一次时不要引入缩写, 除非缩写比全称更有名 (例如 DNA, NASA).

Many nouns have both a singular and a plural form. Regular plural formation adds the suffix -s or -es. But there are many exceptions, including the following:

不规则复数, 大多来自拉丁语或希腊语: -ix → -ices, -on → -a, -is → -es, -us → -ora (corpus), -um → -a. 计算语言学里最常用的是 corpus/corpora (语料库). 最常见的错误是把复数当单数: 「a criteria」「this phenomena」应为 a criterion、this phenomenon. data 在计算机科学里常当作不可数的单数用 (the data is …), 也可以严格当复数 (the data are …), 全文统一即可.

时态与语气
Tenses · Moods · Shall vs. Will · Past vs. Present Perfect

Slide 18

Tenses

English distinguishes between three main verb tenses:

  • present (e.g., walk);
  • past (e.g., walked);
  • future (e.g., will walk).

Both the past and the present tense are common in academic writing:

David A. Huffman invented the algorithm around 1950.
Our study confirms that system programmers prefer Rust to C.

When quoting Shafi Goldwasser, both Goldwasser writes and Goldwasser wrote work, but try to be locally consistent.

英语有三个主要时态: 现在时、过去时、将来时. 学术写作里过去时和现在时都很常见: 历史事实用过去时 (Huffman 在 1950 年左右发明了这个算法); 当前成立的结论用现在时 (我们的研究证实系统程序员更喜欢 Rust 而不是 C; 注意 prefer A to B). 引用别人的观点时, Goldwasser writes 和 Goldwasser wrote 都可以, 但要在局部保持一致.

Huffman 编码是 Huffman 1951 年在 MIT 读研究生时想出的, 论文发表于 1952 年, 所以「around 1950」是大致的说法.

Slide 19

Tenses

The future tense competes with the present tense. Compare:

In Section 8, we will describe our experiments.
In Section 8, we describe our experiments.

Knuth et al. recommend either of two approaches concerning tenses of verbs:

Either use present tense throughout the entire paper, or write sequentially. Sequential writing means that you say things like, “We saw this before. We will see this later.” The sequential approach is more appropriate for lengthy papers.

将来时和现在时之间有竞争: 「第 8 节我们将描述实验」和「第 8 节我们描述实验」都可以. Knuth 等人推荐两种做法之一: 要么全文都用现在时; 要么「按顺序写」, 也就是说「我们之前看过这个. 我们之后会看到那个」(把文章当作读者一起走过的一段路). 按顺序写更适合长篇论文.

Slide 20

Moods

English distinguishes between five verb moods:

  • indicative (for facts);
  • imperative (for commands);
  • interrogative (for questions);
  • optative (for wishes);
  • subjunctive (for conditions).

In modern English, the optative and the subjunctive moods are often replaced by the indicative mood. Compare:

I wish I were going.
I wish I was going.
They require that the resources be released at the end.
They require that the resources are released at the end.

语气 (mood): 陈述语气 (事实)、祈使语气 (命令)、疑问语气 (问题)、祈愿语气 (愿望)、虚拟语气 (条件). 现代英语里, 祈愿和虚拟语气常被陈述语气取代. 对比: I wish I were going (虚拟) 和 I wish I was going (陈述); They require that the resources be released (虚拟, 动词原形) 和 … are released (陈述). 每组第一句是正式写法, 第二句在日常英语里也常见.

正式技术写作建议用虚拟语气: require/recommend/insist that + 动词原形, 这在 API 文档里很常见 (The specification requires that the method return null.).

不同语法书对英语有几种语气说法不一: 很多英语语法只区分三种 (陈述、祈使、虚拟), 把疑问看作句型而不是语气, 把祈愿 (God save the Queen) 归入虚拟语气. 幻灯片的五分法是一种说法, 考试按幻灯片答.

Should you write we shall see or we will see?

Summary of the traditional approach:

you shallyou will–

you shallyou will–

Traditionally, you would write we shall see rather than we will see. Notice how the I and we rows are shifted to the right.

shall 还是 will? 传统用法: 表示单纯的将来 (「going to」那一列), 第一人称 (I, we) 用 shall, 第二、三人称用 will; 表示意愿 (「want」), 第一人称用 will; 表示义务、命令 (「must」), 第二、三人称用 shall. 所以表里 I 和 we 两行「向右移了一格」. 按传统, 「我们将会看到」写 we shall see.

Slide 22

Shall vs. Will

Compare:

I shall drown; no one will save me!
I will drown; no one shall save me!

According to the traditional approach:

  • The first speaker is expecting to drown.
  • The second speaker is expressing suicidal intent.

经典例子: 「I shall drown; no one will save me!」按传统用法, I shall 是单纯的将来, no one will 也是单纯的将来: 「我要淹死了, 没人会救我!」(呼救). 「I will drown; no one shall save me!」里 I will 表示意愿, no one shall 表示命令: 「我就要淹死, 谁也不准救我!」(想自杀). 这是英语教科书里流传很久的笑话 (常说是一个落水的外国人用反了, 结果没人救他).

Slide 23–24

Shall vs. Will

Summary of the modern approach:

–I will
you shallyou will
she/he/it shallshe/he/it will

you shallyou will

Using the modern approach, you would write we will see rather than we shall see.

You can choose either approach, but do not mix and match. The modern approach is slightly less formal. It is also easier to use.

Regardless of the approach, beware of using shall instead of will with the second or third person. Example from a course’s web site:

The course shall cover the textbook’s first four chapters.

The course will cover the textbook’s first four chapters.

现代用法: 单纯的将来一律用 will (I will, we will); shall 只用于第二、三人称表示义务 (第一人称的「must」和「want」两列都没有了). 按现代用法写 we will see. 两种做法任选, 但不要混用; 现代用法稍微不那么正式, 也更容易用.

不管用哪种, 第二、三人称都不要用 shall 表示单纯的将来. 某课程网站上写「The course shall cover …」, 按两种用法 shall 在这里都表示「必须」(像合同条款), 而作者想说的只是「本课程将讲……」, 应该用 will. 反过来, 规范文档里正是用 shall 表示强制要求 (The system shall log every access.).

Slide 25

Past vs. Present Perfect

Should you write we developed (past) or we have developed (present perfect)?

Generally:

  • Use the past tense for an action that took place in the past and is not continuing now.
  • Use the present perfect tense when the action started in the past and is continuing now.

A gray area is the conclusion of a thesis or paper. Both forms are widely used—e.g.:

We presented a technique to reconstruct a network from the observations of the nodes’ phase dynamics.

We have presented a technique to reconstruct a network from the observations of the nodes’ phase dynamics.

一般过去时还是现在完成时? 一般来说: 动作发生在过去、现在不再继续, 用过去时; 动作从过去开始、现在仍在继续 (或结果仍然有效), 用现在完成时. 论文的结论是灰色地带, 两种都常见: We presented a technique … / We have presented a technique … (我们 (已经) 提出了一种根据节点相位动态的观测重建网络的技术).

德语母语者要注意: 德语口语用现在完成时讲过去的事 (Ich habe gestern … gemacht), 英语不行: 有明确的过去时间时只能用一般过去时 (We developed the tool in 2024, 不能写 we have developed … in 2024).

Slide 26

Above and Below

Phrases such as the diagram above, the above code, or the table below (but not the below formula) refer to material presented before or after the current sentence.

The material above or below does not need to literally occur on the same page.

above 和 below: the diagram above (上面的图)、the above code (上面的代码)、the table below (下面的表) 都可以; 但 below 不能放在名词前, 不能写 the below formula. 也就是说 above 可前可后, below 只能后置. 「上面」「下面」的内容不必真的在同一页上.

人名与所有格
Phrases · Personal Names · Possessives

Slide 28

Personal Names

Suppose you want to refer to Emmy Noether. Should you write Emmy Noether, E. Noether, or Noether?

Here is a reasonable strategy:

  • If the name is followed by a citation to a paper by the same person, Noether suffices. After all, interested readers can follow the citation.
  • If no citation is present, write Emmy Noether the first time for clarity, then continue with Noether. Apply this rule regardless of how famous she is.
  • In the reference list, write Emmy Noether, E. Noether, Noether, Emmy, or Noether, E., but be consistent.

What about Professor? In most scientific writing, academic titles are not used.

人名: 提到 Emmy Noether (数学家, 诺特定理), 写全名、E. Noether 还是 Noether? 合理的做法: 名字后面紧跟着她本人论文的引用时, 写 Noether 就够了, 想了解的读者可以查引用; 没有引用时, 第一次写全名 Emmy Noether, 之后写 Noether, 不管她多有名都这样. 参考文献列表里四种写法都可以, 但要统一. Professor 这类学术头衔在大多数科学写作里不用 (德语习惯写 Prof. Dr., 英语论文里不写).

Slide 29

Personal Names

Names with particles, such as Nicolaas Govert de Bruijn and John von Neumann, pose special challenges.

  • When the family name starts a sentence, capitalize the particle.
  • In the middle of a sentence, if the first names are omitted, write De Bruijn (following a Dutch convention) but von Neumann.

Personal names from China, Japan, and some other countries put the family name before the first name. However, such names are often reversed to comply with Western conventions. For Yang Chen, is Yang the given or the family name? You can often find out by inspecting the self-citations in Yang Chen’s papers.

A separate challenge is to determine Yang Chen’s pronouns. If you fail to obtain this piece of information, use singular they, or reword to avoid the issue.

带小品词的姓名, 如 Nicolaas Govert de Bruijn (荷兰数学家) 和 John von Neumann, 有特殊的难处: 姓氏在句首时, 小品词大写 (De Bruijn showed …, Von Neumann proposed …); 在句中省略名字时, 按荷兰习惯写 De Bruijn (荷兰人单独用姓时 de 大写), 但写 von Neumann (德语的 von 小写).

中国、日本等国家的姓名姓在前、名在后, 但常为了照顾西方习惯而颠倒. Yang Chen 里的 Yang 是名还是姓? 通常可以看这个人自己论文里的自引用 (参考文献里写成 Chen, Y. 还是 Yang, C.). 另一个难处是确定 Yang Chen 用什么代词; 查不到就用单数 they, 或改写句子避开.

对中文读者: 你自己写英文论文时, 名字的顺序要全文和所有出版物里保持一致 (例如一直用 Given Family 的西式顺序), 并可以把姓全部大写来消除歧义 (Yang CHEN), 但后者在计算机领域不常见.

Slide 30

Possessives

The possessive of singular nouns is generally formed with ’s. The possessive of plural nouns is generally formed with an apostrophe (’).

Examples: Monica’s novel, the Müllers’ textbook, Charles’s (or Charles’) thriller.

There is a subtle distinction between the following:

  • Tremblay and Wilson’s books (their shared books);
  • Tremblay’s and Wilson’s books (their respective books).

The possessive of pronouns is written without an apostrophe—e.g., its name, not it’s name.

所有格: 单数名词加 ’s (Monica’s novel); 以 s 结尾的复数名词只加撇号 (the Müllers’ textbook, 穆勒一家的教科书). 以 s 结尾的单数名词两种都行: Charles’s 或 Charles’. 细微区别: Tremblay and Wilson’s books 是两人合著的书 (只在最后一个名字加 ’s); Tremblay’s and Wilson’s books 是两人各自的书. 代词的所有格不加撇号: its name, 不是 it’s name (it’s = it is).

Pitfalls

Adjectives as adverbs
A common mistake, especially by German speakers, is to use adjectives in place of adverbs—e.g., the program runs automatic instead of the program runs automatically. However, due to a phenomenon called “flat adverbs,” adjectives can be used as adverbs in less formal settings—e.g., drive slow.
Archaisms
Avoid old-fashioned words such as albeit, behoof, and betwixt.
Foreign words
Foreign words that are well integrated into the language need no accents or italics—e.g., role (not rôle), naive (not naïve). The French phrase à la (“in the style of”) is widely understood but informal. Rare foreign words are best avoided unless you explain them.
  • 形容词当副词用: 德语母语者常犯, 因为德语的形容词可以直接作副词 (Das Programm läuft automatisch). 英语要用副词: runs automatically. 不过由于所谓的「平副词」(flat adverbs) 现象, 在不太正式的场合形容词也可以当副词用, 例如 drive slow (慢点开); 正式写作里仍写 slowly.
  • 古语词: 避免过时的词, 如 albeit (虽然, 改用 although)、behoof (利益)、betwixt (在……之间, 改用 between).
  • 外来词: 已经融入英语的外来词不加重音符号、不用斜体: role 而不是 rôle, naive 而不是 naïve. 法语短语 à la (以……的风格) 大家都懂, 但不正式. 罕见的外来词最好别用, 除非你加以解释. 德语词 Zettelkasten 在 STE 02 里就是用斜体并给出了例子的情形.

Malapropisms
A malapropism is the mistake of using a word in place of a similar-sounding one, sometimes with comical outcomes. A few problematic pairs follow:
affecteffect
composecomprise
delegaterelegate
discreetdiscrete
ensureinsure
parableparabola
principalprinciple
proposalproposition
sensiblesensitive

Malapropism 词语误用: 用了一个发音相近的错词, 有时很滑稽. 几组容易混淆的词:

affect / effectaffect 一般是动词「影响」; effect 一般是名词「效果」(作动词时是「实现」: effect a change)
compose / comprise整体 comprises 部分 (The system comprises three modules); 部分 compose 整体 (Three modules compose the system). 「is comprised of」被很多人视为错误
delegate / relegatedelegate 委派 (任务给别人); relegate 降级、贬到次要位置
discreet / discretediscreet 谨慎的、低调的; discrete 离散的 (discrete mathematics)

parable / parabolaparable 寓言; parabola 抛物线

Slide 34

Pitfalls

Near-synonyms
Beware of words, such as simple and simplistic, that have almost the same meaning but not exactly.
Noun trains
Avoid noun phrases such as network packet traffic analysis tool. Use prepositions—e.g., traffic analysis tool for network packets.
Sticky words
These are words that stick in the reader’s mind, so they should be used sparingly. For example, also, but, and now should be used at most once within a paragraph.
  • 近义词: 意思几乎相同但不完全一样的词, 例如 simple (简单的, 褒义或中性) 和 simplistic (过分简单化的, 贬义). 说别人的方法 simplistic 是批评.
  • 名词火车: 避免一长串名词堆在一起, 如 network packet traffic analysis tool (五个名词连用, 读者要从后往前解析). 用介词拆开: traffic analysis tool for network packets. 德语的复合词习惯 (Netzwerkpaketverkehrsanalysewerkzeug) 容易导致这个问题.
  • 黏性词: 会粘在读者脑子里的词, 要少用. 例如 also、but、now 在一段里最多用一次, 重复了读者会注意到.

Problem Words and Phrases

&
Spell out as and, except in the names of firms and such.
all vs. all of
Both have the same meaning. Trust your ear.
alternative
An alternative is another possibility. The word is not a synonym for option. We have two alternatives means something else than We have two options.
as well as
Prefer and when both work.
assist, assistance
These can usually be replaced by the less pretentious help.

易错词表按字母顺序排列. & 要写成 and, 公司名等除外 (AT&T). all 和 all of 意思相同, 凭语感 (all the files / all of the files; 代词前必须用 all of: all of them). alternative 是「另一种可能」, 不是 option 的同义词: 严格说, we have two alternatives 意思是「除了现在这个之外, 还有两种可能」(共三种), 而 we have two options 是「一共两个选项」. as well as 和 and 都行时用 and. assist、assistance 一般可以换成不那么做作的 help.

原文用词: 「means something else than」是德语式的说法 (etwas anderes als). 标准英语说 something other than 或 something different from.

Problem Words and Phrases

associate with vs. associate to
Associate to is ungrammatical.
bound vs. bounded
A bound variable is attached to a binder. A bounded variable ranges over a domain that is limited by a bound.
can vs. may
Prefer may for expressing permission, and use can otherwise—e.g., The user can scroll up and down using the scroll bar.
cannot
The negation of can is normally spelled as one word.
cf.
This Latin abbreviation means “compare with,” not “see.”

associate with, 不能写 associate to (德语 zuordnen 容易导致这个错误). bound 和 bounded: bound variable 是被约束的变量 (被 ∀、λ 等绑定符绑定); bounded variable 是取值范围有界的变量. can 和 may: 表示「允许」用 may, 其他情况用 can, 例如「用户可以用滚动条上下滚动」说的是能力, 用 can. can 的否定通常写成一个词 cannot (can not 只在特殊强调时用). cf. 是拉丁语 confer 的缩写, 意思是「比较」, 不是「参见」: 「cf. [12]」意味着读者应该把这里和 [12] 对比; 只是参见的话写 see [12].

Slide 38

Problem Words and Phrases

compare with vs. compare to
With is the correct preposition when emphasizing the differences—e.g., She compared C# with Java. To is correct if we want emphasize the similarities—e.g., He compared Napoleon to Charlemagne.
consider vs. consider as
Consider as is old-fashioned. Thus, write The fact is considered relevant, not The fact is considered as relevant.
e.g. vs. i.e.
Do not confuse these. They mean “for example” and “that is,” respectively. In American English, they are followed by a comma.
et al.
Be careful with the punctuation. The spelling is not et al nor et. al nor et. al.

compare with 强调差异 (把 C# 和 Java 相比较, 找异同); compare to 强调相似 (把拿破仑比作查理大帝). consider as 过时, 写 is considered relevant. e.g. = for example (例如, 列举不完整); i.e. = that is (即, 换一种说法, 完整等价). 美式英语里两者后面都加逗号 (e.g., this). et al. 是拉丁语 et alii 的缩写: et 是完整的词 (不加点), al. 是缩写 (加点); 所以 et al、et. al、et. al. 都错.

原文笔误: 「To is correct if we want emphasize the similarities」漏了 to, 应为 if we want to emphasize.

Problem Words and Phrases

the fact that
Avoid this verbose phrase.
fewer vs. less
Prefer fewer to less with countable nouns—e.g., fewer lines of code.
the former, the latter
These are often clumsier, and more puzzling, than the words they replace. Use only if n=2.
good, bad
Instead of telling the reader that something is good or bad, tell them why it is good or bad.

the fact that 很啰嗦, 避免 (见 STE 03 Slide 13). 可数名词用 fewer, 不可数用 less: fewer lines of code, less memory. the former / the latter (前者 / 后者) 常常比它们代替的词更笨拙、更让人困惑 (读者得回头找), 而且只能用于正好两项 (n=2). 不要只告诉读者某样东西好或坏, 要告诉他们为什么好或坏.

Problem Words and Phrases

in-, un-, non-, a-
All these prefixes denote negation. Check a dictionary to choose the right prefix in a given situation.
in general
The phrase can mean both “almost always” and “always.” Be careful in mathematical contexts that require precision.
interesting
Avoid this vague word. Instead, tell the reader why something is interesting.
-like vs. -style
Compare D is a C-like language (i.e., “the D language is like C”) with D has a C-style syntax (i.e., “D has a syntax in the style of C’s syntax”).

in-、un-、non-、a- 都表示否定, 具体用哪个查词典 (incorrect, unsound, nonempty, asymmetric; 意思也可能不同: unmoral / immoral / amoral). in general 既可以指「几乎总是」也可以指「总是」, 数学语境里要小心: 「in general, the problem is undecidable」到底是说一般情形不可判定, 还是说存在不可判定的实例? interesting 太含糊, 要说出为什么有趣. -like 和 -style: C-like language 指语言整体像 C; C-style syntax 指语法是 C 的风格.

little, big
As a rule, prefer the more formal small and large to little and big.
note that
Use this phrase sparingly. Often it can be omitted altogether.

Problem Words and Phrases

prior to
Needless variant of before.
quite
It can mean both “very” (especially in American English) and “fairly.” To avoid ambiguity, it should be avoided.
really, very
Use these adverbs with moderation. Often they can simply be omitted or replaced with a near-synonym such as substantially or highly.
this/these vs. that/those
In informal speech, people tend to say that/those where formal English would call for this/these—e.g., . . . the duality principle. That principle . . .

prior to 是 before 的多余变体. quite 既可以是「非常」(尤其在美式英语里) 也可以是「相当, 还算」(英式里常见), 有歧义, 应该避免: quite good 在美国人听来是「很好」, 在英国人听来可能是「还行」. really、very 要节制, 常可以删掉, 或换成 substantially、highly 之类. 刚提到的东西, 口语常用 that/those 回指, 正式英语用 this/these: 「… the duality principle. This principle …」.

Problem Words and Phrases

thusly
Needless variant of thus.
trivial
Do not use it as a synonym for easy. Similarly, avoid euphemisms such as highly nontrivial (for difficult).
unique
Use only when n=1. In particular, never write fairly unique.
utilize, utilization
Needless, pompous variants of use.
whom vs. who

thusly 是 thus 的多余变体. trivial (平凡的) 不要当 easy 用; 在数学里 trivial 有确切含义 (不需要任何论证); 同理避免 highly nontrivial 这种委婉语, 直接说 difficult. unique 只用于正好一个 (n=1): 唯一就是唯一, 没有「相当唯一」. utilize、utilization 是 use 的多余、做作的变体. 宾格用 whom: for whom this user’s manual is designed (这本用户手册是为他们设计的); 介词后面一定是 whom.
