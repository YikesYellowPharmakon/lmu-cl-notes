句子成分
Sentences · Roles of Words in Clauses

Slide 3

Roles of Words in Clauses

Words and phrases can assume the following roles in a clause:

  • subject;
  • verb;
  • direct object;
  • indirect object;
  • complement;
  • adverbial.

These roles are not to be confused with the parts of speech (noun, verb, etc.).

词和短语在从句 (clause, 含主谓的结构) 里可以充当这些角色: 主语、谓语动词、直接宾语、间接宾语、补足语、状语. 不要和上一讲的词类混淆: 词类是词本身的类型, 角色是它在句中的功能. 同一个名词可以作主语、宾语或补足语; 「verb」既是词类又是角色, 正是容易混淆的地方.

Slide 4–5

Subjects

The subject controls the verb in the clause.

Typically:

  • The subject is the doer.
  • The subject is the sentence’s topic.
  • The subject comes before the verb in the clause.

Examples of subjects:

She ate lunch.
Whom did you see?
Naomi and Yuta both looked exhausted.

Verbs

The verb describes an action or state. It forms the core of the clause. It agrees with the subject.

Examples of verbs:

She ate lunch.
Whom did you see?
Naomi and Yuta both looked exhausted.

主语支配动词 (动词的人称和数跟着主语变). 典型情况下, 主语是动作的执行者, 是句子的话题, 位于动词之前. 例句的主语: She; you (疑问句里主语在助动词 did 之后); Naomi and Yuta (并列主语, 所以动词按复数).

动词描述动作或状态, 是从句的核心, 和主语保持一致. 例句的动词: ate; did … see (助动词 + 实义动词, 被主语隔开); looked.

Slide 6–7

Direct Objects

The direct object receives the action of a transitive verb.

Examples of direct objects:

She ate lunch.
Whom did you see?
His grandparents gave him the money he needed.

Indirect Objects

The indirect object is affected by the action of a transitive verb, without being the direct object.

Examples of indirect objects:

Tell me a story.
His grandparents gave him the money he needed.

直接宾语承受及物动词的动作: lunch; Whom (疑问句里宾语提前, 所以用宾格 whom); the money he needed (整个短语是宾语). 间接宾语受动作影响, 但不是直接宾语, 通常是「给谁」: Tell me a story (给我讲个故事), gave him the money. 间接宾语可以改写成介词短语: gave the money to him.

Slide 8–9

Complements

A complement completes the verb’s meaning.

Examples of complements:

Put the dish on the table.
She is the one to whom they gave it.

Adverbials

An adverbial functions like an adverb.

Examples of adverbials:

He arrived after dinner.
Initially, we set i to 0.

补足语把动词的意思补完整, 去掉后句子不完整或意思改变: Put the dish 必须说放在哪里 (on the table); She is 后面必须说她是什么 (the one to whom they gave it, 表语也是补足语). 状语的作用像副词, 去掉后句子仍然完整: after dinner (时间); Initially (句首状语, 后面加逗号).

补足语和状语的区别: He arrived after dinner 去掉 after dinner 仍是完整的句子 (状语); Put the dish 去掉 on the table 就不成句了 (补足语).

信息的顺序
Topic, then Stress · Subject, then Verb · Short before Long · Left-Heavy vs. Right-Heavy

Slide 10

Topic, then Stress

The start of a sentence is the natural topic position. It announces the topic of the sentence. It often connects to previous material.

The end of a sentence is the natural stress position. It contains important new information.

Compare:

Our approach is reliable, but it is incomplete.
Our approach is incomplete, but it is reliable.

The first sentence, in true “yes, but” style, puts the focus on incomplete, whereas the second puts the focus on reliable.

先话题, 后重点. 句子的开头是天然的话题位置: 宣布这句话讲什么, 常常承接前文 (已知信息). 句子的结尾是天然的重音位置: 放重要的新信息. 两句话内容相同, 只是顺序不同: 「我们的方法可靠, 但不完备」重点在不完备 (典型的「是的, 但是」); 「我们的方法不完备, 但可靠」重点在可靠. 读者记住的是句尾.

Slide 11

Topic, then Stress

Notice the topic and the stress in the following sentences.

The mean error will guide the design of the evaluation’s next phase, so it is a critical thing to get a good estimate of.

The mean error will guide the design of the evaluation’s next phase, so it is critical that we get a good estimate of it.

The mean error will guide the design of the evaluation’s next phase, so it is critical that we estimate it accurately.

The mean error will guide the design of the evaluation’s next phase, so it is critical that we get an accurate estimate.

四个版本的话题都是 the mean error (平均误差会指导评估下一阶段的设计), 区别在句尾的重音位置放了什么: ① 介词 of; ② 代词 it; 这两个词都不携带新信息, 重音浪费了. ③ 副词 accurately, 好一些. ④ accurate estimate (准确的估计), 句子的核心新信息落在句尾, 最好. 同时 ① 的 a critical thing 也很口语.

这和本讲后面 Slide 27 的「句子可以用介词结尾」并不矛盾: 句末放介词在语法上没错, 只是在这里浪费了重音位置.

Slide 12

Subject, then Verb

As a rule, get to the subject quickly. Compare:

Although the problems are easy for Jeha (Jeha solves each of these problems within 15 seconds on a modern laptop), without the variable argument pruning rule they become substantially more difficult.

The variable argument pruning rule makes the problems easy for Jeha, which can solve each one within 15 seconds on a modern laptop. Without the rule, the problems become substantially more difficult.

In the first paragraph, the subject of the main clause comes at position 27. In the second paragraph, the subjects come at positions 2 and 4 in the respective sentences.

先主语, 后动词. 一般来说, 尽快让读者看到主语. 第一段的主句主语 they 在第 27 个词才出现 (我数过: 括号里的内容也算), 读者要把前面 26 个词都记在脑子里, 才知道这句话讲的是什么. 改写后拆成两句, 主语分别在第 2 个词 (variable …) 和第 4 个词 (the problems) 就出现了. 另外改写版把「剪枝规则」这个真正的主角放到主语位置 (Jeha 是一个定理证明器, 所以用 which 而不是 who).

Slide 13

Subject, then Verb

As a rule, get to the verb quickly. Compare:

Exploiting knowledge about specific models of a theory for improving automated proof search has received quite some attention in the past.

Many automated theorem provers enhance their proof search by exploiting knowledge about a theory’s specific models.

In the first sentence, the verb comes at position 14. In the second sentence, the verb comes at position 5.

We also shifted the focus from what researchers do (the metascience) to what programs do (the science).

也要尽快让读者看到动词. 第一句的主语是一个 13 个词的动名词短语, 动词 has received 在第 14 个词; 第二句动词 enhance 在第 5 个词 (两个位置我都数过). 改写还把焦点从「研究者在做什么」(元科学: 这个话题受到了很多关注) 转到「程序在做什么」(科学本身: 证明器利用模型知识增强证明搜索). 另外 quite some attention 是不太正式的说法 (见上一讲 quite).

Slide 14

Short before Long

Within sentences—and also paragraphs, sections, and chapters—try to present short segments before long segments. Compare:

These sections develop auxiliary theories about optimum techniques for sorting and searching and about permutations.

These sections develop auxiliary theories about permutations and about optimum techniques for sorting and searching.

先短后长. 在句子里 (也包括段落、节、章), 尽量先放短的部分, 再放长的. 第一句把长的「关于排序和查找最优技术的」放在前面, 中间夹着 sorting and searching and about permutations, 读者一时分不清 and 连接的是什么. 把短的 about permutations 放前面, 结构立刻清楚.

Slide 15–16

Left-Heavy vs. Right-Heavy

  • A left-heavy sentence has a long subject (on the left) and short objects, complements, or adverbials (on the right).
  • A right-heavy sentence has a short subject (on the left) and long objects, complements, or adverbials (on the right).

Left-heavy sentences are difficult to parse. Right-heavy sentences are easy to parse even if they are long.

Example of a left-heavy sentence:

That radix sorting is usually inferior to merge sorting, which happens because the technique of replacement selection gives merge sorting a definite advantage, and there is no apparent way to arrange radix sorts so that we can make use of internal sorts encompassing more than one memory load at a time, is one key consequence of the duality principle.

Example of a right-heavy sentence:

One important consequence of the duality principle is that radix sorting is usually inferior to merge sorting, which happens because the technique of replacement selection gives merge sorting a definite advantage, and there is no apparent way to arrange radix sorts so that we can make use of internal sorts encompassing more than one memory load at a time.

左重句主语很长、右边的宾语/补足语/状语很短; 右重句主语短、右边长. 左重句难以解析, 右重句即使很长也好读: 读者先拿到主语和动词, 知道了句子的框架, 后面的长内容只是往框架里填.

例子来自 Knuth (TAOCP 第 3 卷, 排序与对偶原理). 左重句的主语是一个 that 从句, 一直延续到 at a time, 读到最后的 is one key consequence 才知道整句在说什么. 右重句把「对偶原理的一个重要推论」放到前面作主语, 后面的长内容变成表语从句.

Slide 17

Left-Heavy vs. Right-Heavy

Below, a left-heavy sentence is split into two sentences:

The observation that Huffman’s algorithm might be implemented using a priority queue is of note to computer scientists because it represents a substantial opportunity to improve performance.

Huffman’s algorithm can be implemented using a priority queue. This greatly improves its performance.

把左重句拆成两句: 原句的主语是「the observation that … priority queue」(12 个词), 而且全是空话 (is of note to computer scientists, represents a substantial opportunity). 改写后两句共 14 个词, 主语短, 动作清楚.

Slide 18

Shape of Sentences

Vary the shape and length of your sentences.

Aim for 10 to 20 words per sentence on average.

Use short sentences for important points that need more stress.

Do not bury your main point in a 45-word sentence.

句子的形状: 句子的结构和长度要有变化; 平均每句 10 到 20 个词; 需要强调的重要观点用短句; 不要把主要观点埋在一个 45 个词的长句里.

人物与动作
Characters and Actions

Slide 19

Characters and Actions

To make your text more enjoyable to read and easier to follow, imagine you are telling a story.

  • Make the main characters the grammatical subjects of your sentences.
  • Make the main actions the grammatical verbs of your sentences.

人物与动作原则 (来自 Williams 的 Style): 把文本当作讲故事, 让它读起来更愉快、更好懂. 让主要人物做句子的语法主语; 让主要动作做句子的语法动词.

Slide 20–21

A Story

(Legende: Main character · Main action · Subject · Verb)

Once upon a time, as a walk through the woods was taking place on the part of Little Red Riding Hood, the Wolf’s jump out from behind a tree occurred, causing her fright.

vs.

Once upon a time, Little Red Riding Hood was walking through the woods, when the Wolf jumped out from behind a tree and frightened her.

一个故事的两个版本 (Slide 20 只有第一版, Slide 21 加上了标记和对照). 第一版: 主要人物小红帽和狼都不是主语, 主语是动作名词 walk 和 jump; 真正的动词是 was taking place、occurred 这种空洞的词; fright 也被名词化了. 读起来像公文: 「从前, 当一次穿过树林的行走正在由小红帽进行时, 狼从树后的一跳发生了, 造成了她的惊吓.」第二版: 人物做主语 (Little Red Riding Hood, the Wolf), 动作做动词 (was walking, jumped, frightened). 每个人物 (蓝色) 都带下划线 (主语), 每个动作 (橙色) 都带波浪线 (动词), 两套标记完全重合.

Slide 22

Another Story

(Legende: Main character · Main action · Subject · Verb)

The design of the new supercomputer was more of a struggle for the engineers than had been their expectation.

vs.

The engineers struggled more than they had expected when designing the new supercomputer.

另一个例子: 第一句的主语是 design, 动词是空洞的 was, 人物 engineers 躲在介词 for 后面, 动作 struggle 是名词. 改写后: 工程师 (人物) 做主语, 挣扎 (动作) 做动词: 「工程师在设计新超级计算机时比预期的更吃力.」第一句里 than had been their expectation 也是名词化的 (= than they had expected).

Slide 23

Inanimate Characters

(Legende: Main character · Main action · Subject · Verb)

Characters are often people. But inanimate things and abstractions can also be characters, particularly when they play some active role in a sequence of sentences—e.g.:

The CYK algorithm considers every substring of the word w to determine if it belongs to the language L. It was originally published by Itiroo Sakai in 1961.

无生命的人物: 人物通常是人, 但无生命的东西和抽象概念也可以当人物, 尤其是它们在一连串句子里扮演主动角色时. 例子里 CYK 算法 (计算语言学里的句法分析算法) 是人物: 第一句它是主语, 动作 considers (考察 w 的每个子串, 判断它是否属于语言 L) 是动词. 第二句仍然以它为主语 (It), 用的是被动语态 was published: 被动在这里是为了保持人物不变, 下一页会讲这是可以接受的被动.

CYK 以三位独立提出者 Cocke、Younger、Kasami 命名; Itiroo Sakai 1961 年的工作更早, 所以幻灯片说「最初由 Sakai 发表」. 详细的算法在本专业的形式语言 / 句法分析课程里会讲.

几条具体规则
Only · Relative Clauses · Superstitions · Passives · Nominalizations · Word Repetition

Slide 24

Placement of Only

The adverb only is often misplaced. The following sentences carry different meanings or nuances (Knuth et al.):

Only I hit him in the eye yesterday.
I only hit him in the eye yesterday.
I hit only him in the eye yesterday.
I hit him only in the eye yesterday.
I hit him in only the eye yesterday.
I hit him in the only eye yesterday.
I hit him in the eye only yesterday.
I hit him in the eye yesterday only.

If you write Here we only draw the first two points, you probably mean Here we draw only the first two points.

副词 only 常被放错位置. only 修饰紧跟在它后面的成分, 位置不同意思就不同:

  1. Only I …: 只有我 (没别人) 打了他.
  2. I only hit …: 我只是打了他 (没做更严重的事).
  3. I hit only him: 我只打了他 (没打别人).
  4. I hit him only in the eye: 我只打了他的眼睛 (没打别处).
  5. in only the eye: 意思同上, 语气更强调.
  6. in the only eye: 打在他唯一的那只眼睛上 (only 变成了形容词).
  7. only yesterday: 就在昨天 (刚刚).
  8. yesterday only: 只在昨天 (其他日子没有).

「Here we only draw the first two points」字面是「我们这里只是画出前两个点 (不做别的)」, 想说的其实是「只画前两个点」, 应写 draw only the first two points.

Slide 25–26

Restrictive vs. Nonrestrictive Relative Clauses

A restrictive relative clause narrows the range of the concept it modifies (i.e., the set of things the clause refers to)—e.g.:

The books that I bought yesterday were boring.

“Not all books are boring—only the ones that I bought yesterday.”

A nonrestrictive relative clause adds information about the set’s elements without making it smaller—e.g.:

The books, which I bought yesterday, were boring.

“The books we are talking about are boring. Incidentally, I bought them yesterday.”

The pronoun that is restrictive, whereas which is usually nonrestrictive.

The books that I bought yesterday were boring (restrictive) is right.
The books, which I bought yesterday, were boring (nonrestrictive) is right.
The books which I bought yesterday were boring (restrictive) is controversial.
The books, that I bought yesterday, were boring (nonrestrictive) is wrong.

Searching for controversial (or “wicked”) whichs is called “which hunting.”

限制性定语从句缩小所修饰概念的范围: 「我昨天买的那些书很无聊」, 意思是不是所有书都无聊, 只有昨天买的那些. 非限制性定语从句补充信息, 不缩小范围, 前后用逗号隔开: 「那些书 (顺便说一句, 是我昨天买的) 很无聊」.

规则: that 引导限制性从句, which 通常引导非限制性从句. 四种组合: that + 无逗号 (限制) 正确; 逗号 + which (非限制) 正确; 无逗号的 which 作限制性从句, 有争议 (英式英语和很多美式写作都接受, 但美式编辑规范常要求改成 that); 逗号 + that 一定错. 找出有争议的 which 并改成 that 叫「which hunting」(双关 witch hunting 猎巫).

对计算机文本很重要: 「Delete the files which are older than a week」有歧义的风险; 写成 that (限制: 只删旧文件) 或加逗号的 which (非限制: 删掉这些文件, 它们恰好都旧) 意思完全不同.

Slide 27

Against Superstitions

  • A preposition is a legitimate word to end a sentence with.
  • You need not try to not split infinitives.
  • And it is acceptable to start a sentence with and or but.

破除迷信: 三条流传很广但没有根据的「规则」, 每一条都用违反它的方式写成, 自己示范自己: ① 句子可以用介词结尾 (这句话就以 with 结尾); ② 不必刻意避免分裂不定式 (split infinitive, 在 to 和动词之间插入副词, 如 to boldly go; 这句话的 to not split 就是分裂不定式); ③ 句子可以用 and 或 but 开头 (这一条就以 And 开头).

Slide 28

Acceptable Passives

By default, write in the active voice instead of the passive. Thus, by default, write I lost the money rather than The money was lost by me.

But there are situations where passives can be useful:

  • You might not know who the doer is, you might not care, or it is self-evident—e.g., The chair was reelected with 54% of the vote.
  • The passive might allow you to follow the “topic, then stress” or the “characters and actions” principle—e.g., Gradient descent was discovered by Cauchy in 1847.
  • The passive might let you replace a long subject with a short one. Compare:
    Research demonstrating the soundness of our reasoning and the need for action supported this decision.
    This decision was supported by research demonstrating the soundness of our reasoning and the need for action.

可以接受的被动: 默认用主动语态 (I lost the money 而不是 The money was lost by me). 但这些情况下被动有用: ① 不知道、不关心执行者, 或执行者不言自明 (「主席以 54% 的票数连任」, 投票的当然是选民); ② 被动让你能遵守「先话题后重点」或「人物与动作」原则 (以梯度下降为话题, 把新信息 Cauchy in 1847 放在句尾; 梯度下降确实由 Cauchy 在 1847 年提出); ③ 被动能把长主语换成短主语 (第三组: 主动句的主语是 12 个词的 Research demonstrating …; 被动句主语只有 This decision, 是右重句).

Slide 29

Acceptable Nominalizations

By default, use characters and actions, and avoid nominalizations. Thus, by default, write The committee intends to audit the records rather than The intention of the committee is to audit the records.

But there are situations where nominalizations can be useful:

  • A nominalization in subject position can succinctly refer to previous material—e.g., Such objections can be addressed as follows.
  • Nominalization can replace clunky, tangled constructions with simple and direct phrasings—e.g., After recompilation, the program ran twice as fast.
  • Nominalizations can serve as characters in your narrative—e.g., Optimization is not the root of all evil.

可以接受的名词化: 默认用人物和动作, 避免名词化 (The committee intends … 而不是 The intention of the committee is …). 但这些情况下名词化有用: ① 名词化放在主语位置, 能简洁地指代前文 (Such objections 概括了前面说的几条反对意见); ② 名词化能代替笨重缠绕的结构 (After recompilation 比 After we had recompiled the program 简洁); ③ 名词化可以充当叙述里的人物: 「优化不是万恶之源」(反用 Knuth 的名言「过早优化是万恶之源」).

Slide 30–31

Word Repetition

Sometimes, you keep referring to the same concepts using the same words. For clarity, it is often better to accept the repetition than to employ synonyms.

By contrast, the accidental repetition of words to refer to different concepts is harmful. This will likely distract the reader and perhaps confuse them.

Example of the harmless type:

To prove the theorem, we must prove the base case and the induction step.

Example of the harmful type:

It proved difficult to prove the induction step.

Solution: Replace proved by turned out to be.

重复用词: 用同一个词反复指同一个概念, 为了清楚, 通常接受这种重复比用同义词好 (见 STE 03 的对称). 相反, 无意中用同一个词指不同概念是有害的, 会分散甚至迷惑读者. 无害的例子: 「要证明定理, 我们必须证明基础情形和归纳步骤」, 两个 prove 意思相同. 有害的例子: 「It proved difficult to prove the induction step」, 第一个 proved 是「结果是」, 第二个 prove 是「证明」. 解决办法: 把 proved 换成 turned out to be.

段落
Paragraphs

Slide 33–34

Topic of a Paragraph

Each paragraph should have one topic.

The first sentence of a paragraph often announces the topic. It is called a topic sentence. The rest of the paragraph expands on the topic.

The topic sentences alone should provide a reasonable outline of the section.

Length of a Paragraph

To ease reading, paragraphs should be kept short—perhaps no more than ten lines.

Split long paragraphs into shorter ones, such that each resulting paragraph has a single identifiable topic.

Conversely, try to avoid single-sentence paragraphs.

段落的主题: 每段只有一个主题. 段落的第一句常常宣布主题, 叫主题句; 段落其余部分展开这个主题. 只读各段的主题句, 就应该得到这一节的合理提纲 (这正是 STE 02 金字塔原理在段落层面的体现).

段落的长度: 为了好读, 段落要短, 也许不超过十行. 长段落拆成几个短段落, 每段有一个可识别的主题. 反过来, 尽量避免只有一句话的段落.

Slide 35

Flow of a Paragraph

Sentence k+1 in a paragraph should have a logical link to sentence k. They should fit like two pieces of a jigsaw puzzle. Compare:

There are several efficient sorting algorithms. Recursion is easy to reason about, so merge sort is particularly straightforward to implement.

There are several efficient sorting algorithms. Of these, merge sort is particularly straightforward to implement, since recursion is easy to reason about.

段落的流动: 第 k+1 句和第 k 句之间要有逻辑联系, 像拼图的两块那样咬合. 第一版: 第一句讲排序算法, 第二句突然以 Recursion 开头, 读者不知道它和前一句有什么关系. 第二版: Of these (在这些算法中) 把第二句接到第一句上, 话题从「几种算法」自然过渡到「归并排序」, 原因放到句尾. 这也是「先话题后重点」: 句首承接旧信息, 句尾放新信息.

Slide 36

Coherent Paragraphs

Coherence “is when all the sentences in a piece of writing add up to a larger whole, the way all the pieces in a puzzle add up to the picture on the box” (Williams and Bizup).

To achieve coherent paragraphs:

  • The topic of each sentence should be clear from the first eight words or so.
  • The sequence of topics in a paragraph should form a small set of related ideas.

连贯的段落: Williams 和 Bizup 说, 连贯就是「一篇文字里所有的句子合起来成为一个更大的整体, 就像拼图的所有碎片合起来成为盒子上的那幅画」. 做法: 每句的话题在前八个词左右就要清楚; 一个段落里各句话题的序列应该构成一小组相关的概念 (而不是每句换一个新话题).

Slide 37

Coherent Paragraphs

Compare (and pay attention to the subjects):

Consistent metaphors help users understand how to use a graphical application. A sense of consistency arises when a sequence of user actions comprises a narrow set of related ideas. But the context of what they are doing is lost due to inconsistent metaphors.

Users understand how to use a graphical application when they see consistent metaphors deployed in the user interface. They feel that an application is consistent when they execute a sequence of actions based on a narrow set of related ideas. But when the metaphors are inconsistent, users lose the context of what they are doing.

对比两段, 注意主语. 第一段三句的主语依次是 consistent metaphors、a sense of consistency、the context of what they are doing: 每句换一个话题, 第三句的 they 指谁也不清楚. 第二段三句的主语都是 users / they: 话题始终是用户, 段落围绕一个人物展开, 连贯得多. 这就是上一页「话题序列构成一小组相关概念」的意思.

Slide 39

Pitfalls

Chains of because and since
Avoid sentences of the form A because B because C or variations such as A since B since C—e.g., The transition rule is applicable because the side conditions are satisfied because x=y=0. Such sentences are hard to parse.
Consecutive sentences ending with the same word
These disturb the reader. Vary your sentence structure.
Danglers
When a sentence starts with a participle or some other modifying word, the reader naturally expects that this word modifies the sentence’s subject. They are let down when this is not the case—e.g., As an early GitHub adopter, we never had the chance to ask you about your email preferences. Here, it sounds as though the sender is an early GitHub adopter.
  • because / since 链: 避免「A because B because C」这种句子, 很难解析. 例句: 转移规则适用, 因为副条件满足, 因为 x = y = 0. 改写: Since x = y = 0, the side conditions are satisfied; hence, the transition rule is applicable. (按因果顺序写.)
  • 连续几句以同一个词结尾: 让读者不舒服 (句尾是重音位置, 重复格外显眼). 改变句子结构.
  • Dangler 悬垂修饰语: 句子以分词或其他修饰语开头时, 读者自然认为它修饰的是句子的主语; 不是的话读者就会失望. GitHub 的邮件写「作为 GitHub 的早期用户, 我们从没机会问过你的邮件偏好」, 听起来像是发件人 (we) 是早期用户. 应写 As you are an early GitHub adopter, we never …
Slide 40

Pitfalls

Empty verbs
These are verbs with little semantic content relevant to the main action of the sentence (e.g., is, seems, has, occurs). Consider reworking your sentence following the “characters and actions” principle.
False attraction
Sometimes, a word other than the subject “attracts” the verb, leading to a wrong verb form—e.g., Each of these points are . . . (write is), Lupin is one of the most famous thieves that is . . . (write are).
Garden paths
A garden-path sentence starts in such a way that a reader is likely to misparse it—e.g., We now prove A and B imply C. (Until the reader reaches imply C, the sentence sounds as though we would now prove A and B.) Reword to avoid.
  • 空洞动词: 与句子主要动作关系不大的动词 (is, seems, has, occurs). 按「人物与动作」原则改写 (见上面小红帽的 occurred).
  • 错误吸引: 主语以外的词「吸引」了动词, 导致动词形式错误. Each of these points are: 主语是 Each (单数), 不是 points, 应为 is. Lupin is one of the most famous thieves that is: 关系代词 that 指的是 thieves (复数), 不是 Lupin, 应为 are (「亚森·罗宾是那些最有名的盗贼之一」).
  • 花园小径句: 句子的开头让读者很可能解析错. We now prove A and B imply C: 读到 imply C 之前, 读者以为「我们现在证明 A 和 B」. 改写成 We now prove that A and B imply C (加 that 就消除了歧义).

「花园小径句」是心理语言学的术语 (garden-path sentence), 经典例子是 The horse raced past the barn fell. 计算语言学的句法分析课里也会遇到.

Slide 41

Pitfalls

Inelegant variation (also called, ironically, “elegant variation”)
Some authors clumsily use synonyms to avoid repetition. This can distract or even confuse readers. “If you call a car ‘the BMW’ in one place and ‘the sporty import’ in another, can your reader be certain that you’re referring to the same car? If you write about a person’s ‘candor’ in one sentence and ‘honesty’ in the next, is the reader to infer that you are distinguishing between two traits, or using different words to refer to the same one?” (Garner).
Sentence fragments
These are usually wrong in formal English. In particular, do not put a period between a main clause and a subordinate clause—e.g., The proposal was rejected. Because it did not address their main concerns.
  • 不雅的变化 (讽刺地也叫「雅致的变化」): 有些作者笨拙地用同义词来避免重复, 会分散甚至迷惑读者. Garner 说: 如果你在一处把一辆车叫「那辆宝马」, 另一处叫「那辆运动型进口车」, 读者能确定你说的是同一辆车吗? 如果你一句写某人的 candor (坦率), 下一句写 honesty (诚实), 读者该认为你在区分两种品质, 还是在用不同的词说同一种品质?
  • 句子碎片: 正式英语里通常是错的. 特别是不要在主句和从句之间加句号: 「The proposal was rejected. Because it did not address their main concerns.」第二部分是 because 引导的从句, 不能独立成句; 把句号改成空格: … was rejected because it ….
Slide 42

Pitfalls

Unclear antecedent of this
If you present five ideas in a row and then write This leads us to conclude . . ., what does This refer to?
Waffle
Some paragraphs have the form A but B but A. The cure is to rearrange the material.
  • this 的先行词不清: 连着讲了五个想法后写「This leads us to conclude …」, This 指哪个? 补上名词: This observation / This inconsistency leads us to conclude ….
  • Waffle 摇摆: 有些段落的结构是「A, 但 B, 但 A」, 来回摇摆. 解决办法是重新排列材料: 先把 A 的论点集中讲完, 再讲 B (或反过来), 结论放在最后.
