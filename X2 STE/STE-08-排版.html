字体与强调
General Principles · Font Families and Sizes · Bold and Underline · Italics · Colors

Slide 3

Font Families and Sizes

Named collections of related fonts (for bold, italics, etc.) form a font family—e.g., Times New Roman.

Use as few font families as possible while making important distinctions visible. Often a single family suffices for an entire document, including diagrams. You can make an exception for code, which is usually set in a monospaced font.

Combine font families only if they have similar line thickness and the lowercase letter x has a similar height in all of them.

Also ensure that your text is set in a suitably large font. This principle applies especially for slides and posters.

一组相关的字体 (常规、粗体、斜体等) 构成一个font family 字体家族, 例如 Times New Roman. 字体家族尽量少用, 但重要的区别要能看出来; 常常整份文档 (包括图) 只用一个家族就够了, 代码例外, 通常用等宽字体. 只有笔画粗细相近、小写 x 高度 (x-height) 相近的家族才能搭配. 字号要足够大, 幻灯片和海报尤其如此.

Slide 4

Bold and Underline

Emphasis can be achieved using bold, underline, italics, and colors.

Bold is often used for titles and headings. It can be used for emphasis on slides and posters.

Keywords in pseudocode are often set in bold—e.g.:

if x<y then
   x:=y
else
   y:=x

Underline—e.g., Journal of Cryptology—is ugly. It was used on typewriters when italics were not available. Prefer italics if possible.

强调可以用粗体、下划线、斜体和颜色. 粗体常用于标题; 在幻灯片和海报上也可以用来强调. 伪代码里的关键字 (if, then, else) 常用粗体. 下划线很丑: 它是打字机时代没有斜体时的替代品 (打字稿里下划线表示「排版时用斜体」). 能用斜体就用斜体.

Slide 5

Italics

Italics are elegant, but they slow down reading. Beyond emphasis, they have three main uses.

  • Italics highlight uncommon foreign phases, as in This thesis is a veritable tour de force. Do not italicize common Latin phrases such as e.g., et al., etc., and i.e. In case of doubt, check your dictionary.
  • Italics can be used to define a term, as in A palindrome is a list whose reverse is equal to itself.
  • Italics are used, in conjunction with title case, for the titles of “large works” such as theses, books, journals, and music albums when they occur in text—e.g., This paper was submitted to the Journal of Cryptology.

Within an italicized passage, you can emphasize a passage by unitalicizing it.

斜体优雅, 但会拖慢阅读. 除了强调, 斜体有三个主要用途: ① 标出不常见的外来短语 (这篇论文是一部真正的 tour de force 杰作); 常见的拉丁缩写 e.g.、et al.、etc.、i.e. 不用斜体, 拿不准时查词典. ② 定义术语: 第一次定义时把术语设为斜体 (回文是一个反转后等于自身的列表). ③ 「大作品」的标题 (毕业论文、书、期刊、音乐专辑) 用斜体加 title case; 「小作品」用引号 (见 STE 06). 在一段斜体里要强调某部分时, 把它改回正体.

原文笔误: 「uncommon foreign phases」应为 phrases (第 1 讲 Slide 22 也有同样的笔误).

Slide 6

Colors

Colors can be used for emphasis or to make distinctions. In programming, they are frequently used for syntax highlighting.

Some people are color-blind. They often cannot distinguish between red and green, among others. Thus, avoid relying on color alone.

If you use colors, check that your document is readable when printed in black and white. In addition, choose your colors with taste. Choose colors that offer a good contrast with the background and each other. Avoid pure primary colors, and prefer palettes recommended by experts.

颜色可以用于强调或区分; 编程中常用于语法高亮. 有些人是色盲, 常常分不清红和绿等颜色, 所以不要只靠颜色传达信息 (例如图里的两条线除了颜色不同, 还应该用不同的线型或标记). 用颜色时, 检查黑白打印后是否仍可读; 选色要有品味: 和背景、彼此之间要有足够的对比; 避免纯原色, 用专家推荐的调色板 (例如 ColorBrewer, 或对色盲友好的 Okabe–Ito 调色板).

标题与大小写
Headings · Capitalization

Slide 7

Headings

Chapter, section, and subsection headings should be viewed as meta-information. The text following a heading should make sense if the reader skips the heading. Compare:

2.2  First-Order Relational Logic
This logic, implemented in Kodkod, is used . . .

2.2  First-Order Relational Logic
Kodkod’s underlying formalism, first-order relational logic, is used . . .

章节标题应该看作元信息: 读者跳过标题, 后面的正文也应该读得通. 第一个版本的正文以 This logic 开头, 这个 This 指的是标题里的逻辑, 跳过标题就不知道指什么. 第二个版本在正文里重新说出 first-order relational logic (一阶关系逻辑, 模型查找器 Kodkod 的底层形式化).

Slide 8

Capitalization

Titles and headings can be capitalized following two approaches.

  • Title case: “Capitalize the first word, the first word after a colon, and all other words except articles, unstressed conjunctions, and unstressed prepositions” (van Leunen). Chicago has more elaborate rules.
  • Sentence case: “Capitalize the first word, the first word after a colon, and no other words except proper nouns and proper adjectives” (van Leunen).

Compare:

An Intermezzo: Jazzing Up the Data
An intermezzo: Jazzing up the data

For the title of your document, use title case. For chapter, section, and subsection headings, consistently use either case.

标题的大小写有两种做法. Title case: 第一个词、冒号后的第一个词和其他所有词大写, 冠词、不重读的连词和不重读的介词除外; Chicago Manual of Style 的规则更细. Sentence case: 只大写第一个词、冒号后的第一个词, 以及专有名词和专有形容词 (如 English). 对比例子: Jazzing Up 里的 Up 是短语动词的小品词 (重读), 所以在 title case 里大写, 不是介词. 文档的标题用 title case; 章节标题两种都可以, 但要统一.

Slide 9

Capitalization

Should you capitalize phrases such as the Event Dispatcher Thread and Fermat’s Little Theorem? And what about the Introduction?

In case of doubt, prefer lowercase. Thus, write the event dispatcher thread, Fermat’s little theorem, and the introduction.

Lowercase is arguably more pleasant to read and is easier to use consistently.

When introducing an acronym or initialism, there is no need to capitalize the unabbreviated expression unless it is a proper noun. Compare:

The framework fully supports Multiple-Document Interface (MDI).

The framework fully supports multiple-document interface (MDI).

the Event Dispatcher Thread、Fermat’s Little Theorem、the Introduction 要不要大写? 拿不准时用小写: the event dispatcher thread、Fermat’s little theorem (Fermat 是人名, 仍大写)、the introduction. 小写读起来更舒服, 也更容易保持一致. 引入首字母缩写时, 全称不必为了「对应字母」而大写, 除非它本身是专有名词: multiple-document interface (MDI).

德语母语者要注意: 德语名词都大写, 英语只有专有名词大写. 「Section 3」「Figure 2」「Theorem 4.1」这种后面跟编号的情况大写 (它们相当于名字), 但泛指时小写: in the next section.

数字、单位与日期
Numbers · Dates · Coinciding Punctuation

Slide 10

Numbers

Generally spell out numbers from zero to ten (or twelve), unless this would look locally inconsistent—e.g.:

Below, we compare the performance of all eight configurations on our server.
We used a third-party library for programming the user interface.

Since sentences should not start with a number (or symbol), also spell out numbers at the beginning of sentences, or reword to avoid the issue.

Numbers with at least five (or four) digits should use the thousands’ separator, either a traditional comma or, following international standards, a small space—e.g., 1,000,000 or 1 000 000.

For years and page numbers, the thousands’ separator is omitted—e.g., 1999.

数字: 0 到 10 (或 12) 一般用英文单词写 (all eight configurations, a third-party library), 除非这样在局部看起来不一致 (例如「3 to 15 nodes」不写成 three to 15). 句子不应以数字 (或符号) 开头, 所以句首的数字也写成单词, 或改写句子. 五位 (或四位) 以上的数字用千位分隔符: 传统的逗号 1,000,000, 或按国际标准用小空格 1 000 000. 年份和页码不用分隔符 (1999).

易错: 德语用句点作千位分隔符、逗号作小数点 (1.000.000,5), 英语正好相反.

Slide 11

Numbers

In English, the decimal separator is usually the period (.). Thus, two and a half is written 2.5.

There should be a space before units—e.g., 30 s. In compound expressions, there should be no hyphen—e.g., a 30 s timeout.

The percentage sign, the degree sign, and currency symbols have their own conventions—e.g., 3% (or 3 %), 90°, 21 °C, $55, €50.

For measurements, three significant digits usually suffice—e.g., 89.7%. Note that 90.0% is more precise than 90%.

Distinguish between percentages and percentage points. Percentage points denote the difference between percentages. A value of 50% falling by 10 percentage points becomes 40%. By contrast, a 10% fall would yield 45%.

英语的小数点是句点: 2.5. 数字和单位之间有空格: 30 s; 作定语时不加连字符: a 30 s timeout (写成单词时则加: a 30-second timeout). 百分号、度、货币符号各有惯例: 3% (或 3 %)、90° (角度, 不空格)、21 °C (温度, 空格)、$55、€50 (货币符号在前). 测量值一般三位有效数字就够了 (89.7%); 90.0% 比 90% 更精确, 因为它声明了小数点后一位是 0.

区分百分比和百分点: 百分点是两个百分比之差. 50% 下降 10 个百分点变成 40%; 下降 10% 则是 50% × 0.9 = 45%. 论文里比较准确率时最常犯这个错: 从 80% 提高到 88% 是「提高了 8 个百分点」或「相对提高了 10%」.

Slide 12

Dates

In American English, dates are written in month–day–year order, with commas around the year—e.g., May 1, 1990, (with a trailing comma).

If the day or the year is omitted, no commas are needed—e.g., May 1990.

In British English, dates are written in day–month–year order—e.g., 1 May 1990.

Further examples:

We met on Sunday, February 14, 2021.
We met again on Sunday, 21 February 2021.
The semester lasts from October 2026 to March 2027.

Do not abbreviate years. Thus, write 1999, not ’99 or 99.

美式日期按「月 日, 年」, 年份前后都加逗号 (句中的 May 1, 1990, 后面还有一个逗号); 省略日或年时不加逗号 (May 1990). 英式按「日 月 年」, 不用逗号. 三个例句: 美式、英式、只有月和年. 我核对过, 2021 年 2 月 14 日和 21 日都是星期日. 年份不要缩写: 写 1999, 不写 ’99 或 99. 注意 ’99 的撇号形状和右单引号一样 (表示省略), 自动排版常会误排成左引号 ‘99.

例句里一个美式、一个英式只是为了展示; 同一篇文本里要统一. 国际化的技术文档里, 为了避免 05/06 是 5 月 6 日还是 6 月 5 日的歧义, 常用 ISO 8601 格式 2021-02-14.

Slide 13

Coinciding Punctuation

When multiple punctuation signs would occur in sequence, special rules apply:

  • Logically, an abbreviation such as Co. should become Co.. at the end of a sentence, but in practice one period swallows the other.
  • American typesetting conventions dictate comma–quote (,”) and period–quote (.”), also against logic. In British English, punctuation that does not logically belong inside the quote is put outside—e.g., I said ‘OK’.
  • In “‘Who are you?’ she asked,” a tiny space is inserted between the opening double and single quotes for readability, and the question mark swallows the separating comma that would otherwise have followed it.

The conventions governing coinciding punctuation are outlandish. For American English, they are spelled out by van Leunen, pages 60–73.

几个标点连在一起时有特殊规则: ① 缩写 Co. 在句末, 按逻辑应写成 Co.., 实际上一个句点「吞掉」另一个, 只写一个. ② 美式排版规定逗号和句号放在右引号里面 (,” 和 .”), 即使不合逻辑; 英式只把逻辑上属于引文的标点放进引号: I said ‘OK’. ③ 「“‘Who are you?’ she asked,”」里, 双引号和单引号相邻处加一个很小的空格, 便于阅读; 问号吞掉了本应跟在引语后面的分隔逗号 (不写 ‘Who are you?’, she asked). 这些规则很古怪, 美式英语的完整规则见 van Leunen 书的第 60–73 页.

交叉引用与脚注
Cross-References · Footnotes · Item Lists

Slide 14

Cross-References

Cross-references are useful in all sorts of documents, but especially in dictionaries and reference manuals, which are usually read out of order.

Forward cross-references are pointers to a location later in the document—e.g.:

We introduce a new functional programming language, λxyz​ (Section 3).

Backward cross-references are pointers to a location earlier in the document—e.g.:

When we presented the language’s grammar in Section 3.2.1, we silently omitted the ternary operator.

Notice the tenses: present (or sometimes future in lengthy documents) for forward cross-references, past for backward cross-references.

Incidentally, you will rarely need to tell the reader to see a given section or page.

交叉引用在各种文档里都有用, 尤其是不按顺序阅读的词典和参考手册. 向前引用指向后面的位置: 「我们引入一种新的函数式语言 λxyz​ (第 3 节)」. 向后引用指向前面的位置: 「我们在 3.2.1 节介绍语言的语法时, 悄悄省略了三元运算符」. 注意时态: 向前引用用现在时 (长文档里有时用将来时), 向后引用用过去时 (对应 STE 04 里 Knuth 说的「按顺序写」). 另外, 很少需要叫读者「see」某节某页: 括号里写 (Section 3) 就够了.

Slide 15

Cross-References

If your text contains lots of cross-references, this might indicate a serious flaw in your document’s structure. For example, if you have many forward cross-references to Section 7 in Sections 3 to 6, maybe Section 7 should have been Section 3.

“Organization is the most elusive of all qualities in writing. We recognize badly organized material when other people produce it, but spotting our own failures is more difficult. Heavy cross-reference is one of the few hints you’ll ever have to guide you; heed its warning” (van Leunen).

交叉引用很多, 可能说明文档结构有严重缺陷: 如果第 3 到 6 节都频繁向前引用第 7 节, 也许第 7 节应该放在第 3 节. van Leunen 说: 「组织是写作中最难捉摸的品质. 别人写的材料组织得差, 我们一眼就能看出; 发现自己的失败却难得多. 大量交叉引用是你能得到的少数提示之一; 注意它的警告.」

Slide 16

Footnotes

Footnotes are suitable for minor comments that would disrupt the text’s flow and that are nevertheless too important to omit. They are useful for digressions or discussions targeted only at some of the audience.

In English, the footnote call (e.g., 1 or *) generally comes immediately after any punctuation sign, even against logic—e.g.:

Zipperposition’s success inspired me to develop Jeha, a new theorem prover.2 Unlike its predecessor, Jeha is implemented in Standard ML.

2 The name Jeha is appropriated from a fictional character in Middle Eastern folklore.

Most readers will look at the footnote. This will cause them to lose context. So there needs to be a good reason for the footnote to exist.

脚注适合放次要的说明: 放进正文会打断思路, 但又重要得不能删; 也适合离题话或只针对部分读者的讨论. 英语里脚注标记一般放在标点之后, 即使不合逻辑: prover.² (德语习惯是视情况放在标点前或后). 大多数读者会去看脚注, 这会让他们丢失上下文, 所以每个脚注都需要存在的充分理由.

Slide 17

Item Lists

Item lists can be used for listing parallel items or consecutive steps. As a side effect, they visually break up the text.

Items start with bullets (•) or numbers (1, 2, 3, etc.). Keep numbered lists for situations in which the items’ order matters or you need to refer to individual items later.

Make sure that your items are symmetric—e.g., they are all sentences, or all -ing verbs.

Punctuation must be consistent. Some authors punctuate each item, even if it is not a complete sentence. Others omit the punctuation. Compare:

  • Alnitak;
  • Alnilam; and
  • Mintaka.
  • Alnitak;
  • Alnilam;
  • Mintaka.
  • Alnitak
  • Alnilam
  • Mintaka

列表用于列举平行的项目或连续的步骤, 副作用是在视觉上把文本分块. 列表项以圆点或数字开头; 只有顺序重要、或之后要引用某一项时才用编号列表. 各项要对称: 要么都是句子, 要么都是 -ing 动词等. 标点要一致: 有人每项都加标点 (即使不是完整句子), 有人都不加. 例子 (猎户座腰带三星) 给了三种都可以的风格: 分号 + and + 句号; 只有分号和句号; 不加标点. 这门课的幻灯片用的是第二种.

版面: 间距、分段、换行、分页
Spacing · Paragraph Breaks · Line Breaks · Page Breaks · Justification

Slide 18

Spacing

Two fundamental rules of spacing:

  1. Put less space between closely related elements than between loosely related elements.
  2. Try to avoid hardcoding dimensions. And if you cannot avoid it, use multiples of logical units (e.g., baselines, ems).

The first principle applies especially to mathematical operators. Operators that bind more tightly, such as addition (+), need less space around them than operators that bind more loosely, such as equality (=).

间距的两条基本规则: ① 关系紧密的元素之间空隙小, 关系松散的之间空隙大; ② 尽量不要写死尺寸; 实在要写, 用逻辑单位的倍数 (行距、em). 第一条尤其适用于数学运算符: 结合得紧的运算符 (+) 两边空隙小, 结合得松的 (=) 两边空隙大. 读图: 蓝色的 2 + 2 = 4 里, 等号两边明显比加号两边宽; 橙色的四个空隙一样. LaTeX 的数学模式会自动这样排, 所以公式一律放进 $…$, 不要在正文里手打.

Slide 19–21

Paragraph Breaks

The beginning of paragraphs should be clearly marked, through either indentation or vertical space (but not both simultaneously).

Exceptionally, the first line of the first paragraph of a document, chapter, section, or subsection is usually not indented in English.

Examples of Paragraph Breaks

Correct:

The toilet paper dispensers in a certain building are designed to hold two rolls of tissues, and a person can use either roll.

There are two kinds of people who use the rest rooms in the building: . . .

Also correct:

The toilet paper dispensers in a certain building are designed to hold two rolls of tissues, and a person can use either roll.

There are two kinds of people who use the rest rooms in the building: . . .

Incorrect:

The toilet paper dispensers in a certain building are designed to hold two rolls of tissues, and a person can use either roll.

There are two kinds of people who use the rest rooms in the building: . . .

Also incorrect:

The toilet paper dispensers in a certain building are designed to hold two rolls of tissues, and a person can use either roll.

There are two kinds of people who use the rest rooms in the building: . . .

段落的开头必须清楚地标出: 要么首行缩进, 要么段间加空行, 但不能两者同时用. 例外: 英语里文档、章、节、小节的第一段首行通常不缩进 (因为前面就是标题, 不需要再标). 四个例子 (卡片里按幻灯片的版式排出): 正确的有两种, 第二段首行缩进且无空行, 或者有空行且不缩进; 错误的也有两种, 既不缩进也无空行 (看不出分段), 以及既缩进又有空行 (多余).

Slide 22

Line Breaks

Line breaking and hyphenation are largely automated by modern text processors, but they sometimes require special attention.

Titles and headings should be carefully broken. Compare:

Simulation of n-Tape Turing Machines Using 2n-Stack Pushdown
Automata

Simulation of n-Tape Turing Machines
Using 2n-Stack Pushdown Automata

Avoid breaking references to sections, theorems, etc. The break in Section
3 is distracting. Avoid it by using a nonbreaking space (also called “tie”).

Avoid breaking before the last item of a short inline enumeration, as in

The results of our empirical evaluation are summarized in Figures 3, 4, and
5.

换行和断词基本由排版软件自动处理, 但有时要特别注意. 标题要在意义单元处断行: 第一个版本把 Automata 一个词孤零零地留在第二行; 第二个版本在 Using 前断开, 两行各是一个意义完整的短语. 不要在「Section 3」「Theorem 2」中间断行, 用不断行空格 (LaTeX 里叫 tie, 写作 Section~3, 见 Slide 41 的 CDC~6600). 短的行内列举不要在最后一项前断行 (and 后面单独一个 5. 很难看).

Slide 23–24

Page Breaks

Page breaking is largely automated by modern text processors, but it sometimes requires special attention.

In particular, try to avoid these:

  • A widow arises when a page break occurs right before the last line of a paragraph.
  • An orphan arises when a page break occurs right after the first line of a paragraph.

Justification

Most prose documents are justified, meaning that the whitespace between words is stretched or shrunk to reach the right margin.

Ragged-right, whereby no stretching or shrinking takes place, can look better with short lines (e.g., in two-column format) or on slides.

分页也基本是自动的, 但要特别避免两种情况: widow 寡行: 段落的最后一行单独落到下一页顶部; orphan 孤行: 段落的第一行单独留在上一页底部. (LaTeX 用 \widowpenalty 和 \clubpenalty 控制.)

两端对齐: 大多数正文是两端对齐的, 词间空白被拉伸或压缩, 使每行都抵达右边界. ragged-right 左对齐 (右边参差) 不拉伸也不压缩, 在行很短时 (例如双栏排版) 或幻灯片上可能更好看, 因为短行强行两端对齐会出现很大的词间空隙.

Slide 25

Tables and Figures

Tables and figures are welcome distractions in most documents:

  • Tables are grids of textual or numeric data.
  • Figures are diagrams or images illustrating some concepts or visually presenting some data.

Tables and figures can be used

  • as displays—integrated in the text;
  • as floats—labeled with a (sentence-case) caption.

表格和图在大多数文档里是受欢迎的「分心之物」: 表格是文字或数字数据的网格; 图是说明概念或可视化数据的图表或图像. 两种用法: display 展示式, 嵌在正文里, 是句子流的一部分; float 浮动式, 带一个 (sentence case 的) 标题和编号, 由排版软件放到合适的位置 (例如页面顶部).

Slide 28

Tables and Figures

Displayed tables and figures should be connected to the text.

Floats should be referred to in the text, so that they do not get overlooked. Floats are especially useful to avoid ugly page breaks.

Stylish tables contain no double lines and, usually, no vertical lines.

Your text should clarify the semantics of figures with boxes and arrows. What is the meaning of a dotted arrow? Of a rectangular box?

展示式的表格和图要和正文衔接. 浮动体要在正文里被引用, 以免被忽略; 浮动体特别有助于避免难看的分页 (大表格放不下时整体移到下一页, 正文继续填满当前页). 好看的表格没有双线, 通常也没有竖线. 带方框和箭头的图, 正文要说明它们的语义: 虚线箭头是什么意思? 矩形框又代表什么?

数学写作

Slide 30

Mathematical Prose

Fundamentally, mathematical prose is prose. There may be lots of notations, displayed formulas, and other layouts that introduce additional complexity, but the basic rules of style, grammar, and punctuation apply.

Mathematical prose should be optimized for the reader. Carefully chosen notations can help capture key concepts and increase readability. Let the notations work for you.

But be careful: Compact notations that are useful for readers who are immersed in the topic may be overwhelming for readers who are new to the ideas.

数学文字本质上也是文字: 记号、展示公式等增加了复杂性, 但文体、语法、标点的基本规则照样适用. 数学写作要为读者优化: 精心选择的记号能抓住关键概念、提高可读性, 让记号为你服务. 但要小心: 对沉浸在这个领域的读者有用的紧凑记号, 可能让新读者不知所措.

Slide 31

Mathematical Symbols

Three fine points:

  • Denote multiplication by xy or x⋅y or even x×y, but not x∗y.
  • Denote minus by a minus sign (−), which is not the same as a hyphen (-), a short dash (–), or a long dash (—).
  • When using ellipses in the middle of formulas, repeat the operators on both sides, and align the ellipsis with the operators—e.g., 1+⋯+n.

三个细节: 乘法写 xy、x⋅y 或 x×y, 不写编程语言的 x∗y; 减号用真正的减号 (−, Unicode U+2212), 它和连字符、短破折号、长破折号都不同 (LaTeX 数学模式里的 - 会自动排成减号); 公式中间的省略号, 两边都重复运算符, 省略号和运算符居中对齐: 1+⋯+n (LaTeX 的 \cdots, 而不是位于基线的 \ldots).

Slide 32

Logical Symbols

Logical symbols belong in logical formulas and should generally be spelled out elsewhere.

Thus, write

for all, for each, or for every instead of ∀,
for some or there exists instead of ∃,
not instead of ¬,
and instead of ∧,
or instead of ∨,
etc.

When you use English for complex logical statements, make sure that your sentences are unambiguous, especially if they combine and and or.

逻辑符号只用在逻辑公式里, 其他地方一般用英语写出: for all / for each / for every 代替 ∀, for some / there exists 代替 ∃, not、and、or 代替 ¬、∧、∨. 用英语表达复杂的逻辑语句时要确保没有歧义, 尤其是 and 和 or 混用时: 「A and B or C」是 (A and B) or C 还是 A and (B or C)? 用 either … or、both … and 或拆成两句来消除歧义.

Slide 33

Mathematical Formulas

Display important or large formulas. Inline the rest.

Traditionally, displayed formulas are punctuated—e.g.:

This indeterminate is instead handled by the equation

∑n1​=0n2​​S1​gn​=∑n1​=0n2​​gn​.

Many computer scientists would omit the period for consistency with displayed code.

Your text should read smoothly even if you read all formulas aloud as “blah,” which is essentially what many readers will do, especially on a first reading. Introduce redundancy if it helps—e.g., the equation n=m2 instead of n=m2.

重要或大的公式单独成行展示, 其余放在行内. 按传统, 展示公式也要加标点: 公式是句子的一部分, 句子在公式处结束, 公式末尾就加句号 (例子里的句号). 很多计算机科学家为了和展示的代码一致而省略这个句号.

检验方法: 把所有公式都念成「blah」, 文本仍然应该读得通 (很多读者第一遍读时差不多就是这样做的). 必要时加一点冗余: 「the equation n = m²」比光秃秃的「n = m²」好, 念成「the equation blah」仍然知道这里是一个等式.

Slide 34

Mathematical Environments

Environments such as “Definition,” “Lemma,” “Theorem,” “Example,” and “Remark” emphasize important material and ease cross-referencing. Traditionally, the statement of lemmas and theorems is italicized—e.g.:

Theorem 3.1. If n is an even natural number, then n/2 is a natural number.

State definitions, lemmas, and theorems before you invoke them. In this way, circular arguments are ruled out by construction.

Consider numbering all environments using one shared counter—e.g., Theorem 3.1 is followed by Example 3.2, which is followed by Definition 3.3, etc. This helps readers follow cross-references.

Lemmas and theorems need proofs. These start with the label Proof., often typeset in italics, and end with the box symbol (□) or QED.

定义、引理、定理、例子、注释等「环境」能突出重要内容、方便交叉引用. 按传统, 引理和定理的陈述用斜体. 定义、引理、定理要先陈述再使用, 这样从结构上就排除了循环论证. 可以考虑所有环境共用一个计数器 (定理 3.1 之后是例子 3.2, 再之后是定义 3.3), 读者找交叉引用更方便 (分开计数的话, 定理 3.1 和定义 3.1 可能隔很远). 引理和定理需要证明: 以斜体的 Proof. 开头, 以方框 □ 或 QED 结束.

计算机科学: 算法与代码

Slide 36

Algorithms

Algorithms can be used to present the main idea of your code abstractly. Some advice:

  • Explain the algorithm informally first.
  • State its inputs, outputs, and specification clearly.
  • Present it in pseudocode in a modular fashion. The pseudocode can be displayed or put in a float. Use the imperative mood.
  • Explain how the algorithm works using an example.
  • State its properties (e.g., partial correctness, termination, asymptotic complexity) as rigorously as possible.

算法可以抽象地呈现代码的主要思想. 建议: 先非正式地解释算法; 清楚地说明输入、输出和规约; 用模块化的伪代码呈现 (展示式或浮动式均可), 用祈使语气; 用一个例子解释算法怎样运行; 尽可能严格地陈述它的性质 (部分正确性、终止性、渐近复杂度).

Slide 37

Example of an Algorithm

Input: A grammar G=(V,Σ,P,S) in Chomsky normal form
Output: A set W⊆V of all variables that do not generate the empty language

W:={A∈V∣A→a∈P, a∈Σ};
repeat
   Wold​:=W;
   W:=Wold​∪{A∣A→BC∈P, B∈Wold​, C∈Wold​};
until W=Wold​;
return W;

一个算法的例子: 输入是乔姆斯基范式的文法 G = (V, Σ, P, S), 输出是所有「不生成空语言」的变元 (即能推导出至少一个终结符串的变元). 先把有规则 A → a 的变元放进 W; 然后反复把「右边两个变元都已在 W 中」的规则 A → BC 的左部加进去, 直到 W 不再变化. 这个不动点迭代在 |V| 轮之内终止, 因为 W 只增不减. 在 CYK 分析之前常用它判断文法的语言是否为空 (S ∈ W 当且仅当 L(G) 非空). 我按算法的定义核对过, 它是正确的.

写法上: 关键字 repeat、until、return 用粗体, 输入输出单独写在前面, 正是 Slide 4 和上一页的建议.

Slide 38

Code Excerpts

Small code excerpts, of up to one line, can be inlined in your text. Larger code excerpts can be displayed or put in a float. Make sure to use a special (usually monospaced) font to make the code stand out, whether it is inlined, displayed, or floating.

Unless you are writing software documentation, do not show much code, if any. You will probably struggle to explain the code, and chances are that the reader will not understand it anyway. It is usually better to keep things abstract.

一行以内的小段代码可以放在行内; 更长的展示或放进浮动体. 不管哪种, 都用特殊字体 (通常是等宽字体) 让代码醒目. 除非写的是软件文档, 否则不要展示多少代码 (最好不展示): 你可能很难解释清楚, 读者多半也看不懂. 保持抽象 (用伪代码或数学描述) 通常更好.

Slide 40

Typesetting Software

Word processors such as Microsoft Word, LibreOffice Writer, and Google Docs can be used to write theses and papers. Presentation software such as Microsoft PowerPoint and Apple Keynote can be used to prepare slides and posters.

LaTeX is an alternative to all of the above that is preferred by many computer scientists, especially in academia. LaTeX documents are text files containing typesetting commands. The files are translated to PDF.

Two variants of LaTeX are particularly useful:

  • Overleaf is an online interface for LaTeX. It is convenient and simplifies using LaTeX because you do not need to install any packages.
  • LyX is a “what you see is what you mean” graphical interface on top of LaTeX. It encourages you to focus on the structure of your documents, as opposed to their appearance.

排版软件: Word、LibreOffice Writer、Google Docs 等文字处理软件可以写毕业论文和论文; PowerPoint、Keynote 可以做幻灯片和海报. LaTeX 是上面所有软件的替代, 很多计算机科学家 (尤其在学术界) 更喜欢它: LaTeX 文档是包含排版命令的文本文件, 编译成 PDF. 两个特别有用的变体: Overleaf 是 LaTeX 的在线界面, 不用自己安装任何宏包; LyX 是 LaTeX 之上的「所见即所指」(what you see is what you mean, 对比 WYSIWYG 所见即所得) 图形界面, 鼓励你关注文档的结构而不是外观.
