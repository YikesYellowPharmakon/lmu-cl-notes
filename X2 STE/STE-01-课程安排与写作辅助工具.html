Slide 5

Goals

The seminar will help you

  • learn the basics of scientific and technical writing for computer science, such as the proper use of citations, quotations, mathematical notations, and code excerpts;
  • acquire techniques for structuring the writing process, to help avoid the blank page syndrome;
  • learn the fundamentals of writing in plain formal English, including grammar, punctuation, and style;
  • learn how to develop various types of documents, such as papers, theses, slides, posters, and software documentation.

课程目标: 学习计算机科学的科技写作基础 (正确使用引用、引语、数学记号和代码片段); 掌握组织写作过程的技巧, 避免blank page syndrome 白纸综合征 (对着空白页写不出第一句); 学习用plain formal English 平实的正式英语写作的基础, 包括语法、标点和文体; 学会写各类文档: 论文、毕业论文、幻灯片、海报和软件文档.

看这一页本身的写法: 四个要点都以动词开头 (learn, acquire, learn, learn), 结构平行, 每项以分号结尾, 最后一项以句号结尾, 整个列表和引导句「The seminar will help you」连起来是一个完整的句子. 这种列表标点是这门课后面会专门讲的内容.

Slide 6

Why Writing Matters

Extracts from reviews of research paper submissions:

  • The paper is well-written, the design choices are well explained and well-motivated.
  • This is a solid and well written paper.
  • Overall, this is a very well-written paper; the structure is clear, the method is understandable.
  • This is a well-written report on an excellent bit of research.
  • Overall, the paper is well written and I really like the idea.
  • The paper is also very well written.
  • The article is well written and clear.
  • The article is well written and I find the contribution interesting.
  • The submission is well-structured and well-written.
  • Overall, the paper is well-written and easy to follow.

Do you think these papers were accepted or rejected?

为什么写作重要: 这里摘录了十条论文投稿的审稿意见, 全都在夸「写得好」. 问题: 你觉得这些论文被接收了还是被拒了?

这个问题的用意是: 审稿人夸写得好, 并不意味着论文会被接收; 好的写作是被认真对待的前提, 但决定接收与否的是研究的内容. 反过来, 写得差的论文, 审稿人往往读不懂贡献在哪里, 好的研究也会被埋没. (课上公布的答案以老师为准, 幻灯片上没有写.)

顺带一个语法点: 这些审稿人自己的写法就不统一: 有的写 well-written (带连字符), 有的写 well written. 规则是: 复合形容词放在名词前面作定语时加连字符 (a well-written paper), 放在系动词后面作表语时一般不加 (the paper is well written). 按这个规则, 第 1、9、10 条的 is well-written 和第 2 条的 a solid and well written paper 都不合规范. 第 1 条还把两个独立句子用逗号连在一起 (comma splice), 应改成分号.

Slide 7

Two Schools of Writing

The “academic” school favors

  • passive voice;
  • long sentences;
  • impenetrableness;
  • abstractness;
  • verbosity.

The plain English school favors

  • active voice;
  • short to medium sentences;
  • clarity;
  • concreteness;
  • conciseness.

To which school does this seminar belong?

两种写作流派. 「学术」流派 (引号表示讽刺) 偏好: 被动语态、长句、晦涩难懂 (impenetrableness)、抽象、啰嗦. plain English 平实英语流派偏好: 主动语态、中短句、清楚、具体、简洁. 两列一一对应, 每一行都是一对反义.

这门研讨课属于平实英语流派: 上一页的目标里写的就是「plain formal English」. 注意 plain 不等于 informal: 要平实, 同时保持正式.

Slide 13

Example of Peer Review

I found your introduction generally well written and well structured. Nevertheless, I have identified a few points that can probably be improved.

  1. The second and fifth paragraphs are very long. Faced with such long paragraphs, I tend to lose track of their main idea. Maybe split them?
  2. I have noticed a few typos: “occurence” (should be “occurrence”), “red” (should be “read”), and “principal” (should be “principle”). At least the first typo would have been caught by a spellchecker. You should really use one.
  3. Verb tenses seem to be used inconsistently. In the first paragraph, you write “The program takes 45 s to find a solution.” Yet in the fourth paragraph, you use the past tense and write “A solution was found almost immediately.”

These issues should be easy to address, and you will end up with a very pleasant introduction.

一份示范评语, 正好是三明治结构: 开头称赞 (引言总体写得好、结构好), 中间三条具体意见, 结尾再鼓励 (这些问题很容易改, 改完会是一篇很好的引言).

三条意见值得逐条看: ① 第二、五段太长, 读者会忘了主旨, 建议拆开 (用问句「Maybe split them?」, 语气委婉). ② 拼写错误: occurence → occurrence (两个 c 两个 r); red → read; principal → principle. 后两个是拼写检查器查不出的: red 和 principal 本身都是正确的单词, 只是用错了地方 (principal 是「主要的; 校长」, principle 是「原则」). 所以评语说「至少第一个」能被拼写检查器发现. ③ 时态不一致: 第一段用一般现在时 takes, 第四段却用过去时 was found. 描述程序的行为和实验结果时, 全文应统一时态.

写法上的细节: 每条意见都说清楚在哪里、是什么、为什么是问题、怎么改, 这样的评语对作者才有用.

Slide 22

Dictionary Software

Dictionary applications such as Dictionary on Windows and the similarly named application on macOS as well as websites such as Dictionary.com provide general dictionaries, thesauruses, and bilingual dictionaries.

  • General dictionaries are monolingual. They provide definitions and examples of English in English.
  • Thesauruses list synonyms and antonyms of English words.
  • Bilingual dictionaries can be used to map words or phases from your native language to valid English.

词典软件: Windows 上的 Dictionary、macOS 上同名的「词典」应用, 以及 Dictionary.com 这类网站, 提供三种词典. 普通词典是单语的, 用英语解释英语并给例句; thesaurus 同义词词典列出同义词和反义词; 双语词典把母语里的词或短语对应到正确的英语.

写作时最有用的是单语词典: 双语词典给出的英语词, 要再查单语词典确认它的用法和语域, 否则容易用错词.

原文笔误: 「map words or phases」应为 phrases (短语). phases 是「阶段」.

Slide 23

Spell-Checkers and Grammar Checkers

As a minimum, run a spellchecker on your document before you share it with other people (e.g., your supervisor) or upload the final version.

Examples of spellcheckers:

  • Linux and macOS include aspell.
  • Most text editors, including Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and Overleaf, include spellcheckers. Some of these also check the grammar and the style.
  • The Grammarly and LanguageTool add-ons provide more stylistic advice. Both tools are available for free as well as in Pro and Premium editions.

Used properly, Grammarly and similar tools can be very useful. But be careful:

  • Their suggestions are sometimes unsuitable.

Therefore, check every word critically.

拼写和语法检查: 至少要在把文档给别人 (例如导师) 看或上传最终版之前, 跑一遍拼写检查. 例子: Linux 和 macOS 有 aspell; 大多数编辑器 (Word、Google Docs、Overleaf) 自带拼写检查, 有的还检查语法和文体; Grammarly 和 LanguageTool 插件给出更多文体建议, 都有免费版和付费版. 用得好会很有用, 但要小心: 它们的建议有时并不合适, 所以每个词都要批判地检查.

幻灯片前后不一致: 标题写 Spell-Checkers (带连字符), 正文三次都写 spellchecker (连写). 两种拼法都存在, 但同一页里应统一; 写作课的幻灯片更该注意.

macOS 默认并不带 aspell, 要用 Homebrew 等另外安装 (brew install aspell); 很多 Linux 发行版也要另外安装. macOS 自带的是系统级的拼写检查 (在各应用的「编辑 → 拼写和语法」里).

Slide 24

Translation Tools

Tools such as DeepL Translate and Google Translate can be used to translate longer texts from your native language to English.

Used properly, translation tools can be very useful. But be careful:

  • The output might be unidiomatic or even invalid English.
  • The output might have a different meaning than the original.

Therefore, check every word critically.

翻译工具: DeepL、Google 翻译等可以把较长的母语文本翻译成英语. 用得好很有用, 但要小心: 输出可能不地道, 甚至语法错误; 意思也可能和原文不同. 所以每个词都要批判地检查.

Slide 25–26

Generative Artificial Intelligence Assistants

Chatbots such as ChatGPT and Copilot can be used to

  • check spelling and grammar;
  • ensure your text is written in the right register (e.g., formal English);
  • translate between languages;
  • expand a bullet list into continuous text;
  • create new text.

Used properly, chatbots can be very useful tools. But be careful:

  • Check every word critically.
  • Since you have no control over what chatbots do with your input, you should avoid providing them with sensitive data.
  • Using chatbots for generating new text is strongly discouraged. The produced text will usually be substandard (even if the English is smooth), and you might be plagiarizing without noticing, perhaps infringing copyright.

Also be aware of any organizational policies concerning chatbots (e.g., the plagiarism policy1 of the Institute for Informatics of the LMU).

1 https://www.medien.ifi.lmu.de/lehre/Plagiate-IfI.pdf

生成式 AI 助手: ChatGPT、Copilot 等聊天机器人可以用来检查拼写语法; 确保文本用的是正确的register 语域 (例如正式英语); 翻译; 把要点列表扩写成连贯的段落; 生成新文本.

A Sample Flawed Text

The authors of a Java book presented a small puzzling program and suggested that the readers compile and run it before reading the following.

It seems obvious that the program, should print 1cafebabe. After all, that is the sum of the hex numbers 10000000016 and cafebabe16. The program peruses long arithmetic which permits 16 hex digits, so arithemtic overflow is not an issue. Yet, if you ran the programm, you found that it prints cafebabe, with no leading 1 digit. This output represents the low-order 32 bits of the correct sum, but somehow the thirty third bit gets lost. It is as if the program was doing int arithmetic instead of long, or forget to add the first operator. What’s going on here?1

1 Adapted from Joshua Bloch and Neal Gafter, Java Puzzlers: Traps, Pitfalls, and Corner Cases, Addison-Wesley Professional, 2005.

一段有错误的样本文字, 改编自 Java Puzzlers 一书: 作者给出一个小谜题程序, 建议读者先编译运行再往下读. 段落的意思: 程序看起来显然应该输出 1cafebabe, 因为那是十六进制数 10000000016 和 cafebabe16 的和; 程序用 long 运算, 可以容纳 16 位十六进制数, 所以不会溢出. 可是运行后发现它输出 cafebabe, 前面没有 1. 这是正确和的低 32 位, 第 33 位不知怎么丢了, 就像程序做的是 int 运算而不是 long, 或者忘了加第一个操作数.

先自己找错再看下一页. 提示: 这段文字有 13 处问题, 包括标点、拼写、用词、语法和语域.

这个谜题的技术答案 (幻灯片没讲): 原书的程序是 Long.toHexString(0x100000000L + 0xcafebabe). 字面量 0xcafebabe 是 int, 最高位是 1, 所以它是一个负数 (−889 275 714). 转成 long 时做了符号扩展, 变成 0xffffffffcafebabe; 和 0x100000000 相加, 第 33 位的 1 与符号扩展出来的那串 f 相加后进位溢出, 结果正好是 cafebabe. 改法是写成 long 字面量 0xcafebabeL. (我用 Python 按 64 位补码重算过: 0x100000000 + 0xffffffffcafebabe 取低 64 位 = 0xcafebabe.)

Slide 30–31

A Sample Flawed Text (with the Flaws Underlined)

It seems obvious that the program, should print 1cafebabe. After all, that is the sum of the hex numbers 10000000016 and cafebabe16. The program peruses long arithmetic which permits 16 hex digits, so arithemtic overflow is not an issue. Yet, if you ran the programm, you found that it prints cafebabe, with no leading 1 digit. This output represents the low-order 32 bits of the correct sum, but somehow the thirty third bit gets lost. It is as if the program was doing int arithmetic instead of long, or forget to add the first operator. What’s going on here?

Flaws in the Text

  • Wrong comma: the program, should print;
  • informality: print vs. output or display;
  • bad taste: cafebabe;
  • informality: hex vs. hexadecimal;
  • wrong word: peruses vs. uses;
  • missing comma: long arithmetic which permits;
  • spelling mistake: arithemtic vs. arithmetic;
  • spelling mistake: programm vs. program;
  • missing hyphen: thirty third vs. thirty-third;
  • wrong verb form: as if the program was doing vs. … were doing;
  • wrong verb form: forget vs. forgetting;
  • wrong word: operator vs. operand;
  • informality: What’s going on here?

13 处问题, 逐条解释:

  1. 多余的逗号: 主语 the program 和谓语 should print 之间不能用逗号隔开.
  2. 不正式: print 在口语里常说, 正式写作用 output 或 display.
  3. 品味不佳: cafebabe (咖啡宝贝) 是程序员的玩笑, 正式文本里换掉 (最后的修改版换成了 deadc0de, 意思是「死代码」, 程序员圈里更常见, 也不那么轻佻).
  4. 不正式: hex 是 hexadecimal (十六进制) 的口语简称.
  5. 用错词: peruse 的意思是「细读、浏览」, 不是「使用」, 这里应为 uses. 这种误用叫 malapropism 词语误用: 用了一个发音相近但意思不对的词.
  6. 缺逗号: which 引导的是非限制性定语从句 (补充说明 long 运算的性质), 前面要加逗号: long arithmetic, which permits ….
  7. 拼写: arithemtic → arithmetic.
  8. 拼写: programm → program (programm 是德语的拼法 Programm, 德语母语者常犯).
  9. 缺连字符: 21 到 99 的复合数词要加连字符: thirty-third (第三十三).
  10. 动词形式错: as if 引导的是与事实相反的假设, 用虚拟语气: as if the program were doing.
  11. 动词形式错: or forget 和前面的 doing 平行, 应为 or forgetting (… were doing int arithmetic …, or forgetting to add …).
  12. 用错词: 被加的数是 operand 操作数, + 号才是 operator (运算符).
  13. 不正式: What’s going on here? 是口语的反问, 而且有缩写 What’s.

Final Revised Text

It seems obvious that the program should output 1deadc0de. After all, that is the sum of the hexadecimal numbers 10000000016 and deadc0de16. The program uses long arithmetic, which supports 16 hexadecimal digits, so arithmetic overflow is not an issue. Yet, if you ran the program, you would have found that it displays deadc0de, with no leading 1 digit. This output represents the low-order 32 bits of the correct sum, but somehow the thirty-third bit gets lost. It is as if the program were performing int arithmetic instead of long, or forgetting to add the first operand. What could be the cause of this issue?

最终修改版, 逐处对照前面的 13 条: 去掉多余逗号; print → output 和 displays; cafebabe → deadc0de (10000000016 + deadc0de16 = 1deadc0de16, 我核对过); hex → hexadecimal; peruses → uses; 补上 which 前的逗号, permits → supports; 拼写改正; thirty-third; were performing (虚拟语气 + 采纳了 Copilot 的 performing); forgetting; operand; 结尾问句采用 Copilot 的正式版本. 另外一处是新改的: you found → you would have found, 因为读者并没有真的运行过程序, 这是对假设情况的推测.

这一讲的结论: 工具能抓住拼写和一部分语法问题, 但用词、语域、品味和逻辑上的问题要靠人自己判断. 所有建议都要逐词批判地检查.
