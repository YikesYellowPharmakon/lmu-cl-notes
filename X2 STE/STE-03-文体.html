基本选择: 英语变体、语域、人称
Basic Decisions

Slide 3

Varieties of English

Usually you will write in either American or British English. Differences concern spelling, vocabulary, grammar, punctuation, and idioms.

The choice is often yours, but stick to one style.

If multiple authors are involved, the “editor” (or, rarely, an actual copy editor) should enforce a consistent style.

The two varieties may be mixed when, e.g., a Briton writes about the behaviour of the color attribute.

英语变体: 通常用美式英语或英式英语写作. 两者在拼写、词汇、语法、标点和习语上有差异. 选哪种往往由你决定, 但要坚持一种. 多人合写时, 由「编辑」(偶尔是真正的文字编辑) 来统一文体.

两种变体可以混用的情形: 例如一个英国人写 color 属性的 behaviour. behaviour 是他自己的英式拼写, 而 color 是代码里的名字 (CSS、HTML 等都用美式拼写), 代码标识符必须照原样写, 不能改成 colour.

  • 词汇: 薯片在美国叫 chips, 在英国叫 crisps (英国的 chips 是美国的 French fries).
  • 习语: 「柜子里的骷髅」(不可告人的秘密), 美式说 closet, 英式说 cupboard.
  • 主谓一致: 集体名词 committee 在美式里当单数 (agrees), 英式里常当作一群人, 用复数 (agree).
  • 连字符: 前缀 non- 美式通常连写 (nonempty), 英式加连字符 (non-empty). 复合形容词作定语时美式加连字符 (first-class citizen), 英式常不加.

  • 列举: 美式在 and 前加逗号 (1, 2, and 3), 叫 serial comma 或 Oxford comma; 英式通常不加.
  • 日期: 美式「月 日, 年」, 句中年份后面还要再加一个逗号 (January 1, 1990, was …), 所以表里带着结尾的逗号; 英式「日 月 年」, 不用逗号.

表头里的 Subject–verb 用的是 en dash (连接两个并列的词), 不是连字符.

Slide 5

Registers

Scientific and technical English belongs to the formal register.

“Formal” is situated between “informal” and “solemn”—more formal than chat messages, but less formal than legal documents.

Avoid colloquialisms as well as stilted language.

Register 语域: 科技英语属于正式语域. 「正式」位于「非正式」和「庄重」之间: 比聊天消息正式, 但不如法律文件那样正式. 既要避免口语说法 (colloquialisms), 也要避免生硬做作的语言 (stilted language).

Slide 7

Choice of Pronouns

Theses and papers might refer to the authors as we or I. Technical documentation tends to avoid these pronouns.

For single-author texts, I is usually preferable to the royal we, but use either only if you are relevant to the story.

There is also the inclusive we, which encompasses the reader—e.g.:

We can now prove the main theorem.

In technical documentation, there is often a choice between you and we. We is more formal. One is even more formal and usually best avoided. Compare:

You can also provide the string data as an array of chars.
We can also provide the string data as an array of chars.

One can also provide the string data as an array of chars.

Gender neutrality can be achieved using singular or plural they.

人称代词的选择: 毕业论文和论文可以用 we 或 I 指作者; 技术文档倾向于避免这两个代词. 单人作者的文本, 用 I 通常比 royal we (「朕」式的复数自称, 一个人却说「我们」) 好, 但不管用哪个, 都只在作者本身和内容相关时用.

还有inclusive we 包含式「我们」, 把读者也包括进来: 「我们现在可以证明主定理了」, 意思是「作者和读者一起」. 这种 we 在数学写作里很常见, 单人作者也可以用.

技术文档里常要在 you 和 we 之间选择: we 更正式; one 更正式, 但通常最好避免 (听起来生硬). 性别中立可以用单数或复数的 they 实现 (见 Slide 17).

原则: 一致、中立、清楚、简洁、自然
Principles

Slide 9

Consistency

Your text should read as if it had been written by a single author, even if it was not. It should be difficult to tell who wrote what.

To quote Halmos:

Consistency, by the way, is a major virtue and its opposite is a cardinal sin in exposition. Consistency is important in language, in notation, in references, in typography—it is important everywhere, and its absence can cause anything from mild irritation to severe misinformation.

一致性: 文本读起来应该像是一个作者写的, 即使实际上不是; 看不出哪部分是谁写的. Halmos 说: 「顺便说一句, 一致性是阐述中的一大美德, 不一致是大罪. 语言、记号、引用、排版都要一致, 处处都要一致; 缺乏一致, 后果可以从轻微的恼人到严重的误导.」

引文里的 by the way 恰好是上一页列为非正式的说法: 引文要照原样引用, 不能替作者改.

Slide 10

Neutrality

You have a point of view informed by your beliefs, knowledge, and life experience. In scientific and technical texts, it is usually best to adopt a neutral perspective. Even unassuming phrases such as surprisingly and of course can reveal your beliefs.

When writing technical documentation, a certain level of objectivity is expected.

  • It is acceptable to cover all the best aspects and to gloss over the weaknesses or deficiencies.
  • But it is not acceptable to deceive regarding failings or to exaggerate capabilities.

Purchasing decisions can be influenced by technical documentation, and misrepresentation can lead to court cases.

中立: 你的观点受你的信念、知识和经历影响. 科技文本最好采取中立的视角. 连 surprisingly (令人惊讶的是) 和 of course (当然) 这类不起眼的词也会暴露你的看法: 对谁惊讶? 对谁「当然」? 读者可能并不这样觉得.

写技术文档时, 要有一定的客观性: 着重介绍所有优点、对弱点和不足一笔带过, 是可以接受的; 但在缺陷上欺骗、夸大功能, 是不可接受的. 技术文档会影响购买决定, 不实陈述可能导致诉讼.

Slide 11

Clarity

Some texts seem impossible to understand, because they appear to have been written to show off the author’s erudition without any concern for making the material understandable, let alone interesting.

Even the most difficult concepts can be explained in clear, accessible English. To do this, you must begin by thinking about the reader.

  • What is their motivation for reading the document?
  • What specialist knowledge of the field are they assumed to have?

Beware of the curse of knowledge.

Clarity also requires that you use words precisely, with their literal meanings. Developing a graphical user interface in Java is not painful (unless your hands actually hurt) but perhaps tedious or unpleasant. And you certainly did not choose Java because you are allergic to C++.

清楚: 有些文本看起来根本读不懂, 好像写出来只是为了炫耀作者的学识, 完全不在乎内容能否被理解, 更别说有趣了. 再难的概念也能用清楚易懂的英语讲明白. 做法是先想读者: 他们为什么读这份文档? 默认他们具备哪些专业知识? 小心「知识的诅咒」(见 STE 02).

清楚还要求用词准确, 按字面意思用词. 用 Java 开发图形界面不是 painful (除非你的手真的疼), 而是 tedious (乏味) 或 unpleasant (不愉快). 你也肯定不是因为对 C++ allergic (过敏) 才选 Java 的. 这些夸张的比喻在口语里常见, 在正式写作里不准确.

Slide 12

Conciseness

Strunk urges you to omit needless words:1

Vigorous writing is concise. A sentence should contain no unnecessary words, a paragraph no unnecessary sentences, for the same reason that a drawing should have no unnecessary lines and a machine no unnecessary parts. This requires not that the writer make all his sentences short, or that he avoid all detail and treat his subjects only in outline, but that every word tell.

Conciseness also helps when working to a page limit.

1 In other words, you should refrain from using words and phrases that are not strictly necessary to convey your messages.

简洁: Strunk 劝你「删去不必要的词」: 「有力的文字是简洁的. 句子不应有多余的词, 段落不应有多余的句子, 正如画不应有多余的线条, 机器不应有多余的零件. 这并不要求作者把每句话都写短, 也不要求省掉一切细节、只写梗概, 而是要求每个词都有分量.」简洁在有页数限制时也有帮助.

脚注是个玩笑: 它用 20 个词把正文里四个词 (omit needless words) 的意思又啰嗦地说了一遍, 正好示范了什么叫不简洁. 引文里的 make、avoid、tell 用的是虚拟语气 (requires that the writer make …, 不是 makes).

Slide 14

Naturalness

Van Leunen writes:

Scholarly writing is formal, precise, and allusive. It has to be. It does not have to be wooden, finicking, and cabalistic. The idea of this book is to help you achieve the first set of characteristics without sinking into the second.

To find out more, buy her book.

自然: van Leunen 写道: 「学术写作是正式的、精确的、有所指涉的 (allusive, 会引用和暗示别的文献). 它必须如此. 但它不必呆板、过分拘泥细节、晦涩难懂 (cabalistic, 像秘传的学问). 这本书的用意是帮你做到前三个特点, 而不陷入后三个.」

引文本身就是对称结构的示范: 两组各三个形容词, 一组是要的, 一组是不要的.

Strategies

Slide 16

Fresh Language

Clichés generally do not belong in formal writing—e.g.:

last but not least
at the end of the day
take with a grain of salt
in any way, shape, or form
does not exist in a vacuum
42

Some clichés are tempting in titles—e.g.:

To Be or Not to Be Parallel
A Tale of Two Rust Compilers
Database Performance: The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly

Resist the temptation. Also be sparing with jokes.

清新的语言: 正式写作一般不用陈词滥调 (cliché), 例如 last but not least (最后但同样重要), at the end of the day (说到底), take with a grain of salt (对……持保留态度), in any way, shape, or form (无论以任何方式), does not exist in a vacuum (并非孤立存在), 以及 42 (《银河系漫游指南》里「生命、宇宙以及一切的终极答案」, 程序员的老梗).

标题里的陈词滥调尤其诱人: 套用《哈姆雷特》的 To be or not to be, 狄更斯的《双城记》A Tale of Two Cities, 电影《黄金三镖客》The Good, the Bad and the Ugly. 要抵制这种诱惑. 玩笑也要少开.

对照 STE 02 Slide 13: 那里把「k + 1 Heads Are Better than k」这类幽默标题当作例子展示, 这里则建议抵制. 两页并不矛盾: 前者是展示标题的类型, 后者是作者的建议.

Slide 17

Gender Neutrality

Because English nouns have no grammatical gender, the language lends itself well to gender-neutral writing.

He or she, s/he, and (s)he can be replaced by singular they. Often, plural they is also an option. Compare:

The user can enter their preferences in the text area.
Users can enter their preferences in the text area.

A few expressions require some care. Write person-year instead of man-year, chair instead of chairman, etc. Words such as middleman and straw man must be accepted as is or avoided.

Sexism can be subtle. Generally avoid Max Mustermann unless you also have Erika Musterfrau. Do not refer to women by first name only.

性别中立: 英语名词没有语法性别, 很适合性别中立的写法. he or she、s/he、(s)he 都可以换成单数 they; 也常可以改成复数, 用复数 they. 一些说法要注意: 写 person-year (人年) 而不是 man-year, chair (主席) 而不是 chairman 等; middleman (中间人)、straw man (稻草人论证) 这类词没有好的替代, 要么照用, 要么绕开.

性别歧视可能很隐蔽: 一般不要只用 Max Mustermann (德国的「张三」, 男性示例名), 除非也有对应的女性名字; 也不要只用名字称呼女性 (而对男性用姓), 例如「Knuth and Barbara」.

原文小错: 德国通用的女性示例名是 Erika Mustermann (德国身份证和护照样本上用的就是这个名字), 不是「Erika Musterfrau」. 「Musterfrau」偶尔有人戏称, 但不是标准用法.

Slide 18

Examples

Scientific and technical writing tends to be abstract. To make your text accessible, use concrete language, examples, and diagrams. Tell and show.

Your examples should look realistic. If you write a Java tutorial, do not call your classes Foo, Bar, or MyClass. In fact, never use foo or bar for anything. Be creative. If you have to show some data as an example, use real data.

Running examples—examples that are recalled multiple times through a document—can be useful. You can choose them early on, once you have sketched your table of contents. The examples ought to be interesting.

例子: 科技写作容易抽象. 要让文本好懂, 就用具体的语言、例子和图. 「Tell and show」: 既要说, 也要演示 (套用写作课的格言「Show, don’t tell」, 这里改成两者都要).

例子要像真的: 写 Java 教程时不要把类叫 Foo、Bar 或 MyClass; 事实上任何东西都不要叫 foo 或 bar. 要有创意; 要展示数据时, 用真实的数据.

Running example 贯穿全文的例子: 在文档里反复回到同一个例子, 很有用. 列好目录后就可以尽早选定. 这些例子应该有趣.

Slide 19

Analogies

An analogy is a comparison between a concept being introduced and a familiar concept.

Analogies can aid understanding but can be tricky to get right—e.g.:

Finding the right element in an array is like finding a needle in a haystack.

类比: 把要引入的概念和一个熟悉的概念作比较. 类比有助于理解, 但很难用对. 例子「在数组里找到正确的元素就像大海捞针」不好: 大海捞针的意思是几乎不可能, 而在数组里查找只是线性时间, 并不难. 而且这个比喻本身也是陈词滥调.

Slide 20

Examples of Analogies

We may compare a man in the process of computing a real number to a machine which is only capable of a finite number of conditions q1​,q2​,…,qR​ which will be called “m-configurations”. The machine is supplied with a “tape” (the analogue of paper) running through it, and divided into sections (called “squares”) each capable of bearing a “symbol”.1

SETHEO contains a powerful preprocessing module. Its incorporation is motivated by analogy to the action of a human reasoner, say a mathematician, who, before applying a specific proof method, first tries to analyse and reduce the respective problem according to more global aspects.2

1 Alan M. Turing, “On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem,” Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society s2-42(1), pp. 230–265, 1937.
2 R. Letz, J. Schumann, S. Bayerl, and W. Bibel, “SETHEO: A High-Performance Theorem Prover,” Journal of Automated Reasoning 8, pp. 183–212, 1992.

两个好的类比. 图灵在提出图灵机的论文里, 把「一个正在计算实数的人」比作一台只有有限个状态 q1​,…,qR​ (他称为 m-configurations) 的机器; 机器里穿过一条「纸带」(相当于纸), 纸带分成「方格」, 每格可以写一个「符号」. 这个类比把抽象的机器和人用纸笔计算的熟悉情景对应起来, 而且每个对应都准确.

SETHEO (一个定理证明器) 的论文说: 它有一个强大的预处理模块, 加入它是受人类推理者的启发: 比如数学家在使用具体证明方法之前, 会先从更全局的角度分析和化简问题.

两段引文里的 analogue、analyse 是英式拼写, 引文照原样保留. 图灵原文把句号放在引号外 (“m-configurations”.), 也是英式习惯.

Slide 21

Opening Hooks

In your introduction, try to engage the reader, for example using a pun, a quotation, a question, an anecdote, or a provocative statement.

Consider the first two paragraphs of Knuth’s celebrated toilet-paper paper:

The toilet paper dispensers in a certain building are designed to hold two rolls of tissues, and a person can use either roll.

There are two kinds of people who use the rest rooms in the building: big-choosers and little-choosers. A big-chooser always takes a piece of toilet paper from the roll that is currently larger; a little-chooser always does the opposite. However, when the two rolls are the same size, or when only one roll is nonempty, everybody chooses the nearest nonempty roll. When both rolls are empty, everybody has a problem.1

1 Donald E. Knuth, “The Toilet Paper Problem,” The American Mathematical Monthly 91(8), pp. 465–470, 1984.

开篇的钩子: 在引言里设法吸引读者, 例如用双关、引语、问题、轶事或挑衅性的论断. 看 Knuth 著名的「厕纸论文」的前两段: 某栋楼的厕纸架能放两卷纸, 人可以用任意一卷. 用厕所的人分两种: big-chooser 总是从当前较大的那卷取纸, little-chooser 正好相反. 但两卷一样大, 或只有一卷不空时, 每个人都用最近的那卷. 两卷都空时, 每个人都有麻烦了.

这个开头好在: 场景人人熟悉、略带幽默, 但同时精确地定义了整个数学模型 (两类人、各自的规则、边界情况), 读完这两段读者已经知道论文要研究什么问题了. 最后一句是个干巴巴的笑话, 也正好是模型的终止条件.

Slide 22

Positivity

Positive sentences tend to read better than their negative counterparts.

When processing the negative sentence Do not press the red button, the brain

  1. conjures the positive sentence Press the red button;
  2. negates it.

Yet the unnegated sentence Press the red button hangs in the air. Better say, Only press the green button.

Sometimes, we can use the contrapositive to eliminate negations. Compare:

If you do not register the listener, mouseClicked() will not be called.

If you want mouseClicked() to be called, you must register the listener.

肯定句通常比对应的否定句好读. 大脑处理否定句「不要按红色按钮」时, 先想出肯定句「按红色按钮」, 再把它否定掉; 可那个没被否定的「按红色按钮」还悬在脑子里. 更好的说法是「只按绿色按钮」.

有时可以用contrapositive 逆否命题消掉否定: 「如果不注册监听器, mouseClicked() 就不会被调用」改成「如果想让 mouseClicked() 被调用, 就必须注册监听器」. 严格说, 「¬注册 → ¬调用」的逆否命题是「调用 → 注册」(mouseClicked() 被调用, 说明监听器已注册); 幻灯片的改写把它换成了「想要调用就必须注册」的说法, 逻辑上表达的是同一个必要条件, 只是更适合读者 (从读者的目标出发).

Slide 23

Symmetry

Strunk advocates expressing coordinate ideas in similar form:

This principle, that of parallel construction, requires that expressions of similar content and function should be outwardly similar. The likeness of form enables the reader to recognize more readily the likeness of content and function.

Examples:

The variable name stores the input file’s name, whereas the variable path stores the file’s absolute path.

According to Christopher Hitchens, what can be asserted without evidence can also be dismissed without evidence.

It would be a mistake to vary—e.g.:

What can be asserted without evidence can also be dismissed without proof.

对称: Strunk 主张并列的想法用相似的形式表达: 「这个原则, 即平行结构原则, 要求内容和功能相似的表达在外形上也相似. 形式上的相似让读者更容易看出内容和功能上的相似.」

例子: 「变量 name 存输入文件的名字, 而变量 path 存文件的绝对路径」, 前后两半句结构完全相同. Hitchens 的名言「无证据就能断言的, 也可以无证据地驳回」, 两个 without evidence 前后呼应. 如果为了「避免重复」把第二个换成 without proof, 就是错的: 读者会以为 evidence 和 proof 是两个不同的东西, 对称也被破坏了. 中学作文里教的「同义词替换」在技术写作里常常有害: 同一个概念要始终用同一个词.

Slide 24–25

Further Example of Symmetry

For beginning programmers, tiny, artificial, and unrealistically expensive examples can be instructive models for how more realistic systems should—and should not—be structured. For more experienced programmers, such examples can serve as touchstones that provide handy, memorable points of reference—and that can guide them toward more maintainable, more performant solutions.

对称的另一个例子, 先看原文 (Slide 24), 再看拆解 (Slide 25). 意思: 对编程初学者来说, 小巧的、人造的、代价高得不现实的例子, 可以成为有启发的范本, 说明更真实的系统应该 (以及不应该) 怎样构造. 对有经验的程序员来说, 这样的例子可以当作试金石: 提供方便好记的参照点, 并引导他们走向更易维护、性能更好的解决方案.

读图: 大括号标出了每一处并列: 三个形容词 tiny, artificial, and unrealistically expensive; should 和 should not (用一对破折号插入); 两个 that 从句 (that provide … —and that can guide …), 每个从句内部又各有一对并列 (handy, memorable; more maintainable, more performant). 两句话本身也对称: For beginning programmers … / For more experienced programmers …. 正因为结构层层平行, 这么长的句子读起来仍然清楚.

Slide 27

Pitfalls

Academese
This is jargon-heavy, pompous, needlessly complex academic writing—e.g., The individual member of the social community often receives his information via visual, symbolic channels. (In other words, People read.)1
Asymmetric coordination
Beware of unbalanced coordinations—e.g., both in C# and Java. Write either both in C# and in Java or in both C# and Java.
Awkwardness
Various clumsy constructions should be avoided, such as sentences starting with a symbol or number, accidental word repetitions, and consecutive symbols—e.g., If x=y, y=x. (Change to If x=y, then y=x.)

1 Richard P. Feynman, Ralph Leighton (contributor), and Edward Hutchings (editor), “Surely You’re Joking, Mr. Feynman!”: Adventures of a Curious Character, W. W. Norton, 1985.

陷阱按英文字母顺序排列.

  • Academese 学术腔: 满是行话、自命不凡、无谓复杂的学术写作. 例子来自费曼的回忆录: 「社会共同体的个体成员经常通过视觉的、符号的渠道接收信息」, 换句话说就是「人们会阅读」.
  • 不对称的并列: both … and … 后面两部分的形式要相同. both in C# and Java 里, both 后面是「in C#」, and 后面却只有「Java」. 改成 both in C# and in Java (两边都是介词短语), 或 in both C# and Java (两边都是名词).
  • 笨拙的结构: 句子以符号或数字开头 (要么改写, 要么把数字写成单词); 无意的重复用词; 两个符号或公式紧挨着: 「If x = y, y = x.」两个公式只隔一个逗号, 读者分不清; 加上 then 就清楚了.
Slide 28

Pitfalls

Bad taste
Inside jokes or otherwise bad jokes, obscure references, slang, and clichés are all in dubious taste. Make jokes only if you are sure that they will land.
Breach of conventions
Nonstandard terminology and notations can slow readers down or even confuse them.
Broken logic
Some phrases are incompatible, as in e.g., JFrame, JDialog, etc. The leading e.g. (“for example”) suggests an incomplete list, but etc. (“and the others”) completes the list.
  • 品味不佳: 圈内笑话或其他糟糕的笑话、冷僻的典故、俚语和陈词滥调, 都有失品味. 只有确定能逗笑读者时才开玩笑.
  • 违反惯例: 不标准的术语和记号会拖慢读者, 甚至造成混淆. 例如别人都叫 hash table, 你偏叫 key store.
  • 逻辑矛盾: 有些说法互相冲突, 例如「e.g., JFrame, JDialog, etc.」: 开头的 e.g. (例如) 表示列举不完整, 结尾的 etc. (以及其他) 又把列举补全了, 两者重复. 二选一: 「e.g., JFrame and JDialog」或「JFrame, JDialog, etc.」.
Slide 29

Pitfalls

Cultural references
References to movies, celebrities, soccer players, etc., will not be understood by some readers. They also date the text.
Exaggeration
It is impossible to overstate the challenges that we still face in our struggle to revise the computer science curriculum at our university. Is it really impossible?
Excessive backward referencing
Phrases such as as mentioned above should not be overused. Often, they are an indication that the text is poorly structured.
  • 文化典故: 提到电影、名人、足球运动员等, 有些读者看不懂, 而且会让文本很快过时.
  • 夸张: 「我们在修订本校计算机课程的斗争中仍面临的挑战, 怎么说都不为过」. 真的「不可能夸大」吗? 再说 struggle (斗争) 也言过其实.
  • 过多的向后引用: as mentioned above (如上所述) 这类说法不要滥用, 它们往往说明文本结构不好: 如果读者需要被反复提醒前面的内容, 也许内容就该放在这里.
Slide 30

Pitfalls

Excessive forward referencing
Unless the text is a reference manual, when reading Section 2.1, the reader will not suddenly jump to Section 4.7. So why provide a forward reference?
Excessive metadiscourse
Metadiscourse refers to the text itself, not to the actual topic—e.g., In this section, we review the literature related to object-oriented databases or As my work does not rely on these functions, I do not present the algorithms here. However, they can be found in Kim [40]. Metadiscourse is occasionally useful but generally best avoided.
Foreign influence
If English is not your native language, it is easy to make certain mistakes. False friends are an issue—e.g., n! is called factorial in English, not faculty (cf. Fakultät in German). Even comma rules differ across languages.
  • 过多的向前引用: 除非是参考手册, 读者读到 2.1 节时不会突然跳到 4.7 节, 那何必写「见 4.7 节」?
  • 过多的元话语: Metadiscourse 元话语谈的是文本本身, 而不是主题. 例如「本节我们回顾与面向对象数据库相关的文献」, 或「由于我的工作不依赖这些函数, 这里不介绍其算法; 它们可以在 Kim [40] 中找到」. 元话语偶尔有用, 但一般最好避免: 第一句可以直接开始回顾文献, 节标题已经说明了这一节是什么.
  • 母语的影响: 母语不是英语时容易犯某些错误. 一个问题是false friends 假朋友 (形似而义不同的词): n! 在英语里叫 factorial (阶乘), 不叫 faculty, 尽管德语叫 Fakultät (英语的 faculty 是「能力; 院系; 教职员」). 连逗号规则在不同语言里也不一样 (例如德语在 dass 从句前必须加逗号, 英语在 that 从句前不加).
Slide 31

Pitfalls

Negativity
Negativity can often be recast into positivity. Compare:
Thus, an equation such as (1) would not be allowed.
Thus, an equation such as (1) would be forbidden.
Nominalization (also called “zombie nouns”)
Nominalizations are “abstract nouns formed from verbs or adjectives through the addition of a suffix such as -ance, -ence, -ity, -ness, -ion, -ment, or -ism” (Sword). Compare:
The introduction of the well-formedness condition leads to a proliferation of occurrences of wf in the program.
If we need objects to be well formed, calls to wf will be required throughout the program.
  • 否定: 否定的说法常可以改成肯定的: would not be allowed → would be forbidden.
  • Nominalization 名词化 (也叫「僵尸名词」): 按 Sword 的定义, 是由动词或形容词加上 -ance、-ence、-ity、-ness、-ion、-ment、-ism 等后缀构成的抽象名词. 对比: 「良构条件的引入导致了 wf 出现次数在程序中的激增」(introduction, condition, proliferation, occurrences 四个名词化, 句子没有真正的施动者) 和「如果我们需要对象是良构的, 程序里到处都要调用 wf」(动作用动词表达, 主语是 we). 「僵尸」的意思是: 这些名词把动词里的生命吸干了.

注意形容词的连字符: 名词化的 well-formedness 加连字符; 作表语的 to be well formed 不加 (对比 STE 01 Slide 6 讲过的规则).

Slide 32

Pitfalls

Passivity
The passive voice is widespread in scientific writing, especially in German but also in English. Yet often the active voice is clearer and therefore preferable. Compare:
Several experiments using the new procedure were conducted.
We conducted several experiments using the new procedure.
Self-reference
The reader will feel more at home if you avoid references to yourself. In technical documentation, avoid screenshots that reveal your user name. “An author in his book must be like God in the universe, present everywhere and visible nowhere” (Gustave Flaubert).
  • 被动: 被动语态在科学写作里很普遍, 在德语里尤其如此, 英语里也有. 但主动语态往往更清楚, 因而更可取. 「使用新方法的几次实验被进行了」不如「我们用新方法做了几次实验」: 谁做的一目了然, 句子也更短.
  • 自我指涉: 少提到你自己, 读者会更自在. 技术文档里避免用暴露自己用户名的截图. 福楼拜说: 「作者在书中应当像上帝在宇宙中一样, 无处不在, 又无处可见.」

Flaubert 的引文里用 his 指 an author, 是 19 世纪的写法, 引文照原样保留; 自己写时按 Slide 17 用 their.

Slide 33

Pitfalls

Tics
“Everyone has pet phrases and constructions that grow wearisome with reuse. I once read through a thirty-page paper in which the expression ‘the case that’ occurred more than a hundred times (I counted)” (van Leunen). So let your friends and colleagues read your texts. They will catch your pets.
Understatement (also called “hyperqualification”)
This is the opposite of exaggeration—e.g., Partial orders are relatively simple mathematical objects. (Omit relatively.)
  • Tic 口头禅: van Leunen 说: 「每个人都有自己偏爱的说法和结构, 用多了就让人厌烦. 我读过一篇 30 页的论文, the case that 出现了一百多次 (我数过).」所以请朋友和同事读你的文本, 他们会抓住你的口头禅 (pets, 呼应前面的 pet phrases, 双关).
  • Understatement 轻描淡写 (也叫 hyperqualification 过度限定): 夸张的反面. 「偏序是相对简单的数学对象」: 相对于什么? 删掉 relatively. 类似的词还有 quite, rather, somewhat, fairly.

注意引文里的引号嵌套: 外层用双引号, 里面的 the case that 用单引号, 这是美式的嵌套方式 (英式正好相反).
