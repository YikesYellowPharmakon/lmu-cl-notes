文献检索与阅读
Literature Search · Literature Reading Techniques · The Target Audience

Slide 3

Literature Search

Work on a thesis usually starts with a literature search. Your supervisor might give you papers to read, or you might find papers by following citations or using online databases such as Google Scholar.

Papers can play different roles:

  • Some papers form the basis of your work.
  • Some papers help justify your work by demonstrating that there is a research gap.
  • Some papers are not directly related to your work but provide some broad context for it.

写论文通常从文献检索开始: 导师可能直接给你几篇文章, 你也可以顺着文献里的引用往下找, 或用 Google Scholar 这类数据库. 文献在你的论文里扮演不同角色: 有的是你工作的基础; 有的证明存在研究空白 (research gap), 从而说明你的工作有必要; 有的和你的工作不直接相关, 只提供宽泛的背景.

Slide 4

Literature Reading Techniques

Not all papers need to be read in full. To work efficiently, first read a paper’s abstract, introduction, and conclusion, then decide whether to continue reading—or skimming—its core sections.

Approach papers you read with specific questions. Look for the answers while reading.

Read critically. Question the statements made in papers, even if these are published at prestigious venues.

不是每篇都要读完. 高效的读法: 先读摘要、引言和结论, 再决定要不要读 (或浏览) 核心部分. 带着具体的问题去读, 边读边找答案. 读的时候要批判性地读: 即使是发表在顶级会议或期刊上的文章, 对其中的论断也要提出质疑.

Slide 5

The Target Audience

Now you start writing your thesis. You should write primarily for fellow students—not for your supervisors.

Thus, you need to explain the fundamental concepts behind your work that are not part of the curriculum. For example, if your project was to develop a compiler, you should briefly define and explain the different compiler phases.

学位论文主要写给同学看, 而不是写给导师. 所以课程里没讲过、但你的工作要用到的基本概念, 都要解释: 比如做的是编译器, 就要简短地定义并解释编译的各个阶段 (词法分析、语法分析、代码生成等).

题目、摘要、致谢
The Title · The Abstract · The Acknowledgment

Slide 6

The Title

As a rule, the title of a thesis should be informative rather than catchy. It should capture your contribution precisely without being too long—e.g.:

Benchmarking of Quantum Generative AI Models
Strategies for Exchanging Clauses in SAT Solvers
Haskell for Natural Language Processing: A Comprehensive Survey

学位论文的题目要信息量大, 而不是吸引眼球; 要准确概括你的贡献, 又不能太长. 三个例子: 量子生成式 AI 模型的基准测试; SAT 求解器之间交换子句的策略; 用 Haskell 做自然语言处理: 一份全面的综述. 题目用 title case (见 STE 08).

Slide 7

The Abstract

The abstract of a thesis might be up to half a page long. It usually follows the model “context, main idea, contributions.”

The abstract should be readable by your target audience. Thus, avoid abbreviations, and beware of jargon that fellow students might not know.

The abstract should not contain citations. If necessary, refer to sources by naming the authors.

学位论文的摘要最多半页左右, 一般按「背景、核心思路、贡献」三步写. 摘要要让目标读者 (同学) 看得懂: 避免缩写, 小心同学可能不认识的行话. 摘要里不放引用 (摘要常常脱离正文单独出现, [12] 这种编号没有意义); 必要时直接写作者的名字.

Slide 8

The Acknowledgment

Most theses include an acknowledgment at the beginning. This is where you thank your supervisors followed by anyone else who helped you with your research project or your thesis (e.g., friends or fellow students who read your text).

大多数学位论文开头有一段致谢: 先感谢导师, 再感谢其他帮助过你的研究或论文写作的人, 例如读过初稿的朋友和同学.

目录结构
The Table of Contents · Placing the Related Work · Example

Slide 9

The Table of Contents

In computer science, tables of contents tend to have a hybrid structure:

  • The outer chapters or sections have conventional titles—e.g., Introduction, Preliminaries, Discussion, Conclusion.
  • The core chapters or sections have custom titles—e.g., Design of the Garbage Collector.

A bachelor’s thesis usually consists of sections. A master’s thesis may consist of chapters or sections. Chapters may contain multiple sections, and sections may contain multiple subsections.

计算机科学论文的目录通常是混合结构: 外层的章节用常规标题 (引言、预备知识、讨论、结论), 核心章节用自定义的、描述内容的标题 (例如「垃圾回收器的设计」). 学士论文一般只分节 (section); 硕士论文可以分章 (chapter) 或分节. 章下面可以有节, 节下面可以有小节 (subsection).

Slide 10

The Table of Contents

Should the related work be discussed up front or near the end?

Two approaches:

1. Introduction
2. Related Work
3. Preliminaries
   Core chapters or sections
n. Conclusion
1. Introduction
2. Preliminaries
   Core chapters or sections
n − 1. Related Work
n. Conclusion

With the second approach, some related work might need to be discussed earlier, if the thesis builds on it.

Slide 11

The Table of Contents

Why place the related work early?

  • The related work is coherently located at the thesis’s beginning. instead of being distributed over the thesis.
  • You can refer to the related work from the thesis’s core.
  • You can use the related work to identify a research gap or open question motivating your work.

Why place the related work late?

  • You can focus first on your own work.
  • You can discuss the related work in more depth with respect to your work.

「相关工作 (related work)」放在前面还是后面, 两种做法都常见. 放前面 (引言之后): 相关工作集中在论文开头, 不会散落各处; 核心章节可以引用它; 可以用它引出研究空白或待解决的问题, 作为你工作的动机. 放后面 (结论之前): 读者先看到你自己的工作; 读者已经了解你的工作, 可以更深入地比较. 放后面时, 如果论文直接建立在某些前人工作上, 这部分仍要在前面 (预备知识里) 先讲.

标点笔误 (第 11 张): 「… at the thesis’s beginning. instead of being distributed over the thesis.」中间多了一个句号, 把一句话断成了两半; 应为「… at the thesis’s beginning instead of being distributed over the thesis.」.

Slide 12

Example of a Table of Contents

    Abstract
    Acknowledgment
    Table of Contents
1. Introduction
2. Background
3. Design of the Website
4. Visualization of the Automata
5. Empirical Evaluation
6. Discussion
7. Related Work
8. Conclusion
    References

一个目录的例子 (相关工作放在后面的做法): 摘要、致谢、目录不编号; 1 引言, 2 背景, 3–5 是核心部分 (网站的设计、自动机的可视化、实证评估), 6 讨论, 7 相关工作, 8 结论; 最后是不编号的参考文献.

各章怎么写
The Introduction · Preliminaries or Background · Core Chapters · Related Work · Conclusion · Examples

Slide 13

The Introduction

In the introduction, clearly state what the purpose or use case of your work is. Be clear about what was done by others and what your contribution is.

Ways to achieve this:

  • State of the art vs. contribution: You start by explaining the state of the art in your field. Do not hesitate to use the term state of the art to make this completely unambiguous for the reader. You then summarize your contribution. Do not hesitate to write contribution to make this clear.
  • Problem vs. solution: You start by presenting a problem. You then present your solution to the problem. Do not hesitate to use the terms problem and solution to make things clear.

You can combine either approach with an opening hook.

引言要讲清楚你的工作的目的或用途, 并分清哪些是别人做的、哪些是你的贡献. 两种写法: ① 现状 vs. 贡献: 先讲本领域的研究现状, 再概括自己的贡献; 大胆直接写「the state of the art」「our contribution」这样的词, 读者一看就明白. ② 问题 vs. 解法: 先提出问题, 再给出解法; 同样直接写「problem」「solution」. 两种写法都可以配一个开头的「钩子 (opening hook)」, 用一个有趣的例子或问题吸引读者 (见 STE 03).

Slide 14

The Introduction

When explaining the motivation for your work, focus on the science, not the metascience. Do not write

In recent years, an increasing number of computer scientists have turned their attention to the problem of explainable artificial intelligence.

or

Explainable artificial intelligence is an important research topic.

Instead, explain why the research is important, and let the facts speak for themselves.

If you have research questions, ask them in the introduction, then answer them in the thesis’s body.

讲动机时要讲科学本身, 不要讲「元科学」(关于这个研究领域的社会现象): 「近年来越来越多的计算机科学家关注可解释人工智能」「可解释人工智能是一个重要的研究课题」都是空话. 应该说明这项研究为什么重要 (例如: 医生在采纳模型的诊断前需要知道它的依据), 让事实自己说话. 如果有研究问题, 在引言里提出, 在正文里回答.

Slide 15

The Preliminaries or Background

Early in your thesis, you will likely have a chapter or section called Preliminaries or Background. Some authors make this distinction:

  • Preliminaries are basic definitions, notations, and other conventions that are used in the rest of the thesis.
  • Background is related work on which your thesis builds.

You should define or explain any specialized terms that might be unfamiliar to your target audience. When in doubt, briefly summarize the important concepts. For simple definitions and theorems, one or two sentences are often sufficient. For more complex concepts, you might want to write a full paragraph.

论文前面通常有一章或一节叫「预备知识 (Preliminaries)」或「背景 (Background)」. 有的作者区分两者: 预备知识是后文用到的基本定义、记号和约定; 背景是你的论文所依据的前人工作. 目标读者可能不熟悉的专门术语都要定义或解释; 拿不准就简短概括一下. 简单的定义和定理一两句话就够, 复杂的概念可以写一整段.

Slide 16

The Core Chapters or Sections

The thesis’s core is where you can explain what you did at length.

You can hardly ever give too many examples. Diagrams can also be useful. But you should also describe at least the core parts of your work (e.g., the main data structures and algorithms) precisely and in full generality. Focus on the nonobvious parts of your work, and try to explain them in simple words.

Focus on the final result, and avoid anecdotes about how you arrived at it. Exceptionally, in case studies, your journey might be relevant.

Some theses have a separate chapter or section titled Discussion, where results are analyzed. You can also inline discussions throughout the thesis.

核心章节详细讲你做了什么. 例子几乎嫌不多, 图也很有用; 但至少核心部分 (主要的数据结构和算法) 要讲得精确、完全一般化, 不能只靠例子. 重点放在不显然的部分, 尽量用简单的话解释. 只写最终结果, 不要讲你是怎么一步步走到这里的故事; 例外是案例研究 (case study), 那时过程本身可能有意义. 有的论文单设一章「讨论」分析结果, 也可以把讨论分散在各章里.

Slide 17

The Related Work

The related work chapter or section is where you explain how your work fits into the scientific edifice.

When describing other people’s work, get into the habit of naming them. Make them the subjects of sentences—e.g., Navarro [27] designed the first monomorphization algorithm. This especially helps if Navarro is among your reviewers.

You may feel that there is no or little related work to write about. But if you broaden the definition of “related,” you will find something. Your supervisors should be able to help.

相关工作一章说明你的工作在「科学大厦」里处在什么位置. 介绍别人的工作时要写出他们的名字, 并让他们做句子的主语 (Navarro [27] 设计了第一个单态化算法, 而不是「第一个单态化算法见 [27]」), 如果 Navarro 恰好是你的审稿人, 效果更好. 觉得没什么相关工作可写时, 把「相关」的定义放宽一点, 总能找到; 导师也能帮忙.

Slide 18

The Conclusion

The conclusion typically consists of a summary optionally followed by a brief discussion of future work.

The conclusion can be short. One or two pages might suffice. To quote Paulson: “A page of ramblings about everything you might do in the next five years, or would do if you had your time again, belongs on your blog.”

The conclusion should make sense to readers who have read only the abstract and the introduction.

结论一般是一段总结, 可以再简短地谈谈未来的工作. 结论可以短, 一两页就够. Paulson 说: 「一整页漫谈你未来五年可能做的事, 或者重来一次你会怎么做, 那应该写在你的博客上.」结论要让只读过摘要和引言的读者也看得懂 (很多读者会跳着读).

Paulson 指剑桥大学的 Lawrence C. Paulson, 交互式定理证明器 Isabelle 的主要作者; 这一讲引的两句话 (另一句在第 29 张) 出自他在个人博客上写的论文写作建议.

Slide 19

Examples of Theses

Let us look at a bachelor’s and a master’s thesis:

  • Matthias Kettl, “Fault Localization for Formal Verification: An Implementation and Evaluation of Algorithms Based on Error Invariants and UNSAT-Cores,” bachelor’s thesis, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München, 2020.
  • Wanja Sajko, “Exploring the Potential of the Filtering Variational Quantum Eigensolver,” master’s thesis, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München, 2023.

课上要分析的两篇 LMU 的学位论文: 一篇学士论文 (形式化验证中的错误定位: 基于错误不变式和 UNSAT 核的算法实现与评估, 2020), 一篇硕士论文 (探索过滤式变分量子本征求解器的潜力, 2023). 注意学位论文的标题按「小作品」用引号, 下一页的博士论文是「大作品」, 用斜体.

博士论文
PhD Theses · Cumulative vs. Noncumulative · Acknowledgment of Coauthorship

Slide 21

The Table of Contents

Two types of PhD thesis:

  • A cumulative thesis consists of a long (perhaps 30-page) introduction followed by verbatim copies of (typically three or more) published papers.
  • A noncumulative thesis, or monograph, has a more traditional structure. A typical table of contents:

    1. Introduction
    2. Background
       Core chapters
    n. Conclusion

    The introduction might be 5 pages long, the background 10 pages long, and the conclusion 3 pages long. The (typically three or more) core chapters each describe a single contribution and would usually be at least 15 pages long. They may be based on published papers.

博士论文有两种. 累积式 (cumulative, 德国常说 kumulative Dissertation): 一篇较长的引言 (大约 30 页), 后面原样附上三篇或更多已发表的论文. 非累积式, 也叫专著 (monograph): 传统结构, 引言约 5 页、背景约 10 页、结论约 3 页; 中间三章或更多核心章节, 每章讲一项贡献, 每章通常至少 15 页, 可以由已发表的论文改写而成.

Slide 22

Acknowledgment of Coauthorship

Often PhD research is carried out in collaboration with coauthors. The precise contributions of each author must be acknowledged in the introduction, the individual chapters, or an appendix.

博士阶段的研究常常和合作者一起完成. 每位作者具体做了什么, 必须写清楚, 可以放在引言、各章开头或附录里. 这在累积式论文里尤其重要, 因为附上的论文署名往往不止一人.

Slide 23

Examples of Theses

Let us look at a cumulative and a noncumulative PhD thesis:

  • Maximilian Bernhard, Deep Learning Methods for Image Recognition in Remote Sensing, PhD thesis, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München, 2024.
  • Sophia Elisabeth Grundner-Culemann, Formal Verification of Revocation Approaches in Identity-Based Cryptography, PhD thesis, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München, 2024.

课上要分析的两篇 LMU 博士论文 (2024): 遥感图像识别的深度学习方法; 基于身份的密码学中撤销方案的形式化验证. 幻灯片只说一篇是累积式、一篇是非累积式, 没有标明哪篇是哪种, 课上对照分析.

研究论文
Why Write a Paper? · Types of Papers · Venues · The Table of Contents

Slide 25

Why Write a Paper?

A scientific contribution exists if and only if it is documented.

You should write a paper if you have discovered something you are excited about and want to share it with other researchers.

Papers are more widely read than theses, and they are more concise. To become well known in your research area, you must get papers published.

When you apply for an academic position, your bibliometrics (including your number of citations and your so-called h-index) matter a lot. The motto “publish or perish” accurately describes the academic world.

科学贡献当且仅当被记录下来才算存在. 发现了让你兴奋、想和其他研究者分享的东西, 就应该写成论文. 论文比学位论文读的人多, 也更精炼; 想在自己的领域出名, 必须发表论文. 申请学术职位时, 文献计量指标 (被引次数、h 指数等) 非常重要. 「不发表就灭亡 (publish or perish)」准确地描述了学术界.

h 指数: 一位研究者有 h 篇论文各自至少被引用 h 次, 他的 h 指数就是 h (取最大的那个 h). 例如有 10 篇论文的被引次数从高到低是 25, 18, 12, 9, 7, 6, 3, 2, 1, 0, 前 6 篇都至少被引 6 次, 第 7 篇只有 3 次, 所以 h = 6.

Slide 26

Types of Papers

In addition to regular research papers, the following types of papers also exist, among others:

  • survey papers;
  • system papers;
  • application papers;
  • evaluation papers;
  • position papers.

除了常规的研究论文, 还有: 综述 (总结一个领域的现状)、系统论文 (介绍一个工具或系统的设计与实现)、应用论文 (把已有方法用到新问题上)、评估论文 (系统地比较已有方法)、立场论文 (position paper, 提出观点或主张, 引发讨论).

Slide 27

Venues

Research papers are published at different venues.

  • Workshops are informal, noncompetitive events. Minor or preliminary research is presented there.
  • Conferences are the main publication venue in most of computer science.
  • Journals are the venue of choice for more mature or detailed results.

Workshop and conference papers are published in proceedings.

Slide 28

Venues

The main advantage of workshops and conferences is that you can advertise your research by giving a talk at an in-person event.

Most workshop and conference papers must fit within about 8 to 16 pages. Additional details should be included in an appendix or in a technical report.

Journal articles can be much longer, which makes them suitable for publishing more detailed accounts. Many journal articles are extended versions of conference papers.

Choose the venue that best suits the paper’s content, or make the paper match the venue.

论文发表的场合 (venue) 有三种. Workshop: 非正式、竞争不激烈, 发表小型或初步的研究. 会议: 计算机科学大部分领域的主要发表场合 (这和其他学科不同, 那里期刊才是主流). 期刊: 适合更成熟、更详细的成果. Workshop 和会议论文收录在论文集 (proceedings) 里. 会议的主要好处是可以在现场做报告宣传自己的研究. Workshop 和会议论文一般限 8–16 页, 多出的细节放附录或技术报告 (第 10 讲); 期刊论文可以长得多, 很多是会议论文的扩展版. 根据内容选合适的场合, 或者让论文去适应场合.

Slide 29

The Table of Contents

Structurally, papers resemble theses, but the acknowledgments are often at the end, after the conclusion but before the reference list.

For a workshop or conference paper, it is especially important to compress the background, by referring to other sources.

As Paulson remarked: “Finally you get to describe your actual work. Are you on page 2 or page 7? If the latter, how did you manage to use half of your page allowance before saying anything original?”

论文的结构和学位论文相似, 但致谢通常放在最后: 结论之后、参考文献之前. Workshop 和会议论文尤其要压缩背景部分, 能引用别处的就引用. Paulson 说: 「终于讲到你真正的工作了. 你现在在第 2 页还是第 7 页? 如果是后者, 你是怎么在说出任何原创内容之前就用掉一半篇幅的?」

同行评审与排版
Peer Reviewing · Rebuttal and Shepherding · Typesetting

Slide 30

Peer Reviewing

Your paper is reviewed by at least two or three researchers who do not have a conflict of interest.

Peer reviewing is generally anonymous, meaning that the reviewers’ identities are not revealed to you.

Additionally, with double-blind reviewing, your identity is not revealed to the reviewers. If you need to cite yourself, do so as if you were a third party. Thus, I might write According to Blanchette (2012), ….

Reviews usually include a summary, an assessment, a recommendation (e.g., “weak accept”), and a confidence level (e.g., “expert”). Often, reviewers also ask you questions and make suggestions to improve your paper.

论文至少由两三位没有利益冲突的研究者审稿. 审稿一般是匿名的: 你不知道审稿人是谁. 双盲审稿时, 审稿人也不知道你是谁; 这时引用自己要像引用第三方一样写 (According to Blanchette (2012), …, 而不是第 9 讲教的「I argued elsewhere」). 审稿意见通常包括: 论文概要、评价、推荐意见 (如 weak accept 弱接收) 和自信程度 (如 expert); 还常常提问题、提改进建议.

Slide 31

Peer Reviewing

Journals do several rounds of reviewing. In each round, you must submit a new version of your manuscript and reply to the reviewers’ comments.

By contrast, most conferences do a single round of reviewing. Some conferences feature a lightweight second round called rebuttal.

At some conferences, your paper may be accepted conditionally. The paper is then assigned a shepherd, who guides you through the revision process.

期刊审稿有好几轮: 每一轮都要交新版稿件, 并逐条回复审稿意见. 会议大多只有一轮; 有些会议有一个简短的第二轮, 叫 rebuttal (作者答辩: 看到审稿意见后可以简短回应、澄清误解). 有些会议会有条件地接收论文, 并给它指派一位 shepherd (「牧羊人」), 指导你完成修改.

Typesetting

Most computer scientists typeset their papers using LaTeX. A small minority use Microsoft Word. LaTeX is often mandated by publishers.

Some publishers work directly with the LaTeX sources. Others import the papers into some proprietary tool. Either way, the publisher might introduce typesetting errors during copy editing.

Fortunately, you get to check galley proofs before publication.

大多数计算机科学家用 LaTeX 排版论文, 少数用 Word; 出版社也常常强制要求 LaTeX. 有的出版社直接处理 LaTeX 源文件, 有的导入到自家的专有工具里; 无论哪种, 出版社在编辑加工 (copy editing) 时都可能引入排版错误. 好在出版前你可以检查校样 (galley proofs), 一定要认真看.
