逗号: 总览
Commas · Types of Commas

Slide 3–4

Commas

Commas (,) are, together with hyphens, the most troublesome punctuation mark. The rules governing comma placement in English are complex and vary across the Atlantic, and some commas are optional.

A common misconception is that a comma corresponds to a pause in speech. Most commas are required based on grammatical and logical criteria.

We cover eight correct types and two controversial types of commas. Other commas are wrong.

Types of Commas

Correct types:

  • listing;
  • joining;
  • serial;
  • bracketing;
  • introductory;
  • separating;
  • gapping;
  • vocative.

Controversial types:

  • splice;
  • discretionary.

逗号和连字符是最麻烦的标点. 英语的逗号规则复杂, 大西洋两岸不同, 有些逗号是可选的. 一个常见误解是逗号对应说话时的停顿; 其实大多数逗号是由语法和逻辑标准决定的. 本讲讲 8 种正确的逗号和 2 种有争议的逗号, 其他的逗号都是错的.

8 种正确的: 列举 (listing)、连接 (joining)、系列 (serial, 即 Oxford comma)、括注 (bracketing)、引导 (introductory)、分隔引语 (separating)、省略 (gapping)、呼语 (vocative). 2 种有争议的: 粘连 (splice)、酌情 (discretionary).

原文小错: 主语是复数 Commas (再加上 hyphens), 表语应为复数: the most troublesome punctuation marks.

Slide 5

Listing Commas

Listing commas are used to separate items in enumerations together with and or or—e.g.:

They have offices in New York, Paris and Tokyo.
The ink cartridge is available in cyan, magenta, yellow or black.

They are also used in “quick lists”—e.g.:

Let x,y be real numbers.
Most Polynesian languages have five vowels: a, e, i, o, u.
A radical, comprehensive plan was conceived.
She believed in the one, holy, universal, apostolic church of Haskell.

Nonparallel adjectives require no commas, as in the second example below:

Our method solves complex, third-order, nonlinear differential equations.

Our method solves complex third-order nonlinear differential equations.

列举逗号在列举里分隔各项, 最后两项用 and 或 or 连接 (这两例没有 Oxford comma, 见下面 Slide 7). 也用于没有 and 的「快速列表」: 数学里的 Let x, y be …; 元音 a, e, i, o, u; 并列的形容词 radical, comprehensive (激进的、全面的计划); 「她信奉唯一的、神圣的、普世的、使徒的 Haskell 教会」(戏仿《尼西亚信经》的 one, holy, catholic and apostolic church).

不平行的形容词之间不加逗号. 判断方法: 能在形容词之间插入 and、能调换顺序的, 是平行的, 用逗号 (a radical and comprehensive plan); 不能的不平行. 「complex third-order nonlinear differential equations」里, 每个形容词修饰的是它右边的整体 (nonlinear differential equations 是一类方程, third-order 修饰这一类, complex 再修饰整体), 不能说 complex and third-order and nonlinear, 所以不加逗号.

Slide 6

Joining Commas

Joining commas (also called “conjunction commas”) are used, especially in formal writing, between independent clauses connected by and, or, or but—e.g.:

Addition is denoted by +, and subtraction is denoted by −.
The data are incomplete, but we can already form a plausible conjecture.

Joining commas should also be used with imperative clauses—e.g.:

Type in your user name, and press the Enter key.

If the connected items are not independent clauses, no joining comma is used—e.g.:

He entered his user name and pressed Enter.

Joining commas can be used together with listing commas—e.g.:

Jane talked, Brian listened, and Alice read her emails.

连接逗号: 正式写作里, 用 and、or、but 连接两个独立分句 (各有自己的主语和谓语) 时, 连词前加逗号: 「加法记作 +, 减法记作 −」; 「数据不完整, 但我们已经能提出一个合理的猜想」. 两个祈使句之间也加 (Type …, and press …). 如果连接的不是独立分句, 就不加: He entered his user name and pressed Enter, 后半没有自己的主语, 只是第二个谓语. 连接逗号和列举逗号可以同时出现: Jane talked, Brian listened, and Alice read her emails (三个独立分句).

例句「The data are incomplete」把 data 当复数, 是正式写法 (见 STE 04 复数表).

Slide 7

Serial Commas

The serial comma (also called the “Oxford comma”) is optionally used before and (or et) and or in an enumeration of three or more items—e.g.:

They have offices in New York, Paris, and Tokyo.
The ink cartridge is available in cyan, magenta, yellow, or black.
We refer to Bartos, Bassalat, et al. for details.

Intuitively, and, or, and et ought to be sufficient.

Serial commas are widespread in American English, especially in formal, nonjournalistic writing. In British English, they are used mostly when they help parsing. Compare:

I admire my parents, Gandhi and Mother Teresa.

I admire my parents, Gandhi, and Mother Teresa.

系列逗号 (又叫 Oxford comma 牛津逗号) 是三项以上列举中, 最后一个 and、or (或 et al. 的 et) 前面可选的逗号. 直觉上 and、or、et 已经够了, 所以它是「多余」的; 美式英语里很普遍 (尤其在正式的非新闻写作中), 英式英语里多半只在有助于解析时使用.

经典例子: 「I admire my parents, Gandhi and Mother Teresa」可以读成「我敬佩我的父母, 也就是甘地和特蕾莎修女」(逗号后面是同位语); 加上系列逗号就只能理解为三方: 我的父母、甘地、特蕾莎修女. 选定用不用之后, 全文统一 (这门课的幻灯片一贯使用).

Slide 8

Bracketing Commas

Bracketing commas (also called “interruptive commas”) set off a nonrestrictive phrase—a phrase that could be removed without breaking the sentence. They mark the phrase as mildly parenthetical—e.g.:

Peter Hitchens’s brother, Christopher, wrote a popular but controversial book.

Essential information does not belong within bracketing commas. This is wrong:

The Nobel prize winner, Nadia Murad, was born in Kocho, Iraq.
Isaac Asimov’s book, Building Blocks of the Universe, was a popular success.

Remember the closing comma. This is wrong:

The prime minister of Great Britain, Jim Hacker is answerable to Parliament.

Sometimes the opening or the closing comma is left out, if it coincides with the start or end of the sentence or with another punctuation mark.

括注逗号 (也叫插入逗号) 把一个非限制性短语隔开: 这个短语去掉, 句子仍然完整、意思不变. 它把短语标记为轻度的插入语. 「Peter Hitchens 的哥哥 Christopher 写了一本畅销但有争议的书」: Peter Hitchens 只有一个哥哥, 名字是补充信息, 所以加逗号.

必要的信息不能放进括注逗号里. 「The Nobel prize winner, Nadia Murad, …」错: 诺贝尔奖得主不止一位, 名字是用来确定是哪一位的, 是必要信息, 不加逗号 (The Nobel prize winner Nadia Murad was born …). 阿西莫夫写了很多书, 书名同理, 不加逗号.

别忘了收尾的逗号: 「The prime minister of Great Britain, Jim Hacker is …」少了 Hacker 后面的逗号 (Jim Hacker 是英剧《是, 首相》里的虚构首相). 如果开头或结尾的逗号碰上句首、句尾或别的标点, 可以省掉 (例如插入语在句末时, 收尾的逗号被句号取代).

这和 STE 05 的限制性 / 非限制性定语从句是同一个道理.

Slide 9

Bracketing Commas

Bracketing commas can be used around nonrestrictive subordinate clauses—e.g.:

Her paternal grandmother, who moved to Brazil, was originally Indian.
The invasion of Kyushu was planned for X-Day, which was set for November 1.

They can also be used around complements that we want to parenthesize—e.g.:

This is, indeed, not the only possible scenario.
For example, the numbers 49999 and 33333331 are prime.

In conjunctions, bracketing commas can be used around the second term to mark it as a parenthetical addition or afterthought—e.g.:

General relativity is hard to understand, and even harder to explain.
The integers Z are an abelian, or commutative, group under addition.
This mode is theoretically more powerful, since it allows user interaction.

括注逗号也用在非限制性从句两边: 她的祖母 (只有一位父系祖母) 搬到了巴西; X 日 (九州登陆作战的日期) 定在 11 月 1 日. 也用在想作为插入语的补足成分两边: This is, indeed, …; For example, …. 在并列结构里, 可以把第二项用逗号隔开, 标明它是插入的补充或事后想到的: 「广义相对论很难理解, 而且更难解释」(对比 STE 06 用破折号的同一句); 「整数 Z 在加法下构成阿贝尔群, 或者说交换群」(or commutative 是同义的补充说明, 不是另一种选择); 「这种模式在理论上更强大, 因为它允许用户交互」.

例句里的 49999 和 33333331 我核对过, 确实都是素数.

Slide 10

Introductory Commas

Introductory commas are used after an adverb, adverbial phrase, or dependent clause placed at the beginning of a clause—e.g.:

If R is Noetherian, then R′ is Noetherian.
Even before she was elected, the president targeted journalists she disliked.

These commas are more frequent in American English than in British English. But even in British English, they are sometimes necessary for clarity. This is hard to parse:

According to the mine worker standard safety procedures are ineffective.

There is no introductory comma if a sentence is inverted (with the subject after the verb). This is wrong:

Between the Forum and the Campus Martius, is the Capitoline Hill.

引导逗号用在位于分句开头的副词、状语短语或从句后面: If R is Noetherian, then …; Even before she was elected, …. 美式比英式用得多, 但英式里有时也是为了清楚而必须的: 「According to the mine worker standard safety procedures are ineffective」很难解析, 读者会把 the mine worker standard safety procedures 当成一个名词短语; 应写 According to the mine worker, standard safety procedures are ineffective (据矿工说, 标准安全规程无效).

句子倒装 (主语在动词后) 时不加引导逗号: Between the Forum and the Campus Martius is the Capitoline Hill (在古罗马广场和战神广场之间的是卡比托利欧山), 主语是 the Capitoline Hill, 加了逗号就把动词 is 和前面的介词短语隔开了.

Slide 11

Separating Commas

Separating commas stand before or after direct speech, together with a verb such as say or write—e.g.:

Oscar Wilde wrote, “There is no such thing as a moral or an immoral book.”
“There is no such thing as a moral or an immoral book,” he wrote.

Notice the capitalization.

No commas are needed when the quotation is embedded in the sentence—e.g.:

Yugoslavia’s motto was “Brotherhood and Unity.”
She claimed that “technology has no value unless it makes people’s lives better.”

分隔逗号用在直接引语前后, 配合 say、write 等动词: Oscar Wilde wrote, “There is …” (引语前); “… book,” he wrote. (引语后, 逗号在引号内, 美式). 注意大写: 直接引语以大写开头, 而后面的 he 小写, 因为整体仍是一句话. 引语嵌入句子 (作表语、作 that 从句) 时不加逗号: 南斯拉夫的格言是「兄弟情谊与团结」; She claimed that “technology …”, 引文以小写开头.

Slide 12–13

Gapping Commas

The gapping comma indicates that some words are omitted. In the following example, the comma stands for is known:

Bangalore is known as the city of gardens; Mumbai, as the city of dreams.

The comma helps parsing.

Vocative Commas

The vocative comma is used to address or invoke someone or something—e.g.:

I know, Kim, I know.
Yes, minister.
Let’s eat, grandma!

Such commas hardly ever arise in scientific and technical English.

省略逗号 (gapping) 表示省掉了一些词: 「班加罗尔被称为花园之城; 孟买, 梦想之城」, 逗号代替了 is known. 这个逗号帮助解析 (STE 04 里的「the other, British」也是这种逗号).

呼语逗号用来称呼某人或某物: I know, Kim, I know; Yes, minister. 经典例子「Let’s eat, grandma!」(奶奶, 吃饭了!) 去掉逗号变成「Let’s eat grandma!」(我们把奶奶吃了吧!). 科技英语里几乎用不到.

Slide 14

Splice Commas

The splice comma separates two clauses. In Caesar’s I came, I saw, I conquered, two splice commas separate three clauses. Comma splice is widely considered poor style, but sometimes it is unavoidable—e.g.:

It is peculiar, is it not?
The Queen is dead, long live the King!
Not all tires should be replaced, just the ones that are worn out.

Most splice commas, including those below, should be replaced by periods (.) or semicolons (;).

Let a=b, then ak=bk.
You must register in time, otherwise you cannot take the examination.
We apologize for the repetition, however we do need your cooperation.

粘连逗号 (comma splice) 只用逗号分隔两个分句, 没有连词. 凯撒的「我来, 我见, 我征服」就有两个. 粘连逗号普遍被认为是坏文体, 但有时不可避免: 附加疑问句 (It is peculiar, is it not?)、固定说法 (女王驾崩, 国王万岁!)、短而紧密的对照 (不是所有轮胎都要换, 只换磨损的).

大多数粘连逗号应换成句号或分号. 例句: 「Let a = b, then a^k = b^k」改成 Let a = b. Then …, 或 If a = b, then …. 「otherwise」和「however」是副词 (见 STE 04 的词类), 不能用逗号连接两个句子: … in time; otherwise, you cannot … / … the repetition; however, we do …. 这是德语和中文母语者最常犯的标点错误之一 (德语 sonst、中文「否则」前用逗号是正确的).

Slide 15

Discretionary Commas

As a rule, there should be no comma between the subject and the verb. The last comma below is wrong:

Dodoma, Lusaka, and Nairobi, are African cities.

Similarly, there should be no comma between the verb and the direct object. The first comma below is wrong:

I visited, Dodoma, Lusaka, and Nairobi.

There are nevertheless circumstances where a discretionary comma is desirable to help parsing—e.g.:

Those who can, do; those who can’t, teach.
Those responsible for sacking the people who have just been sacked, have been sacked.

酌情逗号: 原则上主语和动词之间不加逗号 (Nairobi 后面的逗号错); 动词和直接宾语之间也不加 (visited 后面的逗号错). 但有时为了帮助解析, 可以酌情加一个: 「能者做事, 不能者教书」(萧伯纳的名言), 逗号分开了 Those who can 和 do, 否则读者会把 can do 连起来读; 「负责解雇那些刚被解雇的人的那些人, 也被解雇了」(来自英国喜剧电影《巨蟒与圣杯》的片头字幕), 主语很长, 逗号标出主语在哪里结束 (这也是左重句, 见 STE 05).

Hyphens

Slide 17

Hyphens

Hyphen (-) usage is difficult. It does not help that writers on either side of the Atlantic disagree about hyphens, as do writers from different decades or centuries.

We distinguish between two main types of hyphens:

  • The intraword hyphen joins two parts of a word. One part will usually be a prefix or suffix—e.g., co-axial, un-English, mid-1990s, tax-free, 20-fold.
  • The interword hyphen connects two words. The combined words then form a compound adjective (e.g., well-designed, customer-oriented), noun (e.g., right-winger), or verb (e.g., binge-eat).

连字符的用法很难, 而且大西洋两岸的作者意见不一, 不同年代、世纪的作者也不一样. 两大类: 词内连字符连接一个词的两部分, 其中一部分通常是前缀或后缀 (co-axial, un-English, mid-1990s, tax-free, 20-fold); 词间连字符连接两个词, 组成复合形容词 (well-designed)、复合名词 (right-winger 右翼分子) 或复合动词 (binge-eat 暴食).

Slide 18

Hyphens

Most hyphens can be avoided by joining the two word components directly (e.g., coaxial) or by putting a space (e.g., well designed).

  • The American tendency is to avoid intraword hyphens by joining words directly.
  • The British tendency is to avoid interword hyphens by using a space.

Thus, a nonaddictive second-generation drug is mainly American, whereas a non-addictive second generation drug is mainly British.

Each hyphen plays a small role:

  • In non-addictive, the hyphen cleanly separates the prefix non- from the familiar word addictive, making this combination easier to parse.
  • In second-generation drug, the hyphen prevents the misreading second generation-drug.

大多数连字符都可以避免: 要么把两部分直接连写 (coaxial), 要么用空格隔开 (well designed). 美式倾向于连写来避免词内连字符; 英式倾向于用空格来避免词间连字符. 所以「a nonaddictive second-generation drug (一种不成瘾的第二代药物)」是美式, 「a non-addictive second generation drug」是英式 (两者正好在两个位置上相反).

每个连字符都有一点作用: non-addictive 里, 连字符把前缀 non- 和熟悉的词 addictive 干净地分开, 更好解析; second-generation drug 里, 连字符防止读成 second generation-drug (第二种「代药」).

词内连字符
Intraword Hyphens

Slide 19

Intraword Hyphens

Some prefixes, such as ex- and Greek letters, are always followed by an intraword hyphen—e.g., ex-servicewoman, β-carotene.

Other combinations that require a hyphen involve the combining forms all-, cross-, and self-—e.g., all-important, cross-examiner, self-delusion.

To avoid awkward symbol combinations, use a hyphen when attaching a prefix to a capitalized word or a number, even if the prefix normally does not require a hyphen—e.g., un-Pythonic, pre-1984.

Hyphens can also help avoid ambiguity. Distinguish between re-prove (“prove again”) and reprove (“reprimand”), between co-op (“cooperative,” two syllables) and coop (“cage,” one syllable).

有些前缀后面总是加连字符: ex- (前任的: ex-servicewoman 退伍女军人) 和希腊字母 (β-carotene β-胡萝卜素, 计算机里的 λ-calculus). 组合形式 all-、cross-、self- 也要加 (all-important 极其重要的, cross-examiner 交叉询问者, self-delusion 自欺). 前缀接在大写词或数字前时加连字符, 避免难看的组合, 即使这个前缀平时不加: un-Pythonic (不符合 Python 风格的), pre-1984. 连字符还能消除歧义: re-prove (再证一次) 和 reprove (责备); co-op (合作社, 两个音节) 和 coop (鸡笼, 一个音节).

Slide 20

Intraword Hyphens

Some words are hyphenated because this avoids unpleasant letter combinations, eases parsing, or both—e.g., anti-inflammatory, ball-like, co-opt, co-owner, and meta-analysis.

Optional intraword hyphens are predominant in British English and older varieties of American English but rarely seen in modern American texts. Thus, anti-clockwise, element-wise, non-empty, pre-war, and rat-like are mainly British, whereas counterclockwise, elementwise, nonempty, prewar, and ratlike are mainly American.

Prefixes and suffixes are not separate words. These are wrong: meta language, non null, raccoon like.

Finally, when splitting a word across two lines, we insert an intraword hyphen be-tween two syllables. To find out where syllables start and end, use a dictionary.

有些词加连字符是为了避免难看的字母组合 (两个相同的元音: anti-inflammatory, co-opt, co-owner, meta-analysis; 三个 l: ball-like), 或便于解析, 或两者兼有. 可选的词内连字符在英式英语和较老的美式英语里占主导, 现代美式文本里很少见: anti-clockwise / counterclockwise (逆时针, 英美用的前缀也不同)、element-wise / elementwise (逐元素)、non-empty / nonempty、pre-war / prewar、rat-like / ratlike.

前缀和后缀不是独立的词, 不能用空格隔开: meta language、non null、raccoon like 都错, 要么连写要么加连字符. 最后, 一个词在行末断开时, 在两个音节之间加连字符 (幻灯片上的 be-tween 正好示范了这一点); 音节在哪里分开, 查词典.

Slide 21

Interword Hyphens

When building compounds, you must often choose between three options:

  • hyphenated compounds (e.g., type-checking);
  • closed compounds (e.g., typechecking);
  • open compounds (e.g., type checking).

There is a strong tendency in favor of open compounds, except when a hyphenated or closed form helps parsing or denotes a new concept, distinct from the composition of its constituent parts.

Below, we review some general principles, but they are no substitute for a good dictionary, search engines, word frequency viewers, and sound judgment.

构成复合词时常要在三种形式之间选择: 带连字符 (type-checking)、连写 (typechecking)、分开 (type checking). 总趋势强烈偏向分开写, 除非连字符或连写的形式有助于解析, 或者表示一个新概念, 不同于各组成部分意思的简单组合. 下面是一些一般原则, 但它们代替不了好词典、搜索引擎、词频查看器和良好的判断力.

Slide 22

Interword Hyphens

Unreadable phrases such as variable state independent decaying sum heuristic abound in technical and scientific writing. Interword hyphens help readability: variable-state-independent decaying-sum heuristic.

A hyphen can be inserted if it eases reading or for consistency. It is good style, especially in American English, to write oil-based paint, this so-called nutritionist, and a step-by-step method. In contrast, a step by step method would be hard to parse. But in We proceed step-by-step, the hyphens are undesirable.

What should you do about middle school physical science curriculum? Since the phrases middle school and physical science are established, they can be left as is. Middle-school physical-science curriculum would be distractingly ugly.

技术和科学写作里满是 variable state independent decaying sum heuristic 这种读不懂的短语 (SAT 求解器里著名的 VSIDS 启发式). 词间连字符提高可读性: variable-state-independent decaying-sum heuristic, 一眼看出是「与变量状态无关的」+「衰减求和的」+ 启发式.

如果有助于阅读或为了一致, 可以加连字符. 尤其在美式英语里, oil-based paint、this so-called nutritionist、a step-by-step method 是好文体; a step by step method 难以解析. 但 step-by-step 作状语 (We proceed step by step) 时不加: 这又是「作定语加, 不作定语不加」的规则. middle school physical science curriculum (初中物理科学课程) 怎么办? middle school 和 physical science 都是固定说法, 读者一看就懂, 保持原样即可; 加成 Middle-school physical-science curriculum 反而难看.

Slide 23

Interword Hyphens

English allows the formation of arbitrary adjectival phrases, as in her you-are-charming-but-useless look. You can also use quotes: her “you are charming but useless” look. Such expressions are informal.

In Latin expressions such as ad hoc, de facto, and ex post facto, hyphens are unwelcome. Unlike free college plan, de facto standard can be parsed in only one way.

A similar case is that of adverbs modifying adjectives qualifying nouns. On the model of free-college plan, you may be tempted to write highly-sensitive information or ever-stronger union. But since adverbs cannot modify nouns, these hyphens resolve no ambiguities and are therefore undesirable. Sensitive information cannot be highly, and a stronger union cannot be ever.

英语允许随意组成形容词短语: her you-are-charming-but-useless look (她那「你很迷人但没用」的眼神), 也可以用引号代替连字符. 这种表达是非正式的.

拉丁短语 ad hoc、de facto、ex post facto 不加连字符: free college plan 可以读成「免费的大学计划」或「免费上大学的计划」(要写成 free-college plan 才明确), 而 de facto standard (事实标准) 只有一种读法, 不需要连字符.

类似地, 「副词 + 形容词 + 名词」不加连字符: highly-sensitive information、ever-stronger union 都不需要. 副词不能修饰名词, 所以 highly 只能修饰 sensitive, 没有歧义可消. 「敏感信息不可能是 highly 的」. 规则: 以 -ly 结尾的副词后面不加连字符.

Slide 24

Interword Hyphens

Hyphens are used to form compounds that seem to defy the laws of grammar. A phrase such as to stress test is disallowed because a noun (stress) cannot modify a verb (test)—this would be the role of an adverb. To solve this, use a hyphen: to stress-test, stress-tested, etc. The hyphen usually disappears in related forms when it is not needed (e.g., a stress test, stress testing).

Besides “noun + verb,” other problematic combinations include “adjective + verb” (e.g., double-check, dry-clean), “noun + adjective” (e.g., vendor-specific), and “noun + adverb” (e.g., carbon-neutrally). For some commonly used combinations, dictionaries record closed compounds (e.g., to copyedit, to proofread, to spellcheck).

连字符用来构成看似违反语法的复合词: to stress test 不行, 因为名词 stress 不能修饰动词 test (那是副词的事); 用连字符: to stress-test (做压力测试)、stress-tested. 不需要时连字符会消失: a stress test (名词修饰名词, 正常)、stress testing. 其他有问题的组合: 形容词 + 动词 (double-check, dry-clean)、名词 + 形容词 (vendor-specific 厂商专用的)、名词 + 副词 (carbon-neutrally). 一些常用组合在词典里已经是连写词: copyedit, proofread, spellcheck.

Slide 25

Interword Hyphens

Besides enhancing readability, interword hyphens can also be used to build a new concept whose meaning might not be obvious from the constituent words.

Accordingly, a bee-eater is a bird and not just any eater of bees, and a man-hater is a person who hates men and not a hater who is a man. A similar convention explains editor-in-chief, parent-in-law, and tug-of-war. However, for some established phrases such as black box and blue ice, the hyphen is curiously missing.

Hyphenated compounds used to be common. They appeared in hay-stack, Oxford-street, tea-pot, to-day, etc. The preference nowadays is for closed and open compounds—e.g., haystack, Oxford Street, teapot, today.

Dictionaries can often help, and for compounds that are not recorded in them, the preferable form is almost always the open compound.

词间连字符除了提高可读性, 还能构造一个新概念, 它的意思从组成的词看不出来: bee-eater 是一种鸟 (蜂虎), 不是随便什么吃蜜蜂的东西; man-hater 是厌恶男人的人, 不是一个男性的仇恨者. editor-in-chief (主编)、parent-in-law (姻亲父母)、tug-of-war (拔河) 同理. 但有些固定短语偏偏不加: black box (黑箱)、blue ice (蓝冰).

带连字符的复合词过去很常见 (hay-stack, Oxford-street, tea-pot, to-day), 现在倾向于连写或分开写 (haystack, Oxford Street, teapot, today). 词典常能帮忙; 词典里没有的复合词, 几乎总是以分开写为好.

Interword Hyphens

What should you do when faced with a specific expression?

Consider farm boy. It could also plausibly be written as farm-boy or farmboy. But:

  • Farmboy is not in the dictionary.
  • There is no awkward letter cluster justifying farm-boy.
  • Farm boy literally means what it says.

The closed compound is not established, and the hyphenated form offers no benefits. By exclusion, we have farm boy, which is indeed about 20 times more frequent in print than either of the other two forms according to Google Books Ngram Viewer. (In retrospect, we could have started with that tool.)

遇到具体的词组时怎么办? 以 farm boy (农家男孩) 为例, 也可以写成 farm-boy 或 farmboy. 但: farmboy 不在词典里; 没有需要用连字符避开的难看字母组合; farm boy 的意思就是字面意思. 连写形式没有确立, 连字符形式没有好处, 用排除法得到 farm boy. 按 Ngram Viewer, 它在出版物里的频率约是另两种的 20 倍 (事后看, 我们本可以一开始就用这个工具). 这个数字我无法核对.

Slide 27

Interword Hyphens

There remains a pair of special cases.

In coordinations, you can have a trailing hyphen after a prefix or word, as in pre- and postnatal (or pre- and post-natal) and water- or oil-based. Remember the space between the hyphen and the coordinating conjunction.

An analogous but worse construction works in reverse: Anglo-Welsh, -Scottish, and -Irish abbreviates Anglo-Welsh, Anglo-Scottish, and Anglo-Irish.

两个特殊情况. 并列结构里, 前缀或词后面可以留一个悬挂连字符: pre- and postnatal (产前和产后)、water- or oil-based (水基或油基). 记得连字符和连词之间有空格. 反方向的类似构造更差: Anglo-Welsh, -Scottish, and -Irish 是 Anglo-Welsh, Anglo-Scottish, and Anglo-Irish 的缩写, 读起来很别扭, 最好写全.
