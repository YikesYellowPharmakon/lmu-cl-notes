为什么做报告
Why Give Talks? · Organization · Content · Audience

Slide 3

Why Give Talks?

Some people process information better when listening or watching than when reading.

Moreover, talks are often unavoidable:

  • In academia: thesis defenses, conference talks, lectures, etc.
  • In industry: project pitches, annual reports, etc.
  • In both: job interviews.

Purposes of an academic talk:

  • Advertise your paper or thesis.
  • Teach something interesting.

有些人听和看比读更容易接受信息. 而且报告往往躲不掉: 学术界有答辩、会议报告、讲课; 工业界有项目推介 (pitch)、年度汇报; 两边都有求职面试. 学术报告的目的: 宣传你的论文或学位论文 (让人愿意去读), 以及教听众一点有趣的东西.

Slide 4

Organization

Remember to motivate your work at the beginning and to summarize it at the end. You can close with directions for future work. Think carefully about the last slide, since it usually remains shown during the questions.

Many talks have too many slides. Allow 1.5 to 2 minutes per slide. You can put extra slides in an appendix to help with the questions.

Use a sensible slide budget. In particular, keep the introduction and background short. An outline can be helpful for longer talks.

开头讲动机, 结尾做总结, 可以再提一下未来的工作. 最后一页要好好设计: 提问环节它通常一直挂在屏幕上 (放核心结论比放「Thank you! Questions?」更有用). 很多报告幻灯片太多: 每页按 1.5–2 分钟算, 20 分钟的报告大约 10–13 页. 备用的幻灯片可以放在附录里, 回答问题时用. 合理分配页数, 尤其是引言和背景要短; 较长的报告可以放一页提纲.

Slide 5

Content

Focus on one or two key ideas, ruthlessly pruning everything else. For example, you can usually omit most related work and most references.

When necessary, you can cite the literature using author names and publication years (e.g., Agarkar et al. 2009), or provide longer references in footnotes.

Mention the key ideas throughout, and summarize them at the end.

集中讲一两个核心思想, 其余的毫不留情地删掉; 大部分相关工作和参考文献通常都可以不讲. 需要引用时用「作者 年份」的形式 (Agarkar et al. 2009), 比编号 [12] 更有信息量, 因为幻灯片上没有文献表; 也可以把较长的出处放进脚注 (第 9 讲说过, 脚注在幻灯片上保留了下来). 核心思想要贯穿始终, 结尾再总结一遍.

Slide 6

Audience

Consider your audience. Beware of the curse of knowledge.

Some talks are necessarily technical and difficult for nonexperts to understand. In view of this:

  • Most slides should be light in detail and accessible to nonexperts.
  • The core slides should contain sufficient detail for experts.

Elaborate as time permits. Be aware that slides make it easy to go too fast for the audience.

考虑听众. 小心「知识的诅咒 (curse of knowledge)」: 自己懂了, 就很难想象别人不懂时是什么样子 (见 STE 02). 有些报告免不了很技术化, 外行难以理解; 办法是: 大部分幻灯片细节少、外行能看懂; 核心的几页给专家足够的细节. 时间允许就多展开. 用幻灯片讲很容易讲得太快 (翻一页只要一秒), 要照顾听众的速度.

Slide 7

Two Schools of Slide Design

Minimalist

  • The goal is to underscore key points while letting the audience focus on what you are saying.
  • This style can be impactful.
  • This style is appropriate for less technical material.

Maximalist

  • The slides alone are sufficient to get the gist of the talk.
  • The slides can serve as a self-contained handout.
  • This style is appropriate for more technical material.

幻灯片设计有两派. 极简派: 幻灯片只用来强调要点, 让听众把注意力放在你讲的话上; 冲击力强; 适合技术性不强的内容 (例图是产品发布会式的一页一个大数字). 极繁派: 只看幻灯片就能明白报告的大意, 幻灯片本身可以当讲义; 适合技术性强的内容 (例图是一页写满三步证明思路的数学幻灯片). 这门课自己的幻灯片 (handout 版) 就是极繁派.

Slide 8

Avoid Clutter

Slides are not a paper. These are far too cluttered even for maximalists:

Slides should summarize or supplement your message. They are not for reading out word for word.

You do not need to repeat your name, your organization, the title of your talk, and the date on every slide.

幻灯片不是论文. 例图里的两页太杂乱, 连极繁派都不能接受. 幻灯片应当概括或补充你说的话, 而不是让你照着念. 也不用在每一页上重复你的姓名、单位、报告题目和日期 (很多模板默认这么做, 占地方又分散注意力).

Slide 9

Writing

Much writing advice also applies to slides:

  • Omit needless words.
  • Use short sentences.
  • Prefer the active voice over the passive.
  • Prefer verbs over zombie nouns.
  • Use abbreviations sparingly.

Moreover, use consistent punctuation, especially periods.

前几讲的写作建议在幻灯片上同样适用: 删去多余的词; 用短句; 主动语态优先于被动; 动词优先于「僵尸名词」(zombie noun, 由动词变来的抽象名词, 如 make an examination of 不如 examine, 见 STE 03); 少用缩写. 另外标点要一致, 尤其是句号: 要么所有要点都加句号, 要么都不加.

Slide 10–11

Formatting

Some formatting advice:

  • Be consistent.
  • Use large enough fonts.
  • Use emphasis sparingly.
  • Leave space between elements.
  • Use a pleasing color palette.

Background Color

Should you use a light or a dark background?

It depends on the projector or screen and the font. With some projectors, a light background can be very bright and tiring. But text on a dark background can be difficult to read if the font is too thin.

格式建议: 保持一致; 字号够大; 少用强调; 元素之间留出空白; 配色要悦目. 背景用浅色还是深色? 取决于投影仪或屏幕, 以及字体: 有的投影仪上浅色背景亮得刺眼、看久了累; 但深色背景上的文字如果字体太细, 又很难看清 (深底配粗一些的字体更好).

Slide 12

Examples and Visuals

Use examples where possible—e.g.:

Keep the examples simple.

Images, graphs, and tiny code excerpts are doubly useful:

  • They are efficient ways to convey information.
  • They relieve from the monotony of textual slides.

Make sure to explain them.

Highlighting can help draw the audience’s attention to key points.

能举例就举例, 例子要简单. 例图是一个确定有限自动机 (DFA): 从 q0 读两个 a 到达接受状态 q2, 之后再读多少个 a 都留在 q2, 所以它接受至少含两个 a 的串 (aa, aaa, …). 图、图表和很短的代码片段有双重好处: 传递信息效率高; 打破满屏文字的单调. 但要讲解它们. 用高亮可以把听众的注意力引到要点上.

语法 (第 12 张): 「They relieve from the monotony of textual slides」缺宾语: relieve 是及物动词, 要说「relieve something / someone from …」. 应为「They relieve the audience from the monotony …」, 或改成「They provide relief from the monotony …」/「They break the monotony …」.

Slide 13–14

Animations

Animations help the audience follow you through the slides.

You can reveal elements one by one.

Using too many animations can come across as patronizing.

Live Demonstrations

Live demonstrations, including live coding and writing on the white- or blackboard, can be part of a successful talk.

You need to be very well prepared and have good nerves.

动画能帮听众跟上你的思路, 例如把要点一条一条显示出来; 但动画太多会显得居高临下 (像在哄小孩). 现场演示 (现场写代码、在白板或黑板上写) 可以让报告更成功, 但要准备得非常充分, 还要神经够坚强 (现场出错的概率不小, 最好备一段录屏).

讲稿与软件

Slide 15

Presenter Notes

You can write presenter notes if necessary. This may increase your confidence. In addition, notes are helpful if you have difficulties expressing yourself in English.

Most presentation software allows you to see the notes while you show the slides. You can also print the notes.

Beware that if you stick too closely to the notes, your talk might become stilted and unnatural.

需要的话可以写演讲者备注 (presenter notes): 能增加信心; 英语表达有困难时尤其有用. 大多数演示软件在放映时可以让你在自己的屏幕上看到备注, 也可以打印出来. 但太依赖备注, 报告会变得生硬、不自然.

Slide 16

Presentation Software

Some presentation software:

  • Microsoft PowerPoint and Apple Keynote offer a “what you see is what you get” interface.
  • Google Slides is a basic alternative that supports collaborative slide writing.
  • LaTeX’s beamer class is ideal for highly mathematical content. It is very flexible but difficult to master. Customization is necessary to make your slides look attractive.

演示软件: PowerPoint 和 Keynote 是「所见即所得」的界面; Google Slides 功能基础, 但支持多人协作; LaTeX 的 beamer 文档类最适合数学内容很多的报告, 非常灵活但难以精通, 而且要定制才好看 (默认主题一眼就能认出来). 这门课的幻灯片就是用 beamer 做的.

Examples of Slide Decks

Let us look at a minimalist slide deck for a PhD defense and a maximalist slide deck for a scientific conference talk (first draft and final version):

  • Roy Overbeek, “A Unifying Theory for Graph Transformation,” 2024.
  • Lydia Kondylidou, “Augmenting Model-Based Instantiation with Fast Enumeration: Extending SMT Solving,” 2025 (first draft).
  • Lydia Kondylidou, “Augmenting Model-Based Instantiation with Fast Enumeration in SMT,” 2025 (final version).

课上要看的三份幻灯片: 一份极简派的博士答辩幻灯片 (图变换的统一理论, 2024); 一份极繁派的会议报告幻灯片的初稿和终稿 (2025). 对照初稿和终稿, 连题目都改短了: 「…: Extending SMT Solving」变成「… in SMT」, 正好是第 10 讲修改的实例.

Slide 19

Why Present Posters?

Poster presentations are a way to present your research, beyond conference and workshop talks.

During a poster session, researchers present their work in parallel to their peers, who walk around from one poster to the next.

Purposes of an academic poster:

  • Advertise your paper or thesis.
  • Teach something interesting.

海报展示是会议报告之外介绍研究的另一种方式. 海报环节 (poster session) 里, 许多研究者同时在各自的海报前讲解, 同行在一张张海报之间走动. 目的和学术报告一样: 宣传你的论文, 教点有趣的东西.

Slide 20

Organization and Content

A typical poster consist of about 9 to 12 sections or text boxes, each of which is similar to a slide.

Much of the advice on slide deck design also applies to posters, but you generally have even less space for content.

Appealing visuals are crucial to attract attention.

一张典型的海报大约有 9–12 个部分或文本框, 每个相当于一页幻灯片. 幻灯片设计的建议大多也适用于海报, 但篇幅通常更紧. 吸引人的图是关键: 路过的人先被图吸引, 才会停下来.

语法 (第 20 张): 「A typical poster consist of …」主语是单数, 动词要加 -s: 「A typical poster consists of …」.

Slide 21

Two Schools of Poster Design

Minimalist

  • The goal is to underscore key points while letting the visitor focus on what you are saying.
  • This style is appropriate for less technical material.

Maximalist

  • The poster makes sense on its own, so it can be viewed without explanation.
  • This style is appropriate for more technical material—or if you will not stand next to the poster.

海报设计也分两派. 极简派: 只强调要点, 让参观者专心听你讲; 适合技术性不强的内容. 极繁派: 海报本身就能看懂, 不需要讲解; 适合技术性强的内容, 或者你不会一直站在海报旁边的时候 (比如海报要在走廊里挂好几天).

Slide 22

Poster Software

Some poster software:

  • Microsoft PowerPoint and Apple Keynote can be tweaked to produce a poster as one large slide. Then there is no need to learn a new tool.
  • Adobe Illustrator supports sophisticated graphic design and image processing.
  • LaTeX is ideal for highly mathematical content. It is very flexible but difficult to master. Customization is necessary to make your poster look attractive.

做海报的软件: PowerPoint 和 Keynote 把整张海报做成一页超大的幻灯片 (把页面尺寸设成 A0 之类), 不用学新工具; Adobe Illustrator 支持复杂的平面设计和图像处理; LaTeX (例如 tikzposter、baposter 等宏包) 最适合数学内容多的海报, 同样灵活但难精通, 要定制才好看.
