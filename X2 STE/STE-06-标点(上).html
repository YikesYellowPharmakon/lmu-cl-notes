总论
Generalities

Slide 3–4

Punctuation Marks

The main punctuation marks are

  • periods;
  • question marks;
  • exclamation points;
  • colons;
  • semicolons;
  • commas;
  • long dashes;
  • short dashes;
  • hyphens;
  • slashes;
  • apostrophes;
  • quotation marks;
  • parentheses;
  • ellipses.

Commas and hyphens are more difficult and kept for the next lecture.

The Role of Punctuation

The main goals of punctuation are clarity and comprehensibility. Many punctuation rules directly serve these goals, but some can also seem arbitrary.

Even following the arbitrary rules ultimately serves clarity, because readers will parse your sentences faster and more reliably if you follow the established conventions.

主要的标点符号: 句号 (period)、问号、感叹号、冒号、分号、逗号、长破折号 (long dash, 即 em dash —)、短破折号 (short dash, 即 en dash –)、连字符 (hyphen -)、斜线、撇号、引号、圆括号、省略号. 逗号和连字符更难, 留到下一讲.

标点的主要目标是清楚和易懂. 很多规则直接服务于这个目标, 有些规则看起来很随意; 但即使遵守随意的规则, 最终也有助于清楚: 读者熟悉既定惯例, 你遵守它们, 读者解析句子就更快、更可靠.

句号、问号、感叹号
Easy Punctuation Marks · Periods · Question Marks · Exclamation Points

Slide 6

Periods

Periods (.) (also called “full stops” in British English) appear

  • at the end of sentences;
  • in some abbreviations (e.g., e.g., Dr., U.S.).

句号 (英式英语叫 full stop) 用在句末和一些缩写里. 缩写的句点在句末时只写一个: 句子以 etc. 结尾时不再加句号 (… and so on, etc.).

Slide 7

Question Marks

Use question marks (?) for actual questions. Questions can liven up your text by engaging the reader.

Beware of indirect speech. The following sentence is a statement, not a question:

I wonder whether there is a more efficient approach?

Avoid multiple question marks in a row—e.g.:

How come there is a memory leak??

Avoid question marks in parentheses—e.g.:

Lisp was invented around 1960 (?) by John McCarthy.

问号只用于真正的问题. 问题能吸引读者, 让文本生动. 注意间接引语: 「I wonder whether there is a more efficient approach」是陈述句 (我想知道是否……), 句末用句号. 不要连用几个问号 (而且 How come 是口语). 不要在括号里放问号表示不确定: 不确定就去查, 查不到就写「around 1958, according to …」之类.

例句本身的事实: McCarthy 在 1958 年开始设计 Lisp, 1960 年发表论文「Recursive Functions of Symbolic Expressions and Their Computation by Machine, Part I」, 所以「around 1960」大致不错, 也正说明不确定时应该查证, 而不是写 (?).

Slide 8

Exclamation Points

Apart from their mathematical uses (e.g., n!), exclamation points (!) have virtually no place in formal writing. You can be emphatic without using them. Compare:

This is still the case when postprocessing after every step!
The current definition of fib does not terminate!
Try to think of this as a stimulus to further investigation!

Frustratingly, this is still the case when postprocessing after every step.
Shockingly, the current definition of fib does not terminate.
Try to think of this as a stimulus to further investigation.

Avoid multiple exclamation points in sequence, and avoid exclamation points in parentheses. Compare:

The information is stored in an unordered (!) pair.

Notice that the information is stored in an unordered pair.

除了数学用途 (阶乘 n!), 感叹号在正式写作里几乎没有位置. 不用感叹号也能表达强调: 用句子副词说出你的情绪 (Frustratingly, Shockingly), 或者干脆去掉 (第三句的祈使句本身就够了). 不要连用感叹号, 也不要在括号里放感叹号表示惊讶: (!) 换成 Notice that ….

冒号与分号
Colons · Semicolons

Slide 9

Colons

You can use a colon (:) after a complete clause instead of a period (.) if the clause introduces what follows—e.g.:

There are three paint primary colors: red, yellow, and blue.
An explanation suggests itself: The training data set is heavily biased in favor of easy problems.

Capitalization of what follows the colon is slightly tricky:

  • Sentence fragments are never capitalized.
  • In American English, some authors capitalize full sentences, whereas others do not. In British English, full sentences are not capitalized.

Some authors use colons extensively. Others never use them. The “editor” should ensure a uniform style.

冒号: 如果一个完整的从句引出后面的内容, 可以用冒号代替句号. 关键是冒号前面必须是完整的句子: 「三种颜料原色是: 红、黄、蓝」(There are three … colors: …). 冒号后面要不要大写: 句子片段 (red, yellow, and blue) 从不大写; 完整的句子, 美式英语里有人大写有人不大写 (这门课的幻灯片都大写, 如第二个例子的 The), 英式英语不大写. 有些作者大量用冒号, 有些从不用, 由「编辑」统一文体.

易错: 中文和德语习惯在动词或介词后面加冒号 (「包括: A、B、C」), 英语里这是错的: 不能写 The primary colors are: red, yellow, and blue, 因为 are 后面句子还没完.

Slide 10

Colons

In scientific and technical writing, a colon is frequently used between a sentence and displayed material such as a standalone equation or a code fragment—e.g.:

The following code swaps the contents of two variables:

temp = a
a = b
b = temp

When the displayed material is part of the sentence, no colons should be used. Both colons below are wrong:

To achieve thread-safety, we need to replace:

public void calculate()

with:

public synchronized void calculate()

科技写作里冒号常用在句子和展示的内容 (单独一行的公式、代码片段) 之间: 「下面的代码交换两个变量的内容:」, 冒号前是完整的句子. 但如果展示的内容本身是句子的一部分, 就不能用冒号. 第二个例子读起来是一句话: we need to replace [public void calculate()] with [public synchronized void calculate()]; replace 和 with 后面句子都没完, 两个冒号都是错的, 直接去掉.

这就是上一页规则的应用: 冒号前面必须是完整的从句. 判断方法: 把展示的内容读进句子里, 能连成一句话就不加冒号.

Slide 11

Colons

Finally, a colon is used between a title and a subtitle in running text or reference lists—e.g.:

Lambda, the Ultimate TA: Using a Proof Assistant to Teach Programming Language Foundations1
Guarded Kleene Algebra with Tests: Verification of Uninterpreted Programs in Nearly Linear Time2

1 Benjamin C. Pierce, ICFP 2009, pp. 121–122, ACM, 2009.
2 Steffen Smolka, Nate Foster, Justin Hsu, Tobias Kappé, Dexter Kozen, and Alexandra Silva, Proceedings of the ACM on Programming Languages 4(POPL), pp. 61:1–61:28, 2020.

最后, 在正文或参考文献里, 标题和副标题之间用冒号 (即 STE 02 的「双管标题」). 第一个标题「Lambda, the Ultimate TA」套用了 Lisp 圈著名的「Lambda: the Ultimate …」系列论文标题 (TA 是助教).

Slide 12

Semicolons

Semicolons (;) can be used as “lightweight periods” between sentences that belong closely together. Often, the joined sentences exhibit parallelism—e.g.:

Thanks to computer algebra systems, working mathematicians are no longer spending their time performing tedious calculations; simulation tools bring similar benefits to physicists.

Some systems include a postprocessor that simplifies the data set before presenting it to the user; others simply output the data set as is.

Semicolons are more common in English than in most other languages.

Some authors use semicolons extensively. Others never use them. The “editor” should ensure a uniform style.

分号可以当作「轻量级句号」, 连接关系紧密的两个句子; 这两个句子常常是平行的: 计算机代数系统让数学家不再做繁琐计算; 仿真工具给物理学家带来类似的好处. 有些系统包含后处理器, 在呈现给用户前简化数据集; 另一些直接原样输出. 分号在英语里比在大多数其他语言里更常见. 有些作者大量用分号, 有些从不用, 由「编辑」统一.

分号两边都必须是完整的句子. 这也是改正 comma splice (逗号连接两个独立句子) 的标准方法之一.

Slide 13

Semicolons

Semicolons can also be used as “heavyweight commas” for separating items in an enumeration when the items themselves already contain commas—e.g.:

We reserve the letters f,g,h for functions; m,n for natural numbers; and s,t,u for terms.

分号也可以当作「重量级逗号」: 列举的各项内部已经有逗号时, 项与项之间用分号. 例句: 字母 f、g、h 表示函数; m、n 表示自然数; s、t、u 表示项. 注意最后一项前面仍然是「; and」.

长短破折号与斜线
Long Dashes · Short Dashes · Slashes

Slide 14

Long Dashes

Long dashes (—) are very versatile.

They can be used instead of commas or parentheses to indicate a longer pause—e.g.:

General relativity is hard to understand—and even harder to explain.
The need for users to perform so many interactions—the lack of automation—is undoubtedly the main obstacle to the technology’s adoption.

Especially in British English, long dashes are sometimes shorter and surrounded by spaces—e.g., . . . understand – and even . . . .

Typesetters call ‘—’ an em dash and ‘–’ an en dash.

Some authors use long dashes extensively. Others never use them. The “editor” should ensure a uniform style.

长破折号 (—) 用途很多. 它可以代替逗号或括号, 表示更长的停顿: 单个破折号引出补充 (广义相对论很难理解, 更难解释); 一对破折号插入说明 (用户需要进行如此多的交互, 即缺乏自动化, 无疑是这项技术推广的主要障碍), 一对破折号像一对括号, 但更醒目. 英式英语里长破折号有时更短, 两边加空格 ( – ). 排版术语: — 叫 em dash (约一个 M 宽), – 叫 en dash (约一个 N 宽).

LaTeX 里 em dash 写 ---, en dash 写 --, 连字符写 -. 用 Word 时, 自动更正往往会把 -- 变成 en dash.

Slide 15

Short Dashes

Fine typesetting dictates the use of short dashes (–) in the following cases:

  • Short dashes indicate number ranges—e.g., pages 20–35, in 1789–1914.
  • Especially in American English, short dashes are used to form compound adjectives from two or more nouns that are considered equals—e.g., Church–Turing thesis, Backus–Naur form, Frankfurt–Tokyo flight.
  • Occasionally, short dashes are used in compound adjectives as in pre–World War I era. With a hyphen, pre-World War I era could be misparsed as “War I era that is pre-World.”

Especially in British English, short dashes (–) may look identical to long dashes ( – ), except that they are not surrounded by spaces.

When using short dashes for number ranges, beware of redundant constructions such as between 1789–1914. (Change to between 1789 and 1914 or 1789–1914.)

短破折号 (–) 用于: ① 数字范围: 第 20–35 页, 1789–1914 年; ② (尤其美式英语) 由两个以上地位相等的名词组成复合形容词: Church–Turing thesis (丘奇和图灵两人)、Backus–Naur form、Frankfurt–Tokyo flight (从法兰克福到东京); ③ 偶尔用于前缀加多词复合: pre–World War I era (第一次世界大战之前的时代); 用连字符的话, pre-World 会被读成一个词, 整个短语被误解为「前世界的一战时代」.

英式英语里短破折号可能和 (加空格的) 长破折号外观一样, 区别只在两边没有空格. 用短破折号表示范围时, 不要和 between 或 from 混用: between 1789–1914 是冗余的, 应为 between 1789 and 1914 (或 from 1789 to 1914), 或者只写 1789–1914.

易错: Church–Turing 用 en dash 是因为两个人; 如果是一个人的复姓 (如 Lennard-Jones potential, 一个人叫 Lennard-Jones) 就用连字符. 用错了读者会以为是两个人.

Slide 16

Slashes

Slashes (/) usually mean “or” or “and”—e.g., the pure/applied dichotomy.

Slashes are clumsy and generally best avoided in formal writing. Compare:

The support desk should respond to tickets in a rapid/timely manner.

The support desk should respond to tickets in a timely manner.

If/when the handle is closed, the resource is returned to the operating system.

When the handle is closed, the resource is returned to the operating system.

It is almost always best to replace the construction X and/or Y by one of

  • X and Y;
  • X or Y;
  • X, Y, or both.

斜线通常表示「或」或「和」: the pure/applied dichotomy (纯数学与应用数学的二分). 斜线很笨拙, 正式写作最好避免: rapid/timely 让读者猜到底是哪个, 选定一个 (timely); If/when 也是, 想清楚了选 When. X and/or Y 几乎总能换成 X and Y、X or Y 或 X, Y, or both 三者之一.

注意: 英语的 or 本身通常是可兼的 (inclusive), 「X or Y」已经包括两者都有的情况; 要强调都有时才写 X, Y, or both.

Slide 17

Slashes

Exceptionally, slashes are unobjectionable when used to separate lines of poetry or song lyrics—e.g.:

Do I contradict myself? / Very well then I contradict myself, / (I am large, I contain multitudes.)1

1 Walt Whitman, Leaves of Grass, 1855.

例外: 在正文里引用诗歌或歌词时, 用斜线 (两边加空格) 分隔诗行, 这是没有问题的. 例子是惠特曼《草叶集》里《自我之歌》的名句: 「我自相矛盾吗? / 那好吧, 我自相矛盾, / (我辽阔博大, 我包罗万象.)」

这几行的措辞 (括号、逗号) 是《草叶集》后来版本 (1881–1892 年) 的文本; 1855 年第一版的写法不同 (用一串点代替逗号和括号, 「Very well then.... I contradict myself; / I am large.... I contain multitudes.」). 严格的引用应该让引文和所注明的版本一致.

撇号与引号
Apostrophes · Quotation Marks

Slide 18

Apostrophes

Apostrophes (’) are used within abbreviations as in it’s, didn’t, and rock ’n’ roll. But such forms are usually spelled out in formal English: it is, did not, rock and roll.

Apostrophes are also used to form the possessive—e.g., writer’s, writers’.

Beware of the grocer’s apostrophe. The plural of tomato is tomatoes, not tomato’s.

撇号用在缩约形式里: it’s、didn’t、rock ’n’ roll (注意 ’n’ 两边的撇号都是右撇号形状, 因为都表示省略). 正式英语里通常写全: it is、did not、rock and roll. 撇号也用来构成所有格: writer’s (一位作者的), writers’ (多位作者的). 小心「杂货店撇号」: 杂货店的价目牌常把复数写成 tomato’s, 其实复数就是 tomatoes, 不加撇号. 同理 the 1960s、PDFs、CPUs 都不加撇号.

Slide 19

Quotation Marks

Quotation marks (“ ”) are used mainly for identifying quoted text—e.g.:

It was Alfred Wainwright who said that “there’s no such thing as bad weather, only unsuitable clothing.”

For longer quotes (above about 30 words), a displayed block is more elegant.

Quotation marks are also used to indicate that a phrase is some tag or someone else’s terminology—e.g.:

In Table 7, the “System” column is sorted alphabetically.
She kept reminding us about the “curse of knowledge” in academia.

In nested quotations, double (“ ”) and single (‘ ’) quotation marks alternate—e.g.:

He said, “She kept reminding us about the ‘curse of knowledge’ in academia.”

In British English, double and single quotation marks are exchanged.

引号主要标明引文: 「Alfred Wainwright (英国徒步作家) 说过: 没有坏天气, 只有不合适的衣服.」引文融入自己的句子 (said that …) 时, 首字母小写, 句号放在引号里 (美式). 长引文 (约 30 词以上) 用单独的引文块更优雅.

引号也用来标明一个短语是某种标签 (表格的「System」列) 或别人的术语 (「知识的诅咒」). 嵌套引文里双引号和单引号交替: 美式外双内单, 英式正好相反 (外单内双).

Slide 20

Quotation Marks

Quotation marks are also used with informal expressions or slang, although this is not recommended—e.g.:

The λ-calculus hits a “sweet spot” on the language design continuum.

Instead, either be brave and omit the quotation marks or use another expression.

Quotation marks are used for phrases that should not be taken literally. In general, it is preferable to use phrases that can be taken literally. Compare:

Inconveniently, the “theorem” is not provable.

Inconveniently, the putative theorem is not provable.

Quotation marks surround the titles of “small works” such as papers, articles, songs, and poems, in conjunction with title case—e.g., “Local Type Inference.”

Finally, do not use quotation marks for emphasis. Use italics or bold.

引号也被用于非正式说法或俚语 (λ 演算正好处在语言设计连续谱上的「甜蜜点」), 但不推荐: 要么大胆去掉引号, 要么换个说法. 引号还用于不应按字面理解的短语 (所谓的「定理」), 一般更好的做法是用能按字面理解的词: the putative theorem (假定的定理). 引号用于「小作品」的标题 (论文、文章、歌曲、诗歌), 配合 title case; 书和期刊等「大作品」用斜体 (见 STE 01 的书目). 最后, 不要用引号表示强调, 用斜体或粗体; 引号表示强调时读者会以为你在讽刺.

圆括号与省略号
Parentheses · Ellipses

Slide 21

Parentheses

Parentheses (( )) (also called “brackets” in British English) are used to indicate parenthetical phrases.

The text should be readable with and without the parenthesized content, and punctuation should be logical—e.g.:

For us (and most Python programmers), operator chaining is subtle.
For us and most Python programmers, operator chaining is subtle.
For us, operator chaining is subtle.

Entire sentences, including the period, can be in parentheses. This is usually less distracting than footnotes—e.g.:

. . . of the array. (The [ ] operator returns the element at a given index.) This definition . . .

圆括号 (英式英语叫 brackets) 标明插入语. 括号里的内容去掉或保留, 文本都应该能读通, 标点也要合逻辑. 例句三种形式都通: 带括号、把括号去掉保留内容、连内容一起去掉. 注意逗号放在右括号后面, 因为逗号属于外面的句子. 整句话 (连同句号) 也可以放在括号里, 这时句号在括号里面; 这通常比脚注更不打扰阅读.

例句里的 operator chaining 指 Python 的比较链 (a < b < c 等于 a < b and b < c), 确实容易误解.

Slide 22

Parentheses

Parentheses are also used to introduce abbreviations—e.g.:

This pattern is called model–view–controller (MVC).
This project has received funding from the European Research Council (ERC).

In addition, parentheses are used to establish conventions. In a scientific paper or thesis, a (well-typed) program usually means “a well-typed program, and from now on all programs will be well typed unless noted otherwise.”

Avoid clusmy devices such as file(s) and police(wo)man.

Generally avoid nested parentheses. Compare:

Metis relies on six derivation rules (Hurd (2003)).

Metis relies on six derivation rules (Hurd 2003).

圆括号还用来引入缩写: model–view–controller (MVC), 注意三个对等的名词用 en dash 连接. 括号还用来建立约定: 论文里写 a (well-typed) program, 意思是「一个良类型的程序; 从现在起, 除非另说, 所有程序都是良类型的」. 避免笨拙的写法如 file(s)、police(wo)man (改用复数 files 或 police officer). 一般避免嵌套括号: (Hurd (2003)) 改成 (Hurd 2003).

注意 well-typed 和 well typed: 前者作定语带连字符, 后者作表语不带 (are well typed), 和 STE 01 讲过的规则一致.

原文笔误: 「clusmy devices」应为 clumsy.

Slide 23

Ellipses

Ellipses ( . . . ) can be used to indicate the omission of material in quotations. For example,

“Point-to-point reachability is arguably the simplest model-checking; property it asks whether a program can reach a given target state from a given initial state.”1

can be shortened to

“Point-to-point reachability . . . asks whether a program can reach a given target state from a given initial state.”

1 Julian Müllner, Marcel Moosbrugger, and Laura Kovács, “Strong Invariants are Hard: On the Hardness of Strongest Polynomial Invariants for (Probabilistic) Programs,” Proceedings of the ACM on Programming Languages 8(POPL), pp. 882–910, 2024.

省略号在引文里表示省略了部分内容. 例子: 「点到点可达性可以说是最简单的模型检验性质; 它问一个程序能否从给定的初始状态到达给定的目标状态.」缩短后: 「点到点可达性 . . . 问一个程序能否……」. 省略号用三个间隔的点 (LaTeX 里是 \ldots), 前后加空格.

原文笔误: 引文里分号放错了位置: 「the simplest model-checking; property it asks」应为 the simplest model-checking property; it asks (分号在 property 后面, 连接两个完整的句子). 下一页引用同一句话时写的就是「the simplest model-checking property」, 可以对照.

Slide 24

Ellipses

Ellipses are useful when you omit words in the middle of a quotation. As a rule, there is no need to put ellipses at the beginning or end of a quotation. This is suboptimal:

This property “. . . is arguably the simplest model-checking property . . . ” according to Müllner et al.

In informal writing, ellipses are used to denote hesitation, suspense, or a trailing thought. In formal writing, there is no defense for sentences such as

If only we had . . . well, now we have to live with the consequences.

Finally, ellipses are used in mathematics for finite sequences (e.g., x1​,…,xn​) and infinite sequences (e.g., x1​,x2​,…). If the ellipsis coincides with the end of the sentence, the result is four evenly spaced periods—e.g.:

The process gives rise to a sequence of time stamps t0​,t1​,t2​,… .

省略号用于省略引文中间的词. 一般不需要在引文开头或结尾加省略号 (读者知道引文前后还有别的内容), 所以第一个例子是次优的. 非正式写作里省略号表示犹豫、悬念或言犹未尽, 正式写作里不能这样用. 数学里省略号表示有限序列 x1​,…,xn​ 和无限序列 x1​,x2​,…; 省略号恰好在句末时, 结果是四个等距的点 (三个省略点加一个句号).
