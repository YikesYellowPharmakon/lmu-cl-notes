Our approach to confidence intervals in the previous reading was a combination of standardized statistics and hypothesis testing. Today we will consider each of these perspectives separately, as well as introduce a third formal viewpoint. Each provides its own insight.

  1. Standardized statistic. Most confidence intervals are based on standardized statistics with known distributions like z, t or χ2. This provides a straightforward way to construct and interpret confidence intervals as a point estimate plus or minus some error.

标准化统计量视角最直接: 已知 z,t,χ2 的分布后, 就能把点估计写成「中心 ± 误差」, 并用代数得到参数区间.

  1. Hypothesis testing. Confidence intervals may also be constructed from hypothesis tests. In cases where we don’t have a standardized statistic this method will still work. It agrees with the standardized statistic approach in cases where they both apply. This view connects the notions of significance level α for hypothesis testing and confidence level 1−α for confidence intervals; we will see that in both cases α is the probability of making a ‘type 1’ error. This gives some insight into the use of the word confidence. This view also helps to emphasize the frequentist nature of confidence intervals.

假设检验视角把 1−α 区间看成所有不被拒绝的参数值. 它把检验的显著性水平 α 与区间的错误率 α 联系起来, 也突出频率派解释.

  1. Formal. The formal definition of confidence intervals is perfectly precise and general. In a mathematical sense it gives insight into the inner workings of confidence intervals. 由标准化统计量构造区间
Confidence Intervals via Standardized Statistics

3 标准化统计量的共同思路
3 Confidence intervals via standardized statistics

Page 2

The strategy here is essentially the same as in the previous reading. Assuming normal data we have what we called standardized statistics like the standardized mean, Studentized mean, and standardized variance. These statistics have well known distributions which depend on hypothesized values of μ and σ. We then use algebra to produce confidence intervals for μ or σ.

Don’t let the algebraic details distract you from the essentially simple idea underlying confidence intervals: we start with a standardized statistic (e.g., z, t or χ2) and use some algebra to get an interval that depends only on the data and known parameters.

共同思路是: 先找分布已知的标准化统计量, 保留中间 1−α 的概率, 再解不等式, 使目标参数落在中央.

最后区间只能依赖数据和已知量, 不能含未知的真参数. 代数反转不会改变概率条件, 也不会把固定参数变成随机变量.

这里的「标准化」不是把数据随便变成 z 或 t, 而是利用已知分布把概率问题转成距离问题. 区间端点必须能由样本算出, 才有资格成为区间统计量.

最后得到的概率仍以指定真值为条件, 描述随机区间的覆盖表现. 它并不是在区间已经算出后, 再给参数分配一个后验分布.

3.1 已知标准差的 z 区间
3.1 z-confidence intervals for the mean: normal data with known standard deviation

Page 2

z-confidence intervals for the mean of normal data are based on the standardized mean, i.e. the z-statistic. We start with n independent normal samples x1​,x2​,…,xn​∼N(μ,σ2).

We assume that μ is the unknown parameter of interest and σ is known. Notationally, let’s write the (unknown) true value of μ as μ0​. We know that the standardized mean is standard normal:

z=σ/n​xˉ−μ0​​∼N(0,1).

单个数据服从 N(μ,σ2), 样本均值的标准误为 σ/n​. 已知 σ 后, 标准化均值在 μ=μ0​ 时服从标准正态.

Page 2

For the standard normal critical value zα/2​ we have: P(−zα/2​<Z<zα/2​)=1−α. Thus,

P(−zα/2​<σ/n​xˉ−μ​<zα/2​∣μ=μ0​)=1−α.

A little bit of algebra puts this in the form of an interval around μ:

P(xˉ−zα/2​⋅n​σ​<μ<xˉ+zα/2​⋅n​σ​∣μ=μ0​)=1−α.

We can emphasize that the interval depends only on the statistic xˉ and the known value σ by writing this as

P([xˉ−zα/2​⋅n​σ​,xˉ+zα/2​⋅n​σ​] contains μ∣μ=μ0​)=1−α.

This is the (1−α) z-confidence interval for μ. We often write it using the shorthand xˉ±zα/2​σ/n​. Think of it as xˉ± error.

Make sure you notice that the probabilities are conditioned on μ=μ0​. As with all frequentist statistics, we have to fix hypothesized values of the parameters in order to compute probabilities.

核心不等式是 ∣xˉ−μ0​∣<zα/2​σ/n​. 把它改写成 μ 所在的区间, 就得到以 xˉ 为中心的 z 区间.

概率的条件仍是 μ=μ0​. 代数反转只改变不等式的写法, 不会把固定参数变成随机变量.

推导可分三步: 在标准正态中央保留 1−α 的概率; 乘均值标准误得到 ∣xˉ−μ0​∣ 的上界; 再把 μ0​ 写成区间中央的参数. 最终半径为 zα/2​σ/n​.

这里 μ0​ 只是用来固定当前分布的值. 区间由 xˉ 与已知 σ 计算, 不含未知参数, 因此仍是一个区间统计量.

最终形式可记作「点估计 ± 临界值 × 标准误」. 真正需要理解的是每一项的来源, 而不是只背 1.96.

3.2 未知标准差的 t 区间
3.2 t-confidence intervals for the mean: normal data with unknown mean and standard deviation

Page 3

t-confidence intervals for the mean of normal data are based on the Studentized mean, i.e. the t-statistic.

Again we have x1​,x2​,…,xn​∼N(μ,σ2), but now we assume both μ and σ are unknown. As we did above, let’s write the (unknown) true value of μ as μ0​. We know that the Studentized mean follows a Student t distribution with n−1 degrees of freedom. That is,

t=s/n​xˉ−μ0​​∼t(n−1),

where s2 is the sample variance.

未知 σ 时用样本标准差 s, 分母 s/n​ 也随样本变化. 学生化均值因此服从 t(n−1), 而不是标准正态.

Page 3

Now all we have to do is replace the standardized mean by the Studentized mean and the same logic we used for z gives us the t-confidence interval: start with

P(−tα/2​<s/n​xˉ−μ​<tα/2​∣μ=μ0​)=1−α.

A little bit of algebra isolates μ in the middle of an interval:

P(xˉ−tα/2​⋅n​s​<μ<xˉ+tα/2​⋅n​s​∣μ=μ0​)=1−α.

We can emphasize that the interval depends only on the statistics xˉ and s by writing this as

P([xˉ−tα/2​⋅n​s​,xˉ+tα/2​⋅n​s​] contains μ∣μ=μ0​)=1−α.

This is the (1−α) t-confidence interval for μ. We often write it using the shorthand xˉ±tα/2​s/n​. Think of it as xˉ± error.

未知 σ 时, 用样本标准差 s 替换它, 同时把标准正态临界值换成自由度 n−1 的 t 临界值.

**Studentized mean 学生化均值**的分母含随机变量 s, 所以它不再服从标准正态分布. 小样本时 t 区间通常比误用 z 的区间更宽.

自由度 n−1 来自样本方差估计. 小样本时 t 尾部厚, 要达到相同覆盖率必须取更大的临界值; 大样本时 t 与 z 的差别逐渐缩小.

构造过程与 z 完全一样, 只是标准误由 s/n​ 估计. 因为 s 也会随机变化, 不能一边使用 t 临界值, 一边仍把 s 当成已知常数来解释.

3.3 方差的卡方区间
3.3 Chi-square confidence intervals for variance: normal data with unknown mean and standard deviation

Pages 3–4

You guessed it: χ2-confidence intervals for the variance of normal data are based on the standardized variance, i.e. the χ2-statistic. We follow the same logic as above to get a χ2-confidence interval for σ2. Because this is the third time through it we’ll move a little more quickly.

We assume we have n independent normal samples: x1​,x2​,…,xn​∼N(μ,σ2). We assume that μ and σ are both unknown and write the (unknown) true value of σ as σ0​. The standardized variance is

X2=σ02​(n−1)s2​∼χ2(n−1).

We know that the X2 statistic follows a χ2 distribution with n−1 degrees of freedom.

方差的标准化统计量是 (n−1)s2/σ02​, 在正态样本下服从 χ2(n−1). 这次保留中间概率后, 要对 σ2 解不等式.

Page 3

For Z and t we used, without comment, the symmetry of the distributions to replace z1−α/2​ by −zα/2​ and t1−α/2​ by −tα/2​. Because the χ2 distribution is not symmetric we need to be explicit about the critical values on both the left and the right. That is,

P(c1−α/2​<X2<cα/2​)=1−α,

where cα/2​ and c1−α/2​ are right tail critical values. Thus,

P(c1−α/2​<σ02​(n−1)s2​<cα/2​∣σ=σ0​)=1−α.

A little bit of algebra puts this in the form of an interval around σ2:

P(cα/2​(n−1)s2​<σ2<c1−α/2​(n−1)s2​∣σ=σ0​)=1−α.

We can emphasize that the interval depends only on the statistic s2 by writing this as

P([cα/2​(n−1)s2​,c1−α/2​(n−1)s2​] contains σ2∣σ=σ0​)=1−α.

This is the (1−α) χ2-confidence interval for σ2.

对 σ2 解不等式时, 上下端点的临界值会交换位置: 较大的右尾临界值 cα/2​ 在左端分母, 较小的 c1−α/2​ 在右端分母.

易错:χ2 分布不对称, 不能把一个临界值简单写成另一个的相反数. 这里估计的是方差 σ2, 不是标准差 σ.

取倒数时三个正项的大小关系全部反转, 所以左端反而用较大的 cα/2​. 这也使方差区间一般不关于 s2 对称.

与 z,t 不同, 卡方分布右偏, 所以两端临界值必须分别查找, 不能用正负对称的写法. 最后若需要 σ 的区间, 再对两个正端点开平方.

由假设检验构造区间
Confidence Intervals via Hypothesis Testing

4 检验与置信区间
4 Confidence intervals via hypothesis testing

Page 4

Suppose we have data drawn from a distribution with a parameter θ whose value is unknown. A significance test for the value θ has the following short description.

  1. Set the null hypothesis H0​:θ=θ0​ for some special value θ0​, e.g. we often have H0​:θ=0.
  2. Use the data to compute the value of a test statistic, call it x.
  3. If x is far enough into the tail of the null distribution (the distribution assuming the null hypothesis) then we reject H0​.

In the case where there is no special value to test we may still want to estimate θ. This is the reverse of significance testing; rather than seeing if we should reject a specific value of θ because it doesn’t fit the data we want to find the range of values of θ that do, in some sense, fit the data. This gives us the following definitions.

检验先固定 θ0​, 看统计量是否落入零分布尾部. 构造区间则反过来, 固定观测统计量, 找出所有不会拒绝它的参数值.

Page 4

Definition. Given a value x of the test statistic, the (1−α) confidence interval contains all values θ0​ which are not rejected (at significance level α) when they are the null hypothesis.

Definition. A type 1 CI error occurs when the confidence interval does not contain the true value of θ.

For a (1−α) confidence interval the type 1 CI error rate is α.

固定 θ0​ 时, 非拒绝域是检验统计量 x 的一组取值. 固定观测到的 x 后, 把所有不拒绝该 x 的 θ0​ 收集起来, 才得到参数的置信区间.

**type 1 CI error 第一类置信区间错误**就是随机区间没有覆盖真值. 精确满足覆盖率定义时错误率为 α; 离散或保守构造中可能小于 α.

这里的事件方向很重要: 真值固定, 区间随数据变化. 当某个真值对应的数据落入其拒绝域时, 该真值就不会被收进区间, 于是发生 CI 错误.

置信区间中的 θ0​ 是一系列候选原假设, 不是给这些候选值本身分配概率. 构造过程仍然完全使用各假设下的似然.

The first problem we confront is how exactly to choose the rejection region. We used two rules:

  1. The total probabilitiy of the rejection region, i.e. the significance, should be less than or equal to 0.1. (Since we have a discrete distribution it is impossible to make the significance exactly 0.1.)
  2. We build the rejection region by choosing values of x one at a time, always picking the unused value with the smallest probability. We stop when the next value would make the significance more that 0.1.

There are other ways to choose the rejection region which would result in slight differences. Our method is one reasonable way.

Table 2 shows the rejection (orange) and non-rejection (blue) regions for θ=1.0. This is a special case because most of the probabilities in this row are 0.0. We’ll move right on to the next table and step through the process for that.

拒绝域算法分两步: 先限制橙色概率总和不超过 0.1, 再按当前行概率从小到大逐格加入, 直到下一格会超额.

Page 7

In Table 3, let’s walk through the steps used to find these regions for θ=0.9.

  • The smallest probability is when x=0, so x=0 is in the rejection region.
  • The next smallest is when x=1, so x=1 is in the rejection region.
  • We continue with x=2,…,8. At this point the total probability in the rejection region is 0.026.
  • The next smallest probability is when x=9. Adding this probability (0.09) to 0.026 would put the total probability over 0.1. So we leave x=9 out of the rejection region and stop the process.

对 θ=0.9, 先加入 x=0,…,8, 总概率为 0.026. 再加入 x=9 会超过 0.1, 所以停止, 保持实际显著性不超过上限.

这个算法优先把最不可能的观测放入拒绝域, 但总概率不能超过 0.1. 对 θ=0.9, 加入 x=9 会超额, 所以按规则停止.

这个特殊行说明, 只要数据在模型下不可能出现, 即使橙色区域覆盖很多横轴位置, 也不会产生第一类错误. 显著性看概率质量, 不看格子数量.

显示成 0.00 的小概率格和真正零概率格要分开处理. 前者在实际计算中仍贡献概率, 后者才会让某行显著性精确为 0.

离散分布还有一个重要后果: 拒绝域只能整格加入, 显著性不能连续调到任意指定值. 所以表中通常得到「至多 0.1」而不是恰好 0.1, 对应的区间也就具有至少 90% 的覆盖率.

如果为了凑数随意再加一个中心格, 虽然可能更接近 0.1, 却会破坏备择方向与「极端结果」的含义. 这里按概率从小到大加格, 就是在控制错误率的同时保留合理拒绝域.

右侧 significance 是橙色格概率的总和, 不是蓝色非拒绝域的概率. 蓝色则留给与该 H0​ 相容的观测值.

置信区间的形式定义
Formal View of Confidence Intervals

5 形式视角
5 Formal view of confidence intervals

Page 8

Recall: An interval statistic is an interval Ix​ computed from data x. An interval is determined by its lower and upper bounds, and these are random because x is random.

We suppose that x is drawn from a distribution with pdf f(x∣θ) where the parameter θ is unknown.

Definition: A (1−α) confidence interval for θ is an interval statistic Ix​ such that

P(Ix​ contains θ0​∣θ=θ0​)=1−α

for all possible values of θ0​.

形式定义要求随机区间在真值为 θ0​ 的条件下以 1−α 的概率包含 θ0​, 而且必须对所有可能的 θ0​ 同时成立.

Page 8

We wish this was simpler, but a definition is a definition and this definition is one way to weigh the evidence provided by the data x. Let’s unpack it a bit.

The confidence level of an interval statistic is a probability concerning a random interval and a hypothesized value θ0​ for the unknown parameter. Precisely, it is the probability that the random interval Ix​ (computed from random data x) contains the value θ0​, given that the model parameter truly is θ0​. Since the true value of θ is unknown, the frequentist statistician defines 95% confidence intervals so that the 0.95 probability is valid no matter which hypothesized value of the parameter is actually true.

形式定义的关键是「对所有可能的 θ0​ 都成立」. 不能只在某个方便的参数值下有 95% 覆盖率, 就把构造方法称为普遍的 95% 置信区间.

定义本身也不保证区间短、形状合理或便于计算. 它只约束覆盖概率, 所以还需要标准化统计量或检验构造来得到有用的区间.

例如一些人为构造的区间虽然能满足覆盖率, 却可能非常宽或不连续, 没有实际估计价值. 形式定义保证的是表现, 不自动保证好用.

定义中的概率也不是 P(θ0​∈Ix​) 这样一个脱离条件的后验表达. 它完整写成 P(Ix​ contains θ0​∣θ=θ0​): 先固定真值, 再看随机区间是否覆盖.

「对所有 θ0​ 成立」还意味着不能只挑一个有利参数值证明覆盖率. 一套合格的构造必须把整个参数空间都纳入考虑.

形式定义中的 Ix​ 仍由数据计算. 只有先说明区间如何生成, 才能讨论它的覆盖率; 单给一个固定区间而没有构造规则, 不能直接判断它是不是 95% 置信区间.

=: z, t, χ2

≈: large sample confidence interval

等号、至少和近似对应三类区间. 正态模型下的 z,t,χ2 构造有精确覆盖率; 保守比例区间至少达到标称水平; 大样本区间只在近似意义下达到.

精确等号来自已知分布, 至少来自保守或离散构造, 近似来自大样本定理. 它们都是频率派陈述, 但成立条件不同.

Confidence intervals are a frequentist notion, and as we’ve repeated many times, frequentists don’t assign probabilities to hypotheses, e.g., to the value of an unknown parameter. Rather they compute likelihoods; that is, probabilities about data or associated statistics given a hypothesis (note the condition θ=θ0​ in the formal view of confidence intervals). Note that the construction of confidence intervals proceeds entirely from the full likelihood table.

In contrast Bayesian posterior probability intervals are truly the probability that the value of the unknown parameter lies in the reported range. We add the usual caveat that this depends on the specific choice of a (possibly subjective) Bayesian prior.

频率派计算给定假设时数据或统计量的概率; 贝叶斯后验区间则直接给出参数在区间内的后验概率. 后者依赖所选先验.

  2. Given data x, they give an interval I(x) specifying a range of values for θ.
  3. They come with a number (say 0.95) that is the probability of something.

两类区间的表面共同点是: 都有数据模型、都会给出参数区间、都可能附带 0.95. 但这个数在两种框架里描述的不是同一种概率.

7 疾病检测中的条件方向
7 Misinterpreting confidence intervals

Page 9

It is very tempting to think that given a 95% confidence interval for, say, the mean, the probability that the true mean is in the confidence interval is 95%.

We know this can’t be true because the value of the mean can only be hypothesized and Frequentists don’t assign probabilities to hypotheses. To be more concrete, if the mean is θ and the confidence interval is [45, 55] then the statement 45≤θ≤55 is a hypothesis, so asking for the probability that 45≤θ≤55, is asking for the probability of a hypothesis.

The mistake is subtle and hard to wrap your mind around. It boils down to a question of what is being randomly sampled. Here is an attempt to explain the issue.

误读的关键不是代数, 而是把「随机区间」和「随机参数」混在一起. 固定区间 [45,55] 与命题 45≤θ≤55 已经给出后, 频率派不会给这个命题分配概率.

Page 9

First, consider a test for a disease. Assume a person is given the test. Let T+ be a positive test and D+ be that they have the disease. Assume the test is 95% accurate, i.e. P(T+∣D+)=0.95. We know (base rate fallacy) that this does not imply that P(D+∣T+)=0.95.

检验准确率 P(T+∣D+)=0.95 不等于阳性后患病概率 P(D+∣T+)=0.95. 后者还依赖患病率先验.

Page 9

Let’s look at this from a different angle: Implicit in P(T+∣D+)=0.95 is the following experiment: Draw a random person from the set of all people with the disease and give them the test. Then 95% will test positive. That is, the population sampled is all people with the disease and the event considered is that the chosen person in that population tests positive.

第一个实验先固定「患病者」这个总体, 再看其中 95% 检测阳性. 这对应准确率 P(T+∣D+), 不是阳性后的患病概率.

Page 9

For P(D+∣T+), the experiment is to draw a random person from the set of all people who tested positive. The probability is the fraction who have the disease. That is, the population sampled is all people who test positive and the event considered is that the chosen person in that population has the disease.

We can’t expect P(T+∣D+) and P(D+∣T+) to be the same, since we’re sampling from different populations and looking at different events. The probability P(D+∣T+) can be computed using Bayes’ theorem from the (prior) probability P(D+) and the likelihoods P(T+∣D+),P(T+∣D−).

疾病检测例子中, P(T+∣D+) 与 P(D+∣T+) 把条件方向反过来了. 前者在患病总体中看检测结果, 后者在阳性总体中看患病比例, 两者不需要相等.

置信区间也有类似的方向差异. 频率派让区间随样本变化, 参数保持固定; 贝叶斯给定区间后, 用先验和后验描述参数的不确定性.

把「抽样总体是谁」写出来, 就能避免只看文字相似便把两个概率混为一谈. 这也是基准率谬误的核心.

在 P(T+∣D+) 中, 先限定所有患病者, 再看检测阳性率; 在 P(D+∣T+) 中, 先限定所有阳性者, 再看其中患病比例. 两个人群的构成不同, 概率当然可以不同.

置信区间同样要先问随机对象: 是反复生成的区间, 还是参数本身. 如果不先回答这个问题, 95% 很容易被错误地贴到当前区间上.

随机区间与随机参数
Random Intervals and Random Parameters

Confidence intervals are a little more abstract, but the analysis is similar. Just as in testing for a disease, the populations sampled will be different. One source of diﬀiculty is that the events are essentially the same in both cases.

Let’s assume we have a distribution with unknown mean θ0​. We generate some data and compute a 95% confidence interval for the mean. The value of 95% comes from the following implied experiment: imagine having run many trials and created a confidence interval for each one. Then, 95% of confidence intervals contain the true mean. In notation,

P(random interval contains θ0​)=0.95.

That is, the random sample is drawn from the set of all confidence intervals generated by our trials. The event in question is that the chosen interval contains the true mean.

频率派的抽样对象是「反复试验产生的许多区间」, 看其中有多少覆盖固定真值. 参数不随这些区间随机变化.

Page 10

What if we run one experiment and generate the 95% confidence interval, call it I. To a Frequentist, the true mean is not random and we have a fixed interval. So, to the Frequentist, it makes no sense to ask about the probability θ0​ is in I.

To a Bayesian, it is fine to consider θ0​ as randomly drawn from a probability distribution –they often interpret it as a description of the uncertainty of our knowledge. So, they can ask for the probability

P(random θ0​ is in a given I).

So, here, the random sample is drawn from the set of possible means and the event considered is that the chosen mean is contained in the given interval.

As in the disease testing example, what population is being randomly sampled is different in the two cases, i.e in the first we have a random interval, in the second we have a random value for the true mean. As noted above, in both cases the event is that the true mean is in the interval.

频率派的实验是反复产生区间, 看其中多大比例覆盖固定真值. 贝叶斯则给定一个区间, 用参数的先验和后验讨论它落入其中的概率.

两种实验的随机对象不同: 一个是随机区间, 一个是随机参数. 即使事件文字都写成「真值在区间内」, 概率含义仍不同.

对一份已经得到的数据, 频率派把区间看作本次实现, 不再给真值分配随机性; 贝叶斯则把该区间固定下来, 用参数后验讨论它.

因此「随机区间覆盖固定真值」与「随机参数落入固定区间」是两个不同实验. 混淆它们就会把频率派置信水平误读成后验概率.

后验概率怎样计算
Bayesian Computation

We finish by noting that P(random θ0​ is in I) can be computed using Bayes’ theorem and depends on the prior distribution for the true mean and the likelihoods that each mean will generate the given confidence interval. The formula is a little unwieldy. Here it is.

  • Call the interval I and the true mean θ0​.
  • Call the data I. This is a shorthand for the data that the interval I is based on.
  • Let p(θ) be the prior probability that θ0​=θ.
  • The likelihood f(I∣θ) is the probability (or density) that the experiment would produce the interval I given θ0​=θ.
  • Let p(θ∣I) be the posterior probability that θ0​=θ. This is the updated probability found using the Bayes’ theorem.

Bayes’ theorem gives us

p(θ∣I)=f(I)f(I∣θ)p(θ)​,f(I)=θ∑​f(I∣θ)p(θ).

So we have, P(θ0​∈I∣I)=∑θ in I​p(θ∣I). (As usual, if θ has a continuous range of values, then the sums will be replaced by integrals.)

这里 I 既表示所得区间, 也作为产生该区间的数据摘要. 后验计算仍按「先验 × 似然, 再归一化」进行, 最后把区间内所有参数值的后验概率相加.

这一步明确需要 p(θ). 没有先验, 就不能从频率派置信水平直接推出 P(θ0​∈I∣I)=0.95.

后验计算把候选参数值按先验和似然重新加权, 再对 I 内的值求和或积分. 因此 0.95 不能脱离先验直接搬进贝叶斯框架.

这也是为什么后验区间可以说「参数在区间内的概率是多少」: 它计算的是 p(θ∣I) 在 I 上的总质量, 而不是某个构造规则的长期覆盖率.

这里 f(I∣θ) 也不是单个观测值的似然, 而是整个实验产生该区间 I 的概率或密度. 它概括了数据生成与区间构造的完整过程.

贝叶斯计算时, 每个候选 θ 都要能说明「为什么会得到这个 I」. 再把 f(I∣θ)p(θ) 归一化, 最后对区间内的 θ 求和或积分.

所以后验概率不是把 0.95 换个名字直接搬过来, 而是由先验和产生区间的似然共同决定.

这也说明为什么频率派的置信水平不能直接给贝叶斯答案: 后者还需要先验分布, 以及对整个区间生成过程的建模.

The proof of Formula 1 will rely on the following fact.

Fact. The standard deviation of a Bernoulli(θ) distribution is at most 0.5.

Proof of fact: Let’s denote this standard deviation by σθ​ to emphasize its dependence on θ. The variance is then σθ2​=θ(1−θ). It’s easy to see using calculus or by graphing this parabola that the maximum occurs when θ=1/2. Therefore the maximum variance is 1/4, which implies that the standard deviation σp​ is less the 1/4​=1/2.

Proof of formula (1). The proof relies on the central limit theorem which says that (for large n) the distribution of xˉ is approximately normal with mean θ and standard deviation σθ​/n​. For normal data we have the (1−α) z-confidence interval xˉ±zα/2​σθ​/n​.

The trick now is to replace σθ​ by 1/2: since σθ​≤1/2 the resulting interval around xˉ is always at least as wide as the interval using xˉ±σθ​/n​. A wider interval is more likely to contain the true value of θ so we have a ‘conservative’ (1−α) confidence interval for θ.

Again, we call this conservative because 1/(2n​) overestimates the standard deviation of xˉ, resulting in a wider interval than is necessary to achieve a (1−α) confidence level.

新闻报道中的误差范围
3.3 How political polls are reported

Pages 2–3

Political polls are often reported as a value with a margin-of-error. For example you might hear 52% favor candidate A with a margin-of-error of ±5%.

The actual precise meaning of this is if θ is the proportion of the population that supports A then the point estimate for θ is 52% and the 95% confidence interval is 52% ± 5%.

Notice that reporters of polls in the news do not mention the 95% confidence. You just have to know that that’s what pollsters do.

The 95% rule-of-thumb confidence interval.

Recall that the (1−α) conservative normal confidence interval is xˉ±zα/2​/(2n​).

If we use the standard approximation z0.025​=2 (instead of 1.96) we get the rule-of-thumb 95% confidence interval for θ: xˉ±1/n​.

Other binomial proportion confidence intervals

There are many methods of producing confidence intervals for the proportion p of a binomial(n,p) distribution. For a number of other common approaches, see: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binomial_proportion_confidence_interval

**margin of error 误差范围**是区间的半径, 不是整个区间的宽度. 新闻中的「52% ±5%」通常指点估计 52%, 95% 区间半径 5 个百分点.

经验公式把 1.96 近似为 2, 所以 z0.025​/(2n​)≈1/n​. 样本量扩大 100 倍, 误差范围约缩小到原来的十分之一.

媒体常省略「95%」是因为惯例默认这一水平, 不表示误差范围具有百分之百的确定性. 它仍是近似置信区间, 而且还可能不含非抽样误差.

「半径」与「全宽」也要分清: ±5% 表示半径为 5 个百分点, 全宽则是 10 个百分点. 写区间端点时分别在点估计两侧加减这个半径.

一般分布的大样本置信区间
Large Sample Confidence Intervals

4 大样本区间
4 Large sample confidence intervals

Pages 3–4

One typical goal in statistics is to estimate the mean of a distribution. When the data follows a normal distribution we could use confidence intervals based on standardized statistics to estimate the mean.

But suppose the data x1​,x2​,…,xn​ is drawn from a distribution with pmf or pdf f(x) that may not be normal or even parametric. If the distribution has finite mean and variance and if n is suﬀiciently large, then the following version of the central limit theorem shows we can still use a standardized statistic.

Central Limit Theorem: For large n, the sampling distribution of the studentized mean is approximately standard normal: (xˉ−μ)/(s/n​)≈N(0,1).

So for large n the (1−α) confidence interval for μ is approximately [xˉ−s/n​⋅zα/2​,xˉ+s/n​⋅zα/2​] where zα/2​ is the α/2 critical value for N(0,1). This is called the large sample confidence interval.

这里不要求原始数据正态, 只要求分布有有限均值和方差, 并且样本量足够大. 中心极限定理保证学生化均值的抽样分布近似标准正态.

公式外观像 t 区间, 但临界值用 zα/2​, 覆盖率也是近似的. 「大样本」没有适用于所有分布的统一阈值, 偏态和重尾会影响近似速度.

有限均值和方差是中心极限定理的重要条件. 若方差不存在, 样本均值可能始终无法近似正态, 不能机械增加 n 后仍套这条公式.

「大样本」还要结合分布形状判断. 对称、轻尾分布通常较快接近正态, 强偏态或重尾分布需要更多样本, 因此不能把 30 或 50 当作万能界线.

标称水平来自公式中的 1−α, 实际覆盖率则要看这个近似在特定分布和样本量下的表现. 两者不一致时, 应承认近似误差, 而不是把标称值当成精确结果.
