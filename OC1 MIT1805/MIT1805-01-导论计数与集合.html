1 Probability vs. Statistics

In this introduction we will preview what we will be studying in 18.05. Don’t worry if many of the terms are unfamiliar, they will be explained as the course proceeds.

Probability and statistics are deeply connected because all statistical statements are at bottom statements about probability. Despite this the two sometimes feel like very different subjects. Probability is logically self-contained; there are a few rules and answers all follow logically from the rules, though computations can be tricky. In statistics we apply probability to draw conclusions from data. This can be messy and usually involves as much art as science.

这份导论先预览 18.05 会学什么, 暂时遇到生词不用担心. Probability 概率论和 Statistics 统计学紧密相连, 因为统计结论归根到底都要用概率来说明. 但两者的研究方式不同.

概率论先把随机规则给定, 再从这些规则逻辑地推出答案, 难点通常在计算. 统计学则要用概率从数据中作结论. 数据怎么收、模型怎么选、结论怎么表达都可能影响结果, 所以统计不只是代公式, 还需要判断.

Page 1

Probability example. You have a fair coin (equal probability of heads or tails). You will toss it 100 times. What is the probability of 60 or more heads? There is only one answer (about 0.028444) and we will learn how to compute it.

概率论的例子是: 已知硬币公平, 也就是正反面概率相等, 抛 100 次, 问至少出现 60 次正面的概率. 在这个模型已经明确的前提下, 答案唯一, 约为 0.028444.

这里的问题是「从机制到数据」: 正面概率已知为 0.5, 要算的是这个机制产生某类结果的可能性. 至少 60 次包括 60、61、…、100 次, 不是只算恰好 60 次.

Page 1

Statistics example. You have a coin of unknown provenance. To investigate whether it is fair you toss it 100 times and count the number of heads. Let’s say you count 60 heads. Your job as a statistician is to draw a conclusion (inference) from this data. There are many ways to proceed, both in terms of the form the conclusion takes and the probability computations used to justify the conclusion. In fact, different statisticians might draw different conclusions.

统计学的例子换成一枚来源不明的硬币. 为了研究它是否公平, 抛 100 次后观察到 60 次正面. 现在要根据这份数据作 inference 推断.

这里的问题是「从数据到机制」: 结果已经看见, 未知的是产生结果的随机机制. 可以报告正面概率的估计值, 也可以检验「硬币公平」这个说法, 还可以给出未知概率的范围. 不同方法依赖的假设和概率计算不同, 因此结论的形式也可能不同, 不能只凭 60/100 就宣布硬币不公平.

Page 1

Note that in the first example the random process is fully known (probability of heads = 0.5). The objective is to find the probability of a certain outcome (at least 60 heads) arising from the random process. In the second example, the outcome is known (60 heads) and the objective is to illuminate the unknown random process (the probability of heads).

讲义再把两个方向对照一遍. 第一题完全知道随机过程: 正面概率是 0.5; 要求的是至少 60 次正面这个结果的概率. 第二题已经知道结果: 60 次正面; 要反过来了解未知的随机过程, 尤其是正面概率.

所以区分概率题和统计题, 先看「什么已知、什么未知」. 已知模型去推数据, 是概率计算; 已知数据去认识模型, 是统计推断.

2 Frequentist vs. Bayesian Interpretations

There are two prominent and sometimes conflicting schools of statistics: Bayesian and frequentist. Their approaches are rooted in differing interpretations of the meaning of probability.

Frequentists say that probability measures the frequency of various outcomes of an experiment. For example, saying a fair coin has a 50% probability of heads means that if we toss it many times then we expect about half the tosses to land heads.

统计学有两个重要、有时意见相左的流派: Bayesian 贝叶斯派和 Frequentist 频率派. 分歧的根源是「概率表示什么」.

频率派把概率看作重复试验中某个结果出现的频率. 公平硬币的正面概率为 50%, 意思是重复抛很多次时, 预期约有一半是正面. 「约有一半」不是说每 100 次必定恰好 50 次, 而是说长期频率会呈现出这个稳定趋势.

Page 2

Bayesians say that probability is an abstract concept that measures a state of knowledge or a degree of belief in a given proposition. In practice Bayesians do not assign a single value for the probability of a coin coming up heads. Rather they consider a range of values each with its own probability of being true.

贝叶斯派把概率理解为对知识状态或命题信念程度的度量. 面对来源不明的硬币, 他们不会只给正面概率指定一个值, 而会考虑一系列候选值, 并表示自己对各个候选值的把握.

设未知的正面概率为 θ, 贝叶斯方法会给 θ 一个概率分布, 再用观察数据更新它. 这不等于说硬币每次抛出时都没有确定的条件概率; 在给定 θ 时, 仍有 P(H∣θ)=θ. 分布表达的是我们对未知 θ 的认识.

Page 2

In 18.05 we will study and compare these approaches. The frequentist approach has long been dominant in fields like biology, medicine, public health and social sciences. The Bayesian approach has enjoyed a resurgence in the era of powerful computers and big data. It is especially useful when incorporating new data into an existing statistical model, for example, when training a speech or face recognition system. Today, statisticians are creating powerful tools by using both approaches in complementary ways.

课程会学习并比较这两种方法. 频率派长期主导生物、医学、公共卫生和社会科学等领域. 随着计算机算力和数据量增长, 贝叶斯方法重新兴盛, 因为它很适合把新数据不断并入已有模型, 例如训练语音识别或人脸识别系统.

现代统计实践可以把两者结合起来用. 它们不是两套互相排斥的算术公式, 而是组织未知量、数据和证据的不同方式; 实际问题中应看哪种假设和回答方式更合适.

3 Applications, Toy Models, and Simulation

Probability and statistics are used widely in the physical sciences, engineering, medicine, the social sciences, the life sciences, economics and computer science. The list of applications is essentially endless: tests of one medical treatment against another (or a placebo), measures of genetic linkage, the search for elementary particles, machine learning for vision or speech, gambling probabilities and strategies, climate modeling, economic forecasting, epidemiology, marketing, googling…We will draw on examples from many of these fields during this course.

概率与统计广泛用于物理、工程、医学、社会科学、生命科学、经济学和计算机科学. 讲义举的例子包括: 比较疗法与安慰剂、度量遗传连锁、寻找基本粒子、视觉或语音机器学习、赌博策略、气候建模、经济预测、流行病学、市场营销和网络搜索.

这些应用外表差异很大, 但都在处理「有随机性的数据」和「不完全确定的结论」. 本课程会借用不同领域的例子, 反复练习同一套概率与推断思想.

Page 2

Given so many exciting applications, you may wonder why we will spend so much time thinking about toy models like coins and dice. By understanding these thoroughly we will develop a good feel for the simple essence inside many complex real-world problems. In fact, the modest coin is a realistic model for any situations with two possible outcomes: success or failure of a treatment, an airplane engine, a bet, or even a class.

既然现实应用这么丰富, 为什么还花时间研究硬币和骰子? 因为它们是 toy model 玩具模型: 刻意删去复杂细节, 把随机机制、计数和推断逻辑清楚地暴露出来. 先把简单模型彻底弄懂, 才能看见复杂问题里的相同结构.

只要关注的结果能分成「成功、失败」两类, 硬币就可能是合理模型, 例如治疗是否成功、发动机是否故障、一次打赌是否赢、一门课是否通过. 但有两个结果还不够, 各次试验是否独立、成功概率是否保持不变, 仍要另外判断.

Page 2

Sometimes a problem is so complicated that the best way to understand it is through computer simulation. Here we use software to run virtual experiments many times in order to estimate probabilities. In this class we will use R for simulation as well as computation and visualization. Don’t worry if you’re new to R; we will teach you all you need to know.

有些问题复杂到很难直接算出答案, 这时可以做 simulation 模拟: 用软件反复进行虚拟试验, 再用结果出现的频率估计概率. 课程会用 R 做模拟、计算和可视化, 不要求事先学过.

模拟既能帮助检查解析计算, 也能处理难以写出闭式答案的问题. 不过模拟结果是近似值, 会随运行次数和随机抽样而变化; 它不是把真正的概率「证明」出来.

2.1 Motivating questions

Example 1. A coin is fair if it comes up heads or tails with equal probability. You flip a fair coin three times. What is the probability that exactly one of the flips results in a head?

Solution: With three flips, we can easily list the eight possible outcomes:

{TTT,TTH,THT,THH,HTT,HTH,HHT,HHH}

例 1 问: 公平硬币抛 3 次, 恰好有 1 次正面的概率是多少? 讲义先列出全部 8 个结果. 每个结果都是长度为 3 的完整序列, H 表示正面, T 表示反面.

每次抛掷有 2 种结果, 3 个位置合起来有 23=8 个序列. 公平硬币使每个位置的正反面等可能, 因此这 8 个完整序列也等可能.

Page 1

Three of these outcomes have exactly one head:

{TTH,THT,HTT}

Since all outcomes are equally probable, we have

P(1 head in 3 flips)=total number of outcomesnumber of outcomes with 1 head​=83​.

Think: Would listing the outcomes be practical with 10 flips?

恰好一次正面的序列只有 TTH,THT,HTT, 分别表示正面出现在第 3、第 2、第 1 次. 目标事件含有 3 个等可能结果, 全部结果有 8 个, 所以概率是 3/8.

易错:不能把结果只分成「0、1、2、3 次正面」四类, 再把概率写成 1/4. 这四类包含的序列数不同, 并不等可能.

10 次抛掷有 210=1024 个序列, 逐一手写不现实. 后面会用组合数直接数出符合条件的序列.

A deck of 52 cards has 13 ranks (2, 3, …, 9, 10, J, Q, K, A) and 4 suits (♡, ♠, ♢, ♣,). A poker hand consists of 5 cards. A one-pair hand consists of two cards having one rank and three cards having three other ranks, e.g., {2♡,2♠,5♡,8♣,K♢}

Test your intuition: the probability of a one-pair hand is:

  1. less than 5%
  2. between 5% and 10%
  3. between 10% and 20%
  4. between 20% and 40%
  5. greater than 40%

一副标准扑克有 52 张牌, 分为 13 个点数和 4 种花色. 一手扑克牌由 5 张组成. 这里的 one-pair 一对指恰好两张牌点数相同, 另外三张分别属于三个不同点数, 而且都不是对子的点数. 例子中的两张 2 是对子, 5、8、K 各不相同.

这个定义不包括两对、三条、四条或葫芦. 题目先让人凭直觉猜一对的概率落在哪个区间; 正确选项是 (e), 大于 40%. 精确计数放在本讲末尾的补充中.

At this point we can only guess the probability. One of our goals is to learn how to compute it exactly. To start, we note that since every set of five cards is equally probable, we can compute the probability of a one-pair hand as

P(one-pair)=total number of handsnumber of one-pair hands​.

So, to find the exact probability, we need to count the number of elements in each of these sets. And we have to be clever about it, because there are too many elements to simply list them all. We will come back to this problem after we have learned some counting techniques.

这时还只能猜概率, 但可以把精确计算拆成两个计数问题: 数出所有可能的 5 张牌组合, 再数出其中符合「一对」定义的组合. 因为每个 5 张牌组合等可能, 后一个数除以前一个数就是所求概率.

一手牌是无序组合, 同样的 5 张牌换一下拿到手的顺序, 仍是同一手牌. 组合总数很大, 不能靠逐列穷举, 所以后面先学组合数, 再回来完成计算.

Page 2

Several times already we have noted that all the possible outcomes were equally probable and used this to find a probability by counting. Let’s state this carefully in the following principle.

Principle: Suppose there are n possible outcomes for an experiment and each is equally probable. If there are k desirable outcomes then the probability of a desirable outcome is k/n. Of course we could replace the word desirable by any other descriptor: undesirable, funny, interesting, remunerative, …

前面已经多次利用「所有结果等可能」来计数求概率. 现在把它写成正式原则: 试验共有 n 个等可能结果, 其中 k 个符合我们关心的条件, 那么所求事件的概率就是 k/n.

这里的 desirable 只是「符合目标条件」, 不一定真的是好事, 也可以换成「不理想的、有趣的」等描述. 每个结果的概率是 1/n, 把事件中的 k 个结果相加, 就得到 k/n.

Page 2

Concept question: Can you think of a scenario where the possible outcomes are not equally probable?

Here’s one scenario: on an exam you can get any score from 0 to 100. That’s 101 different possible outcomes. Is the probability you get less than 50 equal to 50/101?

概念题要求想一个结果不等可能的场景. 例如考试得分可以是 0 到 100, 共 101 个可能值; 低于 50 分包含 0 到 49 分, 共 50 个值. 但不能因此直接说概率是 50/101.

计数公式成立的前提是各个分数等可能, 而真实考试中通常不是. 只有额外建立一个「101 个分数等可能」的模型, 才能使用这个比值. 能编号、能计数, 不等于等可能.

2.2 Sets and notation

Our goal is to learn techniques for counting the number of elements of a set, so we start with a brief review of sets. (If this is new to you, please come to office hours).

2.2.1 Definitions

A set S is a collection of elements. We use the following notation.

Element: We write x∈S to mean the element x is in the set S.

Subset: We say the set A is a subset of S if all of its elements are in S. We write this as A⊂S.

Complement:: The complement of A in S is the set of elements of S that are not in A. We write this as Ac or S−A.

为了学习集合元素的计数, 讲义先复习集合记号. Set 集合是元素的汇集. x∈S 表示 x 是 S 的元素; A⊂S 表示 A 的每个元素都在 S 中, 也就是 A 是 S 的子集. 这里只按课件的子集定义理解, 不额外要求两个集合必须不同.

Complement 补集必须相对于一个全集来说. A 在 S 中的补集, 由所有「属于 S 但不属于 A」的元素组成, 写成 Ac 或 S−A. 离开全集单说 Ac, 范围就不明确.

原文笔误:Complement:: 多写了一个冒号, 应为 Complement:.

Page 2

Union: The union of A and B is the set of all elements in A or B (or both). We write this as A∪B.

Intersection: The intersection of A and B is the set of all elements in both A and B. We write this as A∩B.

Empty set: The empty set is the set with no elements. We denote it ∅.

Disjoint: A and B are disjoint if they have no common elements. That is, if A∩B=∅.

Difference: The difference of A and B is the set of elements in A that are not in B. We write this as A−B.

Union 并集 A∪B 收进属于 A、属于 B 或同时属于两者的元素. Intersection 交集 A∩B 只收同时属于两个集合的元素. 英语里的 or 在这里是「包括两者兼有」, 不是二选一.

Empty set 空集 ∅ 没有任何元素. 若 A∩B=∅, 就说两个集合 disjoint 不相交. Difference 差集 A−B 从 A 出发, 删除其中也属于 B 的元素.

易错:差集有方向, 通常 A−B=B−A. 并集也不是把两个列表机械拼接, 因为同一个元素在集合里只算一次.

Example 2. Start with a set of 10 animals

S={Antelope, Bee, Cat, Dog, Elephant, Frog, Gnat, Hyena, Iguana, Jaguar}.

Consider two subsets:

M=the animal is a mammal={Antelope, Cat, Dog, Elephant, Hyena, Jaguar}
W=the animal lives in the wild={Antelope, Bee, Elephant, Frog, Gnat, Hyena, Iguana, Jaguar}.

Our goal here is to look at different set operations.

例 2 的全集有 10 种动物: 羚羊、蜜蜂、猫、狗、大象、青蛙、蚋、鬣狗、鬣蜥、美洲豹. 子集 M 是其中的哺乳动物: 羚羊、猫、狗、大象、鬣狗、美洲豹. 子集 W 是讲义列为野生的动物: 羚羊、蜜蜂、大象、青蛙、蚋、鬣狗、鬣蜥、美洲豹.

接下来所有运算都围绕这两个条件: 「是不是哺乳动物」和「是否野生」. 把每个元素按条件检查一遍, 就能得到交集、并集、补集和差集.

Intersection: M∩W contains all wild mammals: M∩W={Antelope, Elephant, Hyena, Jaguar}.

Union: M∪W contains all animals that are mammals or wild (or both).

M∪W={Antelope, Bee, Cat, Dog, Elephant, Frog, Gnat, Hyena, Iguana, Jaguar}.

M∩W 是野生哺乳动物, 必须同时满足两个条件, 得到羚羊、大象、鬣狗、美洲豹.

M∪W 只要满足「哺乳动物」或「野生」至少一个条件就能进入, 所以除原来的野生哺乳动物外, 还包括蜜蜂、猫、狗、青蛙、蚋、鬣蜥, 合起来恰好是全集 S. 同时在 M 和 W 中的动物仍只写一次.

Complement: Mc means everything that is not in M, i.e. not a mammal. Mc={Bee, Frog, Gnat, Iguana}.

Difference: M−W means everything that’s in M and not in W. So, M−W={Cat, Dog}.

There are often many ways to get the same set, e.g. Mc=S−M, M−W=M∩Wc.

Mc 是全集中不是哺乳动物的成员, 即蜜蜂、青蛙、蚋、鬣蜥. M−W 则先限定在哺乳动物中, 再排除野生动物, 剩下猫和狗.

同一个集合常有不同写法. Mc=S−M 把补集写成全集减子集; M−W=M∩Wc 表示「属于 M 且不属于 W」. 后一个恒等式把差集改写成交集, 在概率计算中很常用.

Page 3

The relationship between union, intersection, and complement is given by DeMorgan’s laws:

(A∪B)c=Ac∩Bc
(A∩B)c=Ac∪Bc

In words the first law says everything not in (A or B) is the same set as everything that’s (not in A) and (not in B). The second law is similar.

De Morgan’s laws 德摩根律描述并集、交集和补集的关系. 第一条说: 不属于「A 或 B」, 等价于既不属于 A、也不属于 B. 第二条说: 不属于「A 且 B」, 等价于至少不属于其中一个.

记忆时可以看补集跨过括号后的变化: 并与交互换. 第一条是否定「至少满足一个」, 所以两个都不能满足; 第二条是否定「两个同时满足」, 所以只要缺一个即可.

2.3 Counting

If S is finite, we use ∣S∣ or #S to denote the number of elements of S.

Two useful counting principles are the inclusion-exclusion principle and the rule of product.

2.3.1 Inclusion-exclusion principle

The inclusion-exclusion principle says

∣A∪B∣=∣A∣+∣B∣−∣A∩B∣.

若 S 是有限集合, ∣S∣ 或 #S 表示它的元素个数, 也叫集合的基数. 这里介绍两个计数原则, 先看 inclusion-exclusion principle 容斥原理.

两个集合的并集大小为 ∣A∪B∣=∣A∣+∣B∣−∣A∩B∣. 先分别数 A 和 B 时, 交集中的元素被数了两次, 因此最后减去一次, 让每个元素恰好计一次.

Page 5

We can illustrate this with a Venn diagram. S is all the dots, A is the dots in the blue circle, and B is the dots in the red circle.

∣A∣ is the number of dots in A and likewise for the other sets. The figure shows that ∣A∣+∣B∣ double-counts ∣A∩B∣, which is why ∣A∩B∣ is subtracted off in the inclusion-exclusion formula.

Example 4. In a band of singers and guitarists, seven people sing, four play the guitar, and two do both. How big is the band?

读图:全集 S 是图中所有点, 蓝圆中的点组成 A, 红圆中的点组成 B. 计算 ∣A∣+∣B∣ 时, 两圆重叠处的点在两个圆中各数了一次, 所以要减去 ∣A∩B∣.

例 4 给出一支由歌手和吉他手组成的乐队: 7 人会唱歌, 4 人会弹吉他, 2 人两样都会. 问乐队总人数. 「两样都会」就是两个集合的交集, 不能把它再算成第三类额外的人.

Solution: Let S be the set singers and G be the set guitar players. The inclusion-exclusion principle says

size of band=∣S∪G∣=∣S∣+∣G∣−∣S∩G∣=7+4−2=9.

令 S 为歌手集合, G 为吉他手集合, 乐队所有人组成 S∪G. 代入容斥原理: ∣S∪G∣=7+4−2=9, 所以乐队有 9 人.

如果直接算 7+4=11, 既唱歌又弹吉他的 2 人就被重复计算了一次. 也可以分成只唱歌的 5 人、只弹吉他的 2 人、两样都会的 2 人, 三类相加仍是 9 人.

2.3.2 Rule of Product

The Rule of Product says:

If there are n ways to perform action 1 and then by m ways to perform action 2, then there are n⋅m ways to perform action 1 followed by action 2.

We will also call this the multiplication rule.

Example 5. If you have 3 shirts and 4 pants then you can make 3⋅4=12 outfits.

Rule of Product 乘法原理也叫乘法规则: 完成第一步有 n 种方式, 随后完成第二步有 m 种方式, 那么依次完成两步共有 n⋅m 种方式.

例 5 中, 3 件衬衫的每一件都能分别搭配 4 条裤子, 所以有 3⋅4=12 套穿搭. 一套穿搭就是「一件衬衫、一条裤子」的有序搭配, 与前面集合积的计数完全相同.

Think: An extremely important point is that the rule of product holds even if the ways to perform action 2 depend on action 1, as long as the number of ways to perform action 2 is independent of action 1. To illustrate this:

Example 6. There are 5 competitors in the 100m final at the Olympics. In how many ways can the gold, silver, and bronze medals be awarded?

这里强调乘法原理成立的条件: 第二步的具体选项可以随第一步改变, 但第二步的选项个数不能随第一步改变.

例 6 有 5 名奥运百米决赛选手, 要颁发金、银、铜牌, 问有多少种颁奖结果. 每枚奖牌给不同选手, 而且金、银、铜有区别, 所以选人顺序和位置都重要.

金牌有 5 种人选; 金牌确定后, 银牌从剩下 4 人中选; 银牌确定后, 铜牌从剩下 3 人中选. 三步相乘, 共有 5⋅4⋅3=60 种.

金牌得主不同, 能拿银牌的具体人也不同, 但银牌人选总是 4 个, 所以仍能直接用乘法原理. 若第二步在某些情况下有 4 种、另一些情况下只有 3 种, 就要先分类, 不能一步乘到底.

2.4.1 Permutations

A permutation of a set is a particular ordering of its elements. For example, the set {a,b,c} has six permutations: abc,acb,bac,bca,cab,cba. We found the number of permutations by listing them all. We could also have found the number of permutations by using the rule of product. That is, there are 3 ways to pick the first element, then 2 ways for the second, and 1 for the third. This gives a total of 3⋅2⋅1=6 permutations.

In general, the rule of product tells us that the number of permutations of a set of k elements is

k!=k⋅(k−1)⋯3⋅2⋅1.

We also talk about the permutations of k things out of a set of n things. We show what this means with an example.

Permutation 排列是集合元素的一种特定顺序. 集合 {a,b,c} 有 6 种全排列, 因为第一位有 3 种选择, 第二位剩 2 种, 第三位只剩 1 种, 共 3⋅2⋅1=6 种.

一般地, k 个不同元素的全排列有 k! 种, 阶乘 k!=k(k−1)⋯3⋅2⋅1. 还可以只从 n 个元素中取出 k 个来排列, 也就是不要求把全部元素都排进去, 但取出的元素仍有先后次序.

2.4.3 Formulas

We’ll use the following notations.

n​Pk​= number of permutations (lists) of k distinct elements from a set of size n

n​Ck​=(kn​)= number of combinations (subsets) of k elements from a set of size n

We emphasize that by the number of combinations of k elements we mean the number of subsets of size k.

These have the following notation and formulas:

Permutations:  n​Pk​=(n−k)!n!​=n(n−1)⋯(n−k+1)
Combinations:  n​Ck​=(kn​)=k!(n−k)!n!​=k!n​Pk​​

n​Pk​ 表示从 n 个不同元素中取 k 个排成有序列表的数目. n​Ck​ 也写成 (kn​), 表示从 n 个元素中取 k 个组成无序子集的数目.

排列公式从乘法原理得到: k 个位置依次有 n,n−1,…,n−k+1 种选择, 连乘可写成 (n−k)!n!​.

组合公式再多除一个 k!, 因为每个 k 元素子集都被它的 k! 种顺序重复计算了. 因此 (kn​)=n​Pk​/k!=n!/(k!(n−k)!).

Page 7

The notation n​Ck​ is read “n choose k”. The formula for n​Pk​ follows from the rule of product. It also implies the formula for n​Ck​ because a subset of size k can be ordered in k! ways.

We can illustrate the relation between permutations and combinations by lining up the results of the previous two examples. Notice that each row in the permutations list consists of all 3! permutations of the corresponding set in the combinations list.

n​Ck​ 读作 n choose k, 意思是「从 n 个中选 k 个」. 从排列数推到组合数, 关键就是每个大小为 k 的子集能排出 k! 个有序列表.

把前两个例子的结果上下对齐, 每一行的 6 个排列都对应右边同一个组合. 关系可以写成 n​Pk​=(kn​)k!. 除以 k! 是在消除同一子集的重复排序, 不是随意补进公式的因子.
