统计实践与统计量
Statistical Practice and Statistics

1 学习目标
1 Learning Goals

Page 1
  1. Know the three overlapping “phases” of statistical practice.
  2. Know what is meant by the term statistic.

本讲有两个目标: 认识统计实践的三个相互重叠阶段, 并理解「统计量」的含义.

三个阶段是收集数据、描述数据和分析数据. 之后还要能区分由样本算出的统计量与总体中的未知参数.

2 统计导论
2 Introduction to statistics

Page 1

Statistics deals with data. Generally speaking, the goal of statistics is to make inferences based on data. We can divide this the process into three phases: collecting data, describing data and analyzing data. This fits into the paradigm of the scientific method. We make hypotheses about what’s true, collect data in experiments, describe the results, and then infer from the results the strength of the evidence concerning our hypotheses.

统计研究数据, 目标是根据数据作推断. 整个过程可分为收集数据、描述数据和分析数据. 对应科学方法中的「提出假设 → 实验收集结果 → 描述结果 → 评价假设的证据强弱」.

这三个阶段相互重叠, 不是完全孤立的流水线. 设计时就要考虑最终分析, 描述结果时也可能发现需要修改模型或实验.

2.1 实验设计
2.1 Experimental design

Page 1

The design of an experiment is crucial to making sure the collected data is useful. The adage ‘garbage in, garbage out’ applies here. A poorly designed experiment will produce poor quality data, from which it may be impossible to draw useful, valid inferences. To quote R.A. Fisher one of the founders of modern statistics,

To consult a statistician after an experiment is finished is often merely to ask him to conduct a post-mortem examination. He can perhaps say what the experiment died of.

Experimental design 实验设计决定数据是否有用. 「垃圾进, 垃圾出」说明设计不良的实验会产生低质量数据, 后续再复杂的方法也可能无法作有效推断.

Fisher 的验尸比喻强调统计咨询应提前进行: 实验结束后才找统计学家, 往往只能分析实验为什么失败, 不能补救设计缺陷.

2.2 描述统计
2.2 Descriptive statistics

Page 1

Raw data often takes the form of a massive list, array, or database of labels and numbers. To make sense of the data, we can calculate summary statistics like the mean, median, and interquartile range. We can also visualize the data using graphical devices like histograms, scatterplots, and the empirical cdf. These methods are useful for both communicating and exploring the data to gain insight into its structure, such as whether it might follow a familiar probability distribution.

Descriptive statistics 描述统计用均值、中位数、四分位距等汇总指标, 以及直方图、散点图、经验 cdf 等图形来理解大量原始数据.

它既用于沟通, 也用于探索结构. 例如可以先看数据是否单峰、偏斜、有异常值, 再判断某种熟悉的概率分布是否值得作为候选模型.

三个阶段互相制约. 实验设计决定能回答什么问题, 描述统计帮助发现数据结构和异常, 推断统计再用模型评价假设. 设计、描述和分析不能完全分开.

如果抽样有偏或测量不可靠, 后续模型再精细也无法自动补救. 反过来, 好的设计也需要事先明确分析目标, 才能决定样本量、分组和指标.

因此统计实践不是「先收集再想办法」, 而是「先明确问题和模型, 再收集并检查数据」.

2.3 推断统计
2.3 Inferential statistics

Pages 1–2

Ultimately we want to draw inferences about the world. Often this takes the form of specifying a statistical model for the random process by which the data arises. For example, suppose the data takes the form of a series of measurements whose error we believe follows a normal distribution. (Note this is always an approximation since we know the error must have some bound while a normal distribution has range (−∞,∞).) We might then use the data to provide evidence for or against this hypothesis.

课程重点是用数据推断模型参数. 例如用 500 次妊娠时长估计正态模型的 μ,σ, 或用民调估计 Bernoulli 支持率 p.

这些参数通常不是只由样本直接确定的常数, 而是总体或模型的未知性质. 推断结果必须带上不确定性.

Page 2

Our focus in 18.05 will be on how to use data to draw inferences about model parameters. For example, assuming gestational length follows a N(μ,σ) distribution, we’ll use the data of the gestational lengths of, say, 500 pregnancies to draw inferences about the values of the parameters μ and σ. Similarly, we may model the result of a two-candidate election by a Bernoulli(p) distribution, and use poll data to draw inferences about the value of p.

We can rarely make definitive statements about such parameters because the data itself comes from a random process (such as choosing who to poll). Rather, our statistical evidence will always involve probability statements. Unfortunately, the media and public at large are wont to misunderstand the probabilistic meaning of statistical statements. In fact, researchers themselves often commit the same errors. In this course, we will emphasize the meaning of statistical statements alongside the methods which produce them.

Inferential statistics 推断统计先为数据生成过程建立模型, 再用数据评价模型或参数. 例如用妊娠数据估计正态模型参数, 或用民调数据估计 Bernoulli 的支持率 p.

模型通常是近似. 真实测量误差有界, 正态分布却覆盖全实数轴, 所以正态假设是否合理也要检查.

数据本身来自随机过程, 因此统计结论通常是概率陈述, 而不是对参数的绝对断言. 课程会同时强调方法及其结论的准确含义.

2.4 什么是统计量
2.4 What is a statistic?

Pages 2–3

We give a simple definition whose meaning is best elucidated by examples.

Definition. A statistic is anything that can be computed from the collected data.

Statistic 统计量是只由收集到的数据计算得到的量. 是否有用不影响定义, 所以奇怪的计算也可能是统计量; 关键在可观测、可计算.

Page 3

Example 3. Consider the data of 1000 rolls of a die. All of the following are statistics: the average of the 1000 rolls; the number of times a 6 was rolled; the sum of the squares of the rolls minus the number of even rolls. It’s hard to imagine how we would use the last example, but it is a statistic. On the other hand, the probability of rolling a 6 is not a statistic, whether or not the die is truly fair. Rather this probability is a property of the die (and the way we roll it) which we can estimate using the data. Such an estimate is given by the statistic ‘proportion of the rolls that were 6’.

样本均值、样本中 6 的次数、平方和减去偶数次数都符合统计量定义, 即使最后一个看起来没有实际用途.

真实正面概率则不是统计量, 它是骰子及投掷方式的性质. 样本中 6 的比例是统计量, 可以用来估计这个总体概率.

Example 4. Suppose we treat a group of cancer patients with a new procedure and collect data on how long they survive post-treatment. From the data we can compute the average survival time of patients in the group. We might employ this statistic as an estimate of the average survival time for future cancer patients following the new procedure. The “expected survival time” for the new procedure (if that even has a meaning) is not a statistic.

Example 5. Suppose we ask 1000 residents whether or not they support the proposal to legalize marijuana in Massachusetts. The proportion of the 1000 who support the proposal is a statistic. The proportion of all Massachusetts residents who support the proposal is not a statistic since we have not queried every single one (note the word “collected” in the definition). Rather, we hope to draw a statistical conclusion about the state-wide proportion based on the data of our random sample.

Statistic 统计量是只由收集到的数据计算得到的量. 样本均值、样本中 6 的次数、样本支持率都是统计量; 总体参数, 如真实正面概率、期望寿命、全州支持率, 不是统计量.

是否有用不影响定义, 所以「平方和减去偶数次数」即使奇怪也仍是统计量. 统计量则常用来估计对应的总体参数.

The following are two general types of statistics we will use in 18.05.

  1. Point statistics: a single value computed from data, such as the sample average xˉn​ or the sample standard deviation sn​.
  2. Interval statistics: an interval [a,b] computed from the data. This is really just a pair of point statistics, and will often be presented in the form xˉ±s.

Point statistic 点统计量给出一个数值, 如样本均值或样本标准差. Interval statistic 区间统计量给出由数据决定的区间, 本质上是一对点统计量, 常写成 xˉ±s.

点估计回答「一个最可能的值是多少」, 区间估计则给出一组可能范围. 两者都来自数据, 不是固定总体常数.

概念题: 它是统计量吗
Concept question: Is it a statistic?

Slide 7

You believe that the lifetimes of a certain type of lightbulb follow an exponential distribution with parameter λ. To test this hypothesis you measure the lifetime of 5 bulbs and get data x1​,…,x5​.

Which of the following are statistics?

  1. The sample average xˉ=5x1​+x2​+x3​+x4​+x5​​.
  2. The expected value of a sample, namely 1/λ.
  3. The difference between xˉ and 1/λ.

1. (a)   2. (b)   3. (c)   4. (a) and (b)   5. (a) and (c)   6. (b) and (c)   7. all three   8. none of them

正确选项是 1, 只有 (a). 样本平均值只由 5 个观察值计算; 1/λ 是模型参数决定的期望值; xˉ−1/λ 含有未知的 λ, 也不能只凭数据算出.

若先人为假设 λ=λ0​, 可以计算 xˉ−1/λ0​, 这是一个含已知常数的统计量. 但题目给的是未指定数值的参数 λ, 所以 (c) 不符合定义.

统计量与参数的区别. 统计量只由样本算出, 例如 xˉ、样本方差和样本支持率. 参数属于总体或模型, 例如 p,λ,μ,σ2, 通常未知.

一个量是否叫统计量, 与它是否有用无关, 与它是否可观测有关. 若公式含未知参数且没有给定数值, 就不能只凭数据算出, 因此不是统计量.

统计量本身也有抽样分布. 抽样前, Xˉ 是随机变量; 抽样后得到的 2.8 是它的观察值. 点估计和区间估计都由数据决定, 新样本会给出新结果.

贝叶斯定理与证据
Bayes’ Theorem and Evidence

3 复习贝叶斯定理
3 Review of Bayes’ theorem

Pages 3–4

We cannot stress strongly enough how important Bayes’ theorem is to our view of inferential statistics. Recall that Bayes’ theorem allows us to ‘invert’ conditional probabilities. That is, if H and D are events, then Bayes’ theorem says

P(H∣D)=P(D)P(D∣H)P(H)​.

In scientific experiments we start with a hypothesis and collect data to test the hypothesis. We will often let H represent the event ‘our hypothesis is true’ and let D be the collected data. In these words Bayes’ theorem says

P(hypothesis is true∣data)=P(data)P(data∣hypothesis is true)P(hypothesis is true)​.

The left-hand term is the probability our hypothesis is true given the data we collected. This is precisely what we’d like to know. When all the probabilities on the right are known exactly, we can compute the probability on the left exactly. This will be our focus next week. Unfortunately, in practice we rarely know the exact values of all the terms on the right. Statisticians have developed a number of ways to cope with this lack of knowledge and still make useful inferences. We will be exploring these methods for the rest of the course.

贝叶斯定理把 P(D∣H) 与 P(H∣D) 区分开并联系起来. 前者是在假设成立时数据出现的可能性, 后者是在拿到数据后假设成立的概率; 两者一般不相等.

要从前者推到后者, 还需要先验 P(H) 和证据总概率 P(D). 这也是后文反复强调「似然不是参数的概率」的原因: 只有 P(D∣H), 没有先验, 不能直接得到 P(H∣D).

Page 4

Example 6. Screening for a disease redux

Suppose a screening test for a disease has a 1% false positive rate and a 1% false negative rate. Suppose also that the rate of the disease in the population is 0.002. Finally suppose a randomly selected person tests positive. In the language of hypothesis and data we have:

Hypothesis: H= ‘the person has the disease’
Data: D= ‘the test was positive.’
What we want to know: P(H∣D)=P(the person has the disease∣a positive test)

In this example all the probabilities on the right are known so we can use Bayes’ theorem to compute what we want to know.

P(H∣D)=P(D)P(D∣H)P(H)​=0.99⋅0.002+0.01⋅0.9980.99⋅0.002​=0.166.

Before the test we would have said the probability the person had the disease was 0.002. After the test we see the probability is 0.166. That is, the positive test provides some evidence that the person has the disease.

患病率 P(H)=0.002, 真阳性率 P(D∣H)=0.99, 假阳性率 P(D∣Hc)=0.01. 因此阳性后的患病概率为 0.00198/(0.00198+0.00998)=0.1655518…, 原文四舍五入为 0.166 正确.

阳性结果把概率从 0.2% 提高到约 16.6%, 证据很强但不是确诊. 即使假阳性率只有 1%, 因为未患病人群占 99.8%, 假阳性总数仍然可观.

似然不是后验概率. P(D∣H) 固定在假设 H 下看数据有多可能; P(H∣D) 固定观察到的数据后看假设有多可信. 两者由贝叶斯定理联系, 但一般不相等.

从似然走向后验还需要先验 P(H) 和证据总概率 P(D). 没有先验时, MLE 只比较不同参数下的数据概率, 不给参数本身分配概率.

筛查例中, 真阳性率 P(D∣H)=0.99 很高, 但阳性后的 P(H∣D) 仍受基准患病率影响. 这就是「检测很准」与「阳性就是患病」之间的差别.

参数模型、似然与二项 MLE
Parametric Models, Likelihood, and Binomial MLE

2 引言与最大似然估计
2 Introduction; 3 Maximum Likelihood Estimates

Pages 1–2

Suppose we know we have data consisting of values x1​,…,xn​ drawn from an exponential distribution. The question remains: which exponential distribution?!

We have casually referred to the exponential distribution or the binomial distribution or the normal distribution. In fact the exponential distribution exp(λ) is not a single distribution but rather a one-parameter family of distributions. Each value of λ defines a different distribution in the family, with pdf fλ​(x)=λe−λx on [0,∞). Similarly, a binomial distribution bin(n,p) is determined by the two parameters n and p, and a normal distribution N(μ,σ2) is determined by the two parameters μ and σ2 (or equivalently, μ and σ). Parameterized families of distributions are often called parametric distributions or parametric models.

指数分布不是单一分布, 而是一族由 λ 决定的分布; 二项由 n,p 决定, 正态由 μ,σ2 决定. 这些叫参数化分布或参数模型.

Page 2

We are often faced with the situation of having random data which we know (or believe) is drawn from a parametric model, whose parameters we do not know. For example, in an election between two candidates, polling data constitutes draws from a Bernoulli(p) distribution with unknown parameter p. In this case we would like to use the data to estimate the value of the parameter p, as the latter predicts the result of the election. Similarly, assuming gestational length follows a normal distribution, we would like to use the data of the gestational lengths from a random sample of pregnancies to draw inferences about the values of the parameters μ and σ2.

Parametric model 参数模型是一族由有限个参数区分的分布. 指数分布由 λ 决定, 二项分布由 n,p 决定, 正态分布由 μ,σ2 决定. 已知参数时算数据概率, 统计推断则利用数据估计未知参数.

Our focus so far has been on computing the probability of data arising from a parametric model with known parameters. Statistical inference flips this on its head: we will estimate the probability of parameters given a parametric model and observed data drawn from it. In the coming weeks we will see how parameter values are naturally viewed as hypotheses, so we are in fact estimating the probability of various hypotheses given the data.

Parametric model 参数模型是一族由有限个参数区分的分布. 指数分布由 λ 决定, 二项分布由 n,p 决定, 正态分布由 μ,σ2 决定. 已知参数时算数据概率, 统计推断则利用数据估计未知参数.

例如选举民调可看作 Bernoulli(p) 模型, 其中 p 未知. 我们希望用样本估计 p, 因为总体支持率能预测选举结果.

There are many methods for estimating unknown parameters from data. We will first consider the maximum likelihood estimate (MLE), which answers the question:

For which parameter value does the observed data have the biggest probability?

The MLE is an example of a point estimate because it gives a single value for the unknown parameter (later our estimates will involve intervals and probabilities). Two advantages of the MLE are that it is often easy to compute and that it agrees with our intuition in simple examples. We will explain the MLE through a series of examples.

Maximum likelihood estimate 最大似然估计 (MLE)选择让已观察数据最可能出现的参数值, 是一种点估计. 它通常容易计算, 也符合简单问题中的直觉.

这里「估计参数的概率」只能按贝叶斯观点宽泛理解; 对频率派 MLE, 不需要给参数先验, 也不会仅由似然得到 P(p∣data).

Example 1. A coin is flipped 100 times. Given that there were 55 heads, find the maximum likelihood estimate for the probability p of heads on a single toss.

Before actually solving the problem, let’s establish some notation and terms.

We can think of counting the number of heads in 100 tosses as an experiment. For a given value of p, the probability of getting 55 heads in this experiment is the binomial probability

P(55 heads)=(55100​)p55(1−p)45.

The probability of getting 55 heads depends on the value of p, so let’s include p in by using the notation of conditional probability:

P(55 heads∣p)=(55100​)p55(1−p)45.

You should read P(55 heads∣p) as: ‘the probability of 55 heads given p,’ or more precisely as ‘the probability of 55 heads given that the probability of heads on a single toss is p.’

例 1 抛 100 次并得到 55 次正面. 对给定 p, 这 55 次的概率是 (55100​)p55(1−p)45. 把这个概率写成 P(55 heads∣p), 强调它随候选参数 p 改变.

这里读作「在单次正面概率为 p 时, 得到 55 次正面的概率」. 数据已经固定, p 是接下来要变化的假设值.

Page 2
  • Experiment: Flip the coin 100 times and count the number of heads.
  • Data: The data is the result of the experiment. In this case it is ‘55 heads’.
  • Parameter(s) of interest: We are interested in the value of the unknown parameter p.
  • Likelihood, or likelihood function: this is P(data∣p). Note it is a function of both the data and the parameter p. In this case the likelihood is P(55 heads∣p)=(55100​)p55(1−p)45.

Notes: 1. The likelihood P(data∣p) changes as the parameter of interest p changes. 2. Look carefully at the definition. Definition: Given data the maximum likelihood estimate (MLE) for the parameter p is the value of p that maximizes the likelihood P(data∣p). That is, the MLE is the value of p for which the data is most likely.

实验是抛 100 次并计数, 数据是 55 次正面, 待估参数是 p. 似然函数把观察结果固定, 把 p 当作变量: L(p)=(55100​)p55(1−p)45. MLE 就是最大化这个函数的 p.

Solution: For the problem at hand, we saw above that the likelihood

P(55 heads∣p)=(55100​)p55(1−p)45.

We’ll use the notation p^​ for the MLE. We use calculus to find it by taking the derivative of the likelihood function and setting it to 0.

dpd​P(data∣p)=(55100​)(55p54(1−p)45−45p55(1−p)44)=0.

Solving this for p we get

55p54(1−p)45=45p55(1−p)44
55(1−p)=45p
55=100p

the MLE is p^​=0.55

端点 p=0,1 的似然都为 0, 内部似然为正, 且只有一个临界点, 因此该点就是唯一最大值. MLE 由数据计算, 所以也是统计量.

数值核对还显示 L(0.55) 同时大于 L(0.54) 和 L(0.56). 这说明临界点是最大, 而不是只满足导数为 0 的其他点.

Note: 1. The MLE for p turned out to be exactly the fraction of heads we saw in our data. 2. The MLE is computed from the data. That is, it is a statistic. 3. If is often easier to work with the natural log of the likelihood function. For short this is simply called the log likelihood. Since ln(x) is an increasing function, the maxima of the likelihood and log likelihood coincide.

Solution: We had the likelihood P(55 heads∣p)=(55100​)p55(1−p)45. Therefore the log likelihood is

ln(P(55 heads∣p)=ln(55100​)+55lnp+45ln(1−p).

Maximizing likelihood is the same as maximizing log likelihood. We check that calculus gives us the same answer as before:

dpd​(log likelihood)=dpd​[ln(55100​)+55lnp+45ln(1−p)]=p55​−1−p45​=0
⇒55(1−p)=45p⇒p^​=0.55

最大似然估计 (MLE)
Maximum likelihood estimate (MLE)

Slide 13

The maximum likelihood estimate (MLE) is a way to estimate the value of a parameter of interest from the data.

The MLE is the value of p that maximizes the likelihood.

Different problems call for different methods of finding the maximum. Here are a few –there are others:

  1. Calculus: To find the MLE, solve dpd​P(data∣p)=0 for p. (We should also check that the critical point is a maximum.)
  2. Sometimes the derivative is never 0 and the MLE is at an endpoint of the allowable range.
  3. If the parameter takes only a finite number of values, you can compute the likelihood for each one and see which is the biggest.

求 MLE 有三条常见路径: 可微时求导并验证最大值; 单调或支撑受限时检查端点; 参数只有有限个候选时逐一枚举.

均匀分布端点、捕获 / 重捕和三枚硬币分别对应后两种思想. 求出临界点后仍要确认它是最大, 不是最小或鞍点.

求 MLE 的固定流程. ① 写出试验模型和独立结构. ② 固定观察数据, 把参数当变量, 得到似然 L(θ). ③ 通常取对数得到 ℓ(θ), 把乘积变和. ④ 用求导、端点或枚举找最大. ⑤ 检查边界和合法参数范围, 并确认该点确实是最大.

求导为 0 只是候选条件, 不自动保证最大. 参数支撑随参数改变时, 端点往往比驻点更重要. 候选只有有限个值时, 直接比较似然往往最稳妥.

最后报告估计值时戴上帽子, 如 p^​,λ^, 以区分估计量与真实参数. MLE 由数据计算, 所以它也是统计量, 会随样本变化.

不同模型的 MLE 对照. 二项模型把成功比例作为估计, 指数模型把 1/xˉ 作为速率估计, 正态模型分别用样本均值和除以 n 的方差, 均匀模型则用样本最小值和最大值确定支撑.

这些答案看似不同, 其实都来自同一条原则: 固定数据, 比较各参数下数据出现的可能性. 真正差别在模型结构、参数支撑和最大化方法.

对数似然适合处理独立数据的乘积, 端点分析适合支撑随参数变化的模型, 枚举适合有限候选参数. 不要把一种模型的公式套到另一种模型上.

For continuous distributions, we use the probability density function to define the likelihood. We show this in a few examples. In the next section we explain how this is analogous to what we did in the discrete case.

Example 3. Light bulbs

Suppose that the lifetime of Badger brand light bulbs is modeled by an exponential distribution with (unknown) parameter λ. We test 5 bulbs and find they have lifetimes of 2, 3, 1, 3, and 4 years, respectively. What is the MLE for λ?

Solution: We need to be careful with our notation. With five different values it is best to use subscripts. Let Xi​ be the lifetime of the ith bulb and let xi​ be the value Xi​ takes. Then each Xi​ has pdf fXi​​(xi​)=λe−λxi​. We assume the lifetimes of the bulbs are independent, so the joint pdf is the product of the individual densities:

f(x1​,x2​,x3​,x4​,x5​∣λ)=(λe−λx1​)(λe−λx2​)(λe−λx3​)(λe−λx4​)(λe−λx5​)=λ5e−λ(x1​+x2​+x3​+x4​+x5​).

Note that we write this as a conditional density, since it depends on λ. Viewing the data as fixed and λ as variable, this density is the likelihood function. Our data had values x1​=2,x2​=3,x3​=1,x4​=3,x5​=4.

独立灯泡寿命的联合密度是各密度相乘: λ5e−λ∑xi​. 大写 Xi​ 表示随机变量, 小写 xi​ 表示观察值.

把数据固定后, 这个联合密度就是 λ 的似然函数. 本例数据为 2、3、1、3、4, 总和为 13, 所以 L(λ)=λ5e−13λ.

So the likelihood and log likelihood functions with this data are

f(2,3,1,3,4∣λ)=λ5e−13λ,ln(f(2,3,1,3,4∣λ)=5lnλ−13λ

Finally we use calculus to find the MLE:

dλd​(log likelihood)=λ5​−13=0⇒λ^=135​.

对数似然是 5lnλ−13λ, 导数为 5/λ−13, 得 λ^=5/13≈0.384615.

Example 4. Normal distributions

Suppose the data x1​,x2​,…,xn​ is drawn from a N(μ,σ2) distribution, where μ and σ are unknown. Find the maximum likelihood estimate for the pair (μ,σ2).

Solution: Let’s be precise and phrase this in terms of random variables and densities. Let uppercase X1​,…,Xn​ be i.i.d. N(μ,σ2) random variables, and let lowercase xi​ be the value Xi​ takes. The density for each Xi​ is

fXi​​(xi​)=2π​σ1​e−(xi​−μ)2/(2σ2).

Since the Xi​ are independent their joint pdf is the product of the individual pdf’s:

f(x1​,…,xn​∣μ,σ)=(2π​σ1​)ne−∑i=1n​(xi​−μ)2/(2σ2).

For the fixed data x1​,…,xn​, the likelihood and log likelihood are

lnf(x1​,…,xn​∣μ,σ)=−nln2π​−nlnσ−i=1∑n​2σ2(xi​−μ)2​.

正态数据的联合密度是各密度相乘. 对数似然为 −nln(2π​)−nlnσ−∑(xi​−μ)2/(2σ2).

这里同时有 μ 和 σ 两个未知量, 所以要分别求偏导. 数据固定后, 平方和项和尺度项共同决定似然形状.

∂μ∂f(x1​,…,xn​∣μ,σ)​=i=1∑n​σ2xi​−μ​=0⇒i=1∑n​xi​=nμ⇒μ^​=n∑i=1n​xi​​=xˉ.

这里实际使用的是对数似然, 所以导数左边应理解为 ∂ℓ/∂μ 和 ∂ℓ/∂σ. 求导对象是 lnf, 不是原密度 f.

均匀分布尤其不能只靠求导, 因为支撑 [a,b] 本身随参数改变. 在覆盖数据的前提下缩短区间才是最大化原则.

捕获 / 重捕则要求 n 是整数, 应比较相邻整数似然. 不同模型采用不同最大化方法, 但都离不开合法范围和边界检查.

Example 5. Uniform distributions

Suppose our data x1​,…,xn​ are independently drawn from a uniform distribution U(a,b). Find the MLE for a and b.

Solution: This example is different from the previous ones in that we won’t use calculus to find the MLE. The density for U(a,b) is b−a1​ on [a,b]. Therefore our likelihood function is

f(x1​,…,xn​∣a,b)={(b−a)−n0​if all xi​ are in the interval [a,b],otherwise.​

This is maximized by making b−a as small as possible. The only restriction is that the interval [a,b] must include all the data. Thus the MLE for the pair (a,b) is

a^=min(x1​,…,xn​),b^=max(x1​,…,xn​).

若任一数据落在 [a,b] 外, 似然就是 0. 要让似然 (b−a)−n 最大, 必须在覆盖所有数据的前提下让区间最短, 所以左端取样本最小值, 右端取样本最大值.

Example 6. Capture/recapture method

The capture/recapture method is a way to estimate the size of a population in the wild. The method assumes that each animal in the population is equally likely to be captured by a trap.

The idea for the maximum likelihood estimate is to find the value of the parameter(s) for which the data has the highest probability. In this section we ’ll see that we’re doing this is really what we are doing with the densities. We will do this by considering a smaller version of the light bulb example.

Example 8. Suppose we have two light bulbs whose lifetimes follow an exponential(λ) distribution. Suppose also that we independently measure their lifetimes and get data x1​=2 years and x2​=3 years. Find the value of λ that maximizes the probability of this data.

Solution: The main paradox to deal with is that for a continuous distribution the probability of a single value, say x1​=2, is zero. We resolve this paradox by remembering that a single measurement really means a range of values, e.g. in this example we might check the light bulb once a day. So the data x1​=2 years really means x1​ is somewhere in a range of 1 day around 2 years.

If the range is small we call it dx1​. The probability that X1​ is in the range is approximated by fX1​​(x1​∣λ)dx1​. This is illustrated in the figure below. The data value x2​ is treated in exactly the same way.

For the interested reader, we note several nice features of the MLE. These are quite technical and will not be on any exams.

The MLE behaves well under transformations. That is, if p^​ is the MLE for p and g is a one-to-one function, then g(p)​ is the MLE for g(p). For example, if σ^ is the MLE for the standard deviation σ then σ^2 is the MLE for the variance σ2.

Furthermore, under some technical smoothness assumptions, the MLE is asymptotically unbiased and has asymptotically minimal variance. To explain these notions, note that the MLE is itself a random variable since the data is random and the MLE is computed from the data. Let x1​,x2​,… be an infinite sequence of samples from a distribution with parameter p. Let p^​n​ be the MLE for p based on the data x1​,…,xn​.

Asymptotically unbiased means that as the amount of data grows, the mean of the MLE converges to p. In symbols: E[p^​n​]→p as n→∞. Of course, we would like the MLE to be close to p with high probability, not just on average, so the smaller the variance of the MLE the better. Asymptotically minimal variance means that as the amount of data grows, the MLE has the minimal variance among all unbiased estimators of p. In symbols: for any unbiased estimator p~​n​ and ϵ>0 we have that Var(p~​n​)+ϵ>Var(p^​n​) as n→∞.

Invariance 不变性表示参数做一一对应变换后, 只需对 MLE 做同样变换: g(p)​=g(p^​). 因此标准差和方差的 MLE 可以直接互相转换.

在适当正则条件下, MLE 渐近无偏, 并渐近达到无偏估计的最小方差. MLE 本身由数据计算, 所以也是随机变量, 有自己的均值和方差.

这些是大样本性质, 不保证每个有限样本都无偏或最优; 例如正态方差的 MLE 除以 n, 小样本时就有向下偏差.

不变性不是「随便变换都可以」. 它要求 g 是一一对应函数. 在这个条件下, 最大化似然的参数点会跟着变换, 所以 g(p)​=g(p^​). 标准差与方差之间的转换正好符合.

渐近无偏和渐近最小方差都描述 n 增大的极限行为, 不表示小样本时估计已经准确. 评价一个具体估计时, 还要看模型是否正确、样本是否独立同分布以及参数范围是否合法.

MLE 本身由数据计算, 所以也有抽样分布. 比较不同估计方法时, 除了点估计值, 还应关注偏差、方差和有限样本表现.
